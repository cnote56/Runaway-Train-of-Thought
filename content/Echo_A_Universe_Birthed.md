The Four Horsemen of Creation

During the Planck Era, which occurred prior to 10<sup>-43</sup> seconds
after the Big Bang, the universe was in a state of extreme conditions.
At that time, the four fundamental forces of nature were believed to be
combined into a single “super” force. During the Planck Era, these
forces were unified, but as the universe expanded and cooled, they
gradually separated into distinct interactions. These forces shaped the
early cosmos and set the stage for the universe we observe today¹²³.
🌌🔭

 

Gravity:

- Dominant force on a large scale.

- The weakest of the four fundamental forces.

- Governs the motion of massive objects, such as planets, stars, and
  galaxies.

 

Electromagnetic Force:

- Dominates atomic and molecular interactions.

- Responsible for phenomena like electric charges, magnetic fields, and
  light.

Strong Nuclear Force:

- Holds atomic nuclei together.

- Overcomes the electromagnetic repulsion between positively charged
  protons.

- Essential for the stability of matter.

Weak Nuclear Force:

- Mediates nuclear reactions.

- Responsible for processes like beta decay.

- Plays a crucial role in the evolution of stars and the production of
  heavy elements.
