This ritual selects random individuals from a species found inhabiting a
highly desirable planet for resources and intergalactic expansion by
“The Legion,” a collective of alien races that have made contact and
brought together a hundred since its inception and exploration of space.

The test is the first step in determining whether a planet’s inhabitants
are and can be trusted to wield the power to traverse space with The
Legions’ Sub-Light technology.

The Legion’s first test seeks to find a mature and enlightened species
and not one that would sooner cut its world in half or destroy an entire
system, such as the great self-destruction of IO-Five. So many lives
were cut short in a flash of intense light when the disease of fear and
greed sought to steal the alien technology given freely, and it
ultimately catalyzed and erupted every molecule nearest to the surface,
leaving the whole surface and its inhabitants atomized and ash.

their powers manifest immediately.
