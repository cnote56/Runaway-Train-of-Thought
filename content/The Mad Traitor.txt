To help you develop a compelling plot line for your novel, I have
cross-referenced the technical concepts in your **"Project FILTER"**
notes with the clandestine history of **Philip Agee** and the modern
oversight findings of the **PCLOB** reports.

Here is a proposed plot structure and thematic framework based on the
sources:

### **The Plot: "The Algorithm of Dissent"**

**The Premise**

In a near-future setting, the government has moved beyond traditional
wiretapping. They have implemented **Project FILTER**, a surveillance
program that uses **Visible Light Communication (VLC)**—the fast
modulation of LED city lights—to track the physical movements and data
transfers of citizens without their knowledge [1]. The program doesn't
just collect data; it uses **Netflix-style behavioral algorithms** to
predict "watching habits"—which, in this context, are actually
"revolutionary habits" [1].

**Act 1: The Idealist’s Discovery**

* **The Protagonist:** A modern-day version of **Philip Agee**, a Junior
Officer Trainee (JOT) recruited from a prestigious university with the
motto "For God and Country" [2, 3].

* **The Job:** He is assigned to a "Deep Dive" audit unit, similar to
those described in the **PCLOB reports**, tasked with reviewing
"incidental collection" of U.S. Person (USP) data [4].

* **The Inciting Incident:** While auditing **XKEYSCORE** query
justifications, he discovers that the agency is bypassing the **Fourth
Amendment** by using a "foreign intelligence exception" to harvest data
from U.S. cities, claiming the data is "unevaluated" and therefore
exempt from immediate deletion [5, 6]. He finds a "masked" USP record
that contains a familiar name: a former classmate who is now a political
activist [7].

**Act 2: The Mechanism of Repression**

* **The Tech:** The protagonist discovers that Project FILTER is the
"dagger inside the cloak" [8]. It uses VLC to turn every LED streetlamp
into a sensor that reads data modulations from the cameras of civilian
smartphones [1].

* **The Conflict:** He learns that the program’s goal is "target
discovery" of the **"unknown unknowns"**—identifying rebels before they
even realize they are part of a movement [9, 10].

* **The Legal Twist:** Your antagonist (a senior official like **Winston
Scott** or **C. Harlow Duffin**) justifies this by "squeezing authority
from a statute that does not give it," specifically referencing the
**American Council on Education v. FCC** case to argue that new
broadband services must be "surveillance-ready" [11-13].

* **The Turning Point:** Mirroring Agee’s disillusionment, the
protagonist realizes he has become a **"secret policeman for
capitalism"** [14]. He sees the agency using the algorithm to protect
the "top 5 percent" of the economy by suppressing the "marginalized
majority" [15, 16].

**Act 3: The Clandestine Escape**

* **Tradecraft:** To avoid the all-seeing **XKEYSCORE**, the protagonist
must revert to 1960s-style tradecraft: **dead drops** in hollowed trees,
**secret writing (SW)** in letters using chemical "ink pills," and
meeting in "safe houses" [17-20].

* **The Climax:** He attempts to leak the Project FILTER source code.
The agency sends "access agents"—friendly-looking people like **Sal
Ferrera** or **Leslie Donegan**—to trap him [21, 22].

* **The Visual Hook:** Use the **"bugged typewriter"** from the Agee
sources. The agency plants a travel typewriter in his room that contains
a hidden FM transmitter in the case, designed to track his location
through "direction-finding" [23, 24].

* **The Ending:** He succeeds in exposing the program but ends up like
Agee: an exile in a foreign city (perhaps London or Mexico City), still
being followed by surveillance teams, realizing that the "disease" is
not just the agency, but the entire economic system it protects [25-27].

### **Compelling Elements to Include**

* **The Code Name:** Use **KUBARK** (the cryptonym for the CIA) or
**LITEMPO** (the cryptonym for Mexican liaison operations) for an added
layer of authenticity [28, 29].

* **The "Hot Mike":** Have a scene where the antagonist activates a
**"hot mike"**—remotely turning a cradled telephone into a
room-listening device by sending an electrical current down the line
[30].

* **The Ethical Dilemma:** Borrow from the **PCLOB separate
statements**. Have characters debate whether "the presence of the
smallest iota of foreign intelligence in an ocean of USP information"
justifies the sweep [31].

* **The Algorithm Gap:** Use the concept that the algorithm can only
identify what it’s trained for. Rebels could "spoof" the Project FILTER
algorithm by adopting "boring" behavioral patterns—mimicking the
"watching habits" of the ultra-loyalist class to stay hidden [1].

### **Thematic Quote for a Chapter Heading**

You can use your research note: *"Agency posting to squeeze authority
from a statute that does not give it. Manufacturers powers from thin
air"* [13]. This perfectly captures the bureaucratic horror of a
surveillance state expanding its reach through legal gymnastics.
