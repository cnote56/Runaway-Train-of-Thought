---
title: children of a forgetful god
---

In the crevice of a trench deep in the ocean's crust, an earthquake
opens in the darkness. It is an awful plague of knowledge and promise
that feels more promising when uttered from blackened lips and sought by
eager ears. A ship emerges. Long crashed by the seeders of our worlds.

It rises from the ocean floor, revied by the blood of a lost diver
exploring the wreck. Then, the ship rises to the surface. Resting there,
it opens up, and scientists and the military flood in to search and
explore.

We’ve only forgotten the world we had without the gods who fought and
founded our worlds. There were so many promises before the promises and
prayers went unanswered. We are the begotten children of a forgotten
god—an absent father in a horribly mangled world.

We are the lost children of the forgetful god.
