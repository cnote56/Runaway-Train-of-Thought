# Surveillance Protocols

Surveillance Protocols refer to the high-tech, deeply invasive intelligence-gathering and network-monitoring programs managed by the Joint Command and civilian intelligence.

## Codenames & Programs
During briefings, operators and directors access data feeds labeled with iconic surveillance program codenames:
- **YELLOWBOUNCE / CHILLYWATER:** Encryption analysis and active signal intercepts.
- **XKEYSTROKE / PRISIM / CARNIVORE:** Legacy and advanced database scrapers monitoring citizen communications and digital activity.
- **ECHELON / GILGAMESH / TURBINE:** Geographical and satellite-based signal tracking networks.
