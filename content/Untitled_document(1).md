Prologue.

Mid-Atlantic Region, United States Eastern Seaboard, near Washington,
DC. Early one winter morning in the near future. A streak of fire and
burning phosphorus and iron painted a harsh cull to the dawning sky.
Largely unnoticed by the humans below, the radar systems noted the flash
of light for a moment but it was largely forgotten moments after their
screens flashed.

The object causing this disruption failed to escape orbit
