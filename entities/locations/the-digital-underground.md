# The Digital Underground

The Digital Underground refers to a network of shielded, off-the-grid safe houses, basements, and rowhouses throughout the D.C. metropolitan area where individuals can escape the reach of the surveillance state.

## Key Features
- **Basement SCIFs:** Rowhouses (such as the one Trevor uses behind New Hampshire Avenue) outfitted with biometric scanner doors and heavy physical shielding that blocks signals from **[[the-filter]]** implants.
- **Operator Bays:** Cryptographic terminals where independent operators (such as OpOne) interact, monitor military networks, and coordinate physical escapes.
