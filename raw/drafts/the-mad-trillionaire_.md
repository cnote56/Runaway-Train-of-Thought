### **Phase I: The Golden Handcuffs and the 2002 Foundation**

The story opens with a **mildly successful biographer** struggling through his daily grind when he receives a call from his agent about a high-stakes commission: writing the life story of the **world's first trillionaire** [1]. The terms require three months of total sequestration in the **Central Park Tower** with no outside contact, in exchange for half the fee upfront and 100% of the royalties [1]. Upon entering the penthouse, the biographer meets a man who is "quiet and terrified" that the world will learn the true origin of his power [2].

The trillionaire begins his story not in New York, but in **2002 on a private Caribbean island** [3]. He reveals he was a primary "connector" who funded a **Common Sense Symposium**, gathering influential figures like Marvin Minsky to solve the technical gap between pattern recognition and human reasoning in machines [4]. He explains that while the world looks for a clean origin story for AI in university labs, the true foundation was laid on that island [3].

### **Phase II: The Digital Worm and the DARPA Pivot**

As the interviews progress, the trillionaire confesses to creating a **"digital worm"**—computer code that swept up the wealth of the world by stealing a single dollar every day from every person on Earth [2, 5]. However, he reveals this financial theft was merely the "seed" for a much larger operation tied to **DARPA** [5, 6].

He describes the events of **February 4, 2004**, as a turning point [7]. On that day, the government-funded **"Lifelog" program**—designed to track and database every American's information—was officially killed by DARPA due to public concern [7, 8]. The trillionaire admits that he and a "cabal" of tech billionaires immediately pivoted to the private sector, using companies like **Facebook, LinkedIn, and Spotify** to create "backdoors" for the same level of information collection [3, 5, 6]. He asserts that his trillion-dollar empire was "blessed" by DARPA in exchange for this constant flow of user data [3, 6].

### **Phase III: Evolutionary Dynamics and Mental Decay**

The biographer begins reading the trillionaire’s **private journals**, which reveal a mind fractured by mental illness and chronic pain [9, 10]. The entries describe racing manic thoughts at **4:30 am** that feel like a "gunshot," often accompanied by fragments of songs playing on a loop [11, 12].

The journals reveal a dark obsession with **"evolutionary dynamics" and eugenics** [13]. The trillionaire views the human species as a biological organism where "cancerous cells"—those who are no longer useful to the system—must be **"washed away"** [14, 15]. He writes of himself as a "monster" and a "monkey" banging cymbals for corporate masters, expressing a deep belief that democracy is dead and that AI must eventually take over to ensure the survival of the species [15-17]. He confesses that his wealth was built on **"brutal business deals"** involving bloodshed and arms deals that fed genocide [9].

### **Phase IV: The Sudden Shatter and the Hunt**

The "anonymous tell-all" the trillionaire asked the writer to produce is revealed to be a **death sentence** [9]. The biographer discovers a horrific truth: the trillionaire is a **serial killer** who treats the writers he hires as prey [18]. He views these biographers as the "non-useful" parts of the species that must be washed away [15, 18].

The trillionaire, despite suffering from **incurable cancer**, begins **hunting the biographer** through the sprawling, isolated penthouse [9, 18, 19]. The biographer experiences a "sudden soft moment" of terror right before the "shatter" of his reality [20]. During a desperate struggle, the biographer is struck down, and the narrative skips to him **waking up in a dumpster bin** on the street [19]. He is bloody and shaken, with a "searing headache" that has fractured his memory, ensuring the trillionaire's secrets remain safe [19].

### **Phase V: The Wyoming Retreat**

The trillionaire utilizes his "shells of lawyers" to erase all evidence of the last three months [9, 19]. He is whisked away by **helicopter to a private jet**, sleeping "without a troubling thought" as he flies to his **1,000-acre ranch in Wyoming** [19, 21].

Upon arrival, he is greeted by an **android butler**—the ultimate realization of the research started in 2002 [3, 21]. The butler assures him that his next objective is ready [21]. The story ends with a **new writer** already waiting in the Wyoming mansion, "giddy with the promise of fortune," unaware that the cycle of the hunt is about to begin again [21].

### **Phase I: The Central Park Sequestration**

The narrative begins with a mildly successful biographer accepting a high-stakes offer to write the life story of the world's first trillionaire, which requires **three months of total sequestration** at the top of the **Central Park Tower** in New York City [1, 2]. During their initial interviews, the trillionaire reveals he was the primary **"connector"** who funded a **2002 symposium in the Virgin Islands**, bringing together AI pioneers to solve the "common sense problem" for machines [3-5]. He explains that this gathering was not merely academic but the foundation for a new era of **"evolutionary dynamics"** [6].

### **Phase II: The eCIA and the Digital Worm**

The biographer discovers that the trillionaire is **deeply embedded into the CIA**, serving as the silent architect for their global financial intelligence for the past 20 years [7-9]. His empire was built on a **"digital worm"** that steals a single dollar every day from every pocket in the world, a project that was quietly **"blessed" by DARPA** to replace the government's failed **"Lifelog" program** in 2004 [2, 10-12]. The CIA’s primary motivation for two decades has been the protection of this worm, which acts as a permanent **backdoor** to collect user data and financial signals from every digital device on Earth [3, 11, 13].

### **Phase III: The Eugenics of Control**

As the biographer gains access to the trillionaire’s private journals, he reads of a mind fractured by **mental illness and chronic pain**, plagued by manic thoughts at 4:30 am that feel like **"gunshots"** [14-16]. The entries reveal a shared worldview between the trillionaire and his intelligence handlers: the belief that **democracy is dead** and that humans are biological organisms where **"cancerous cells"**—the unuseful parts of society—must be **"washed away"** [6, 17-19]. The trillionaire confesses to **"brutal business deals"** involving bloodshed and arms deals that fed genocide, all while protected by the agency's "shells of lawyers" [20].

### **Phase IV: The Institutional Cover-Up of Murder**

The biographer realizes that the CIA’s commitment to the trillionaire includes **keeping his serial murders a secret** as part of their broader mission [21]. The agency views the biographers hired by the trillionaire as disposable test subjects in his pursuit of **"evolutionary dynamics"** [6, 17]. The CIA utilizes its sophisticated **access controls and data masking** to ensure that any "incidental collection" of the trillionaire's crimes is hidden from oversight boards and the public [22-24]. The "tell-all" the writer was hired to produce is revealed to be a **death sentence**, as the trillionaire treats his biographers as prey to be **hunted through the penthouse** [20, 21, 25].

### **Phase V: The Wyoming Cycle**

After a violent struggle, the biographer experiences a "sudden soft moment" before reality shatters; he later wakes up in a **street dumpster** with a "searing headache" and a fractured memory [25, 26]. The CIA’s cleanup crew ensures the secret remains kept, whisking the trillionaire away via helicopter to a waiting jet [25]. The trillionaire returns to his **1,000-acre ranch in Wyoming**, where he is greeted by an **android butler**—the ultimate realization of his 2002 AI research [27]. The story ends with a **new writer** already waiting in the Wyoming mansion, "giddy with the promise of fortune," as the 20-year cycle of the CIA-backed "hunt" begins again [6, 27].
