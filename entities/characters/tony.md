# Tony

## Overview
Tony is a worn, cynical, yet resilient veteran who lives in [[the-capital-city]] under the oppressive regime of [[the-fatherhood]]. He works as a reclamation laborer, harvesting fixtures and metal from old federal buildings and power lines for reuse. ^[raw/drafts/the-peanut-butter-underground.md]

## Appearance & Traits
- **Physical description:** A limping man with scars trailing over his right knee and thigh, the result of a tattered military past. He has a metal knee and hip. ^[raw/drafts/the-peanut-butter-underground.md]
- **Key traits:** Cynical but enduring. He frequently relies on "numbjuice" to manage the constant bang-aching of his artificial joints and the bitter cold of the decaying city. ^[raw/drafts/the-peanut-butter-underground.md]
- **Attire:** Often wears an old, tattered army coat, similar to many other veterans of the working class. ^[raw/drafts/the-peanut-butter-underground.md]

## Biography & History
Tony is a veteran of three successive wars fought by the Empire.
- **First War:** Left him feeling hollow, spending parts of himself in a distant desert that he never expected to recover. ^[raw/drafts/the-peanut-butter-underground.md]
- **Second War:** A conflict he desperately begged to forget. ^[raw/drafts/the-peanut-butter-underground.md]
- **Final War:** Left him physically shattered and limping, deemed no longer useful as a soldier, and relegated to the camps and eventually reclaimed as a manual worker. ^[raw/drafts/the-peanut-butter-underground.md]

## Narrative Arc & Progression
- **Core Motivation:** Survival in a system designed to wear human beings down to dust.
- **Current Goal:** Evading [[bloxes]], managing his chronic pain, and procuring a higher-grade pain relief substance known as "butter" from the mysterious [[the-underground]].
- **Development History:**
  - In *Book One*, Tony meets contacts in [[the-underground]], escapes curfews and drone surveillance, and becomes deeply entangled with [[personati]] and the distribution of Lucinda's [[peanut-butter]].
  - In *Book Two*, Tony uncovers the ancient timeline of how [[the-fatherhood]] took power by finding a sentient digital Archive. ^[raw/drafts/the-peanut-butter-underground.md]

## Relationships
- **[[illisa]]:** Tony's wife in the upper city blocks, with whom his relationship seems stilted or complicated under the extreme stress of the regime. ^[raw/drafts/the-peanut-butter-underground.md]
- **[[willie]]:** A shifty acquaintance in the alleys who introduces him to the existence of underground "butter". ^[raw/drafts/the-peanut-butter-underground.md]
- **[[personati]]:** A redheaded woman with whom he shares a close, romantic relationship and dwelling in [[the-underground]]. ^[raw/drafts/the-peanut-butter-underground.md]
- **[[lucinda]]:** The feline, alluring provider of [[peanut-butter]] who resides in the subterranean market. ^[raw/drafts/the-peanut-butter-underground.md]
