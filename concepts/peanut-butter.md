# Peanut Butter

## Overview
Peanut Butter (or simply "butter") is a rare, sweet-smelling, and highly sought-after substance distributed deep in [[the-underground]]. Unlike standard rations or chemical numbjuice, "butter" is a highly restorative and nutritional commodity with powerful pain-relieving qualities. It is described as white in color. ^[raw/drafts/the-peanut-butter-underground.md]

## Uses & Qualities
- **Medical/Pain Relief:** Highly superior to "numbjuice" (a common, sweet, intoxicating liquid used by workers to dull pain). It provides deep relief for chronic injuries, such as the constant bang-aching of artificial joints or metal bones. ^[raw/drafts/the-peanut-butter-underground.md]
- **Sustenance:** Used to "feed the masses" of outcasts and refugees seeking shelter in the subterranean tunnels. It acts as a primary source of nutritional sustenance. ^[raw/drafts/the-peanut-butter-underground.md]
- **Metaphorical Significance:** Representing hope, warmth, and basic human dignity, the distribution of this illicit food forms the bedrock of the subterranean rebellion—hence the movement's name: *The Peanut Butter Underground*. ^[raw/drafts/the-peanut-butter-underground.md]

## Subterranean Distribution
- The primary provisioner is the mysterious [[lucinda]], who packages the substance in plastic jars inside her subterranean shop. ^[raw/drafts/the-peanut-butter-underground.md]
- Because it is highly restricted and outlawed by [[the-fatherhood]]'s rationing directives, it is bought and sold using high-value salvage (such as gold or smiths reclaimed from ruins). ^[raw/drafts/the-peanut-butter-underground.md]
