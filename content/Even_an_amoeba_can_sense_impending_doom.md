---
title: Even an amoeba can sense impending doom
---

Even an amoeba can sense impending doom. And here I am staring at the
business end of the wrong kind of business.

Call me stupid. Because I slept through my business law classes. Which
sucks because I was a tax lawyer once. I should have known the outcome
of this kind of business. It is never good for the numbers guy. I looked
to my left and then to my right. I was the numbers guy standing in the
middle of a very bad situation.

The smell of cocaine is heavy in the air. Even past the plastic
curtains. The light brown and black skin tones of the girls in their
bras and panties with respirators over their mouths and noses. They cut,
bag, and label the mounds of white powder on the tables they surround.

I threw my halo down the hill long ago. And I’ve never felt better then
I did at that moment. It’s been a downward spiral since jump street with
me.
