# Lucinda

## Overview
Lucinda is a seductive and feline merchant who runs a subterranean shop in [[the-underground]]. She is the "great provisioner" of [[peanut-butter]], a highly prized restorative pain-relief ointment and food substance. ^[raw/drafts/the-peanut-butter-underground.md]

## Appearance & Traits
- **Physical description:** An attractive woman who dresses in a tight skirt and a thin blouse that barely covers her upper half. She displays heavily feline/cat-like attributes: a "purring rasp" to her voice, "claws" that she licks or uses to hold items, and a kitten-like playfulness. ^[raw/drafts/the-peanut-butter-underground.md]
- **Key traits:** Seductive, business-savvy, playful, and sharp-witted. She trades jars of white peanut butter for valuable artifacts and gold salvaged by reclaimers. ^[raw/drafts/the-peanut-butter-underground.md]

## Biography & History
- She has established a hidden store deep within the dark, wet tunnels of [[the-underground]] (the sewage network below the capital city). Here, she serves as a vital provider of life-sustaining "butter" to the outcasts, rebels, and aching workers. ^[raw/drafts/the-peanut-butter-underground.md]

## Narrative Arc & Progression
- **Core Motivation:** Amassing wealth/valuables from reclaimers while maintaining her secure, highly sought-after position as the primary supplier of [[peanut-butter]] in the underworld.
- **Current Goal:** Exchanging her precious jars with [[tony]] and [[willie]], teasing Tony during his visits, and expanding her influence over the subterranean refugees. ^[raw/drafts/the-peanut-butter-underground.md]

## Relationships
- **[[tony]]:** She holds a highly sexualized, playful relationship with Tony. She affectionately calls him "Tony, Darlin'" or "Luci" in return, once plunging a claw-full of white peanut butter directly into his open mouth. ^[raw/drafts/the-peanut-butter-underground.md]
- **[[willie]]:** A frequent trading partner who buys jars of peanut butter to "feed the masses." ^[raw/drafts/the-peanut-butter-underground.md]
- **[[personati]]:** She engages in light, competitive tension with Personati, who displays jealousy over Lucinda's provocative attitude toward [[tony]]. ^[raw/drafts/the-peanut-butter-underground.md]
