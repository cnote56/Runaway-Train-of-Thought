Confessions of a DFS Player

By Cole Dowden

Nobody else would bother to write this; so I had to.

Into the depths. Into the bowels of the daily fantasy grime and greed. Perhaps you’re winning now, but you’ve lost in the long game.

You're a victim, just as I. I've been there, ignoring my work and scouring twitter for the edge. Refreshing the lineups obsessively. Double checking projections up until the picks locked and the games began.

I refuse to name names in this. I just want us all to enjoy this process, but unfortunately that is not going to happen. For half of us to win, the other half must lose. For one to win, 99.9% must lose. Those are just the numbers folks and the house gets the rake. We get raked. And we like it. We like the rub, the slap, and the tickle. That's the hunt. This is the new challenge. Pick the guys that get the most points and you're a genius. Miss it by a few points and you're the goat sitting in the cubicle farm pining for greatness the day after.

It's okay. Consider this group therapy.  Hi, my name is Cole. And I'm an idiot daily fantasy sports player. Err, actually... Daily Fantasy Playee.

I love the challenge, but I'm not going to humor myself with trying to predict the future. I will however strive for greatness. I will however throw down a few bucks at the chance that my insight and skill just might payoff.

So this is the beginning. This is it. Feel free to chime in. I'll be wrong. You'll be wrong. So let's be wrong together. No algorithm is going to hit every time. Twitter is meant for liars.

Prove me wrong, folks. Send me your lineup. I'll watch it fade and wither as the clock ticks down.

I'm going to focus on what we all strive for in this column. I'm going to ignore the haters and try to put together some good matchups with some potential. Really folks, thats it.

I don’t use an algorithm. I don’t have a spreadsheet with projections and what-if’s casting divinations into the digital tea leaves. I do, however, have a serious interest/passion/problem for daily fantasy sports. I honestly don’t care which sport it is. I like the thinking and the analysis.

I'm going to ignore you. I'm going to throw out some ideas and they will likely bounce off your thick skull. You will yell at the computer screen and curse my name, but this is part of the process. We'll learn together that fantasy sports is a dogfight. Daily Fantasy Sports is a pit and you and I are chained against each other.

Let the best DFSP’er win.

We begin with fantasy soccer. Because you are a hater. No, Wayne Rooney will not score you thirty points this weekend.

However, some “one-name mega star in his home country” will score  two goals in the first half and cure cancer on the pitch at halftime. You won’t see it coming. You. Won’t. See. Any. Of. It. The match was broadcast at 6 am on a Saturday morning. Like hell you’re going to get up at that ungodly hour after catching the west coast friday night basketball games at the [insert absurdly named local sports bar here]. But I am going to watch it. I’m going to enjoy the matches. You’re going to read the column I write and follow for recent posts on twitter. You’re going to start to give a shit about Daily Fantasy Soccer because it’s interesting. Think of it as the chess of Daily Fantasy Sports.

Let's begin with a lesson in humility. You're going to lose. Because the Daily Fantasy Sports Gods are a fickle pack of bastards.

For every guy on twitter that says his algorithm is the tits and he is rolling in the winnings, remember twitter is place for liars, scallywags, and nerf-herders. Welcome to the jungle. Let’s begin.
