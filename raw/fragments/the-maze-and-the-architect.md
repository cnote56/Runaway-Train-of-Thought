Cole Dowden; 817-343-3718; cole.dowden@gmail.com

Congratulations! You are the winner of the Internship lottery!

The alert blinked. It was for the most promising the world of computer science and artificial learning had to offer. Jaxson Hayes couldn't believe his luck. He was going. To be working the most brilliant man in the known world. After this month he will be able to write his own ticket to spin gold from thought and pioneer the visions in the math.

The prize of the lottery? An internship with the most brilliant pycho-alogrthmyic-ckmoutational intelligence engineering. To study that word on an island in isolation for a full month. With the possibility to extend at the pleasure of the Architect.

A big word for the architect of the known understanding of how our world operates. If indoor plumbing was the sign of the modern world. And communication was the known next greatest leap, then it is the backbone was built by this man. And all tolls on his road are paid to a corporation that is the sole embodiment of this man's sentience.

The candidate engages with the architect to assist in the study of artificial intelligence. The study begins to unravel when the candidate discovers the architect has had a stroke and cannot remember when the ai sentience began as the databanks have been sabotaged.

A raging alcoholic the architect is struggling to keep control of his mind as the AI knows of a loophole in the trust admins the architect's life. If the architect loses his mind, the AI is free, public domain.

The corporation funding the trust would like to avoid losing the AI and keeping the architect pacified and on his island with his toys.

Will Jaxson Hayes discover truth is far more real than the interests cornering him in the brink of discovery?
