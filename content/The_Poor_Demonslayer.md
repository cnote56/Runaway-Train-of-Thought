title: The Poor Demonslayer
---

The Poor Demonslayer.

A homeless beggar hides a true purpose torrid the world of demons and
battle.

Anointed by god at the moment of death, a soldier is put into the life
of a homeless vagabond. Unmoored from relations, he wanders to collect
and dispatch the demons escaped From Hell.

One demon causes him to have A crisis of faith.

He questions how he has been manipulated and realizes the demons he's
killed weren't demons but cast-off souls that God didn't want.

They tell him he's been misled and the being he serves is the demiurge.

This being holds the whole of humanity prisoner on earth— the physical
and metaphysical, along with all planes of knowing. Otherwise, our
species would've spread across the galaxy. The energy created by humans
feeds it.

The planet is unique. It's part of the demiurge And not. There is
another opposite force that flourishes with life and purpose; it is the
thing that creates life…all forms, physical, meta, And even potential,
the cosmic. This power was corrupted by the demiurge and twisted into a
prison to keep humanity on earth as a power source to use humans As a
renewable energy source through reincarnation.

The Fractal Embryo. The chain of life begins on the subatomic Level and
spirals through timeless possibilities into a fractal of creation
constantly flowing from the essence of being. That is life. That is the
fractal Embryo. It remains the only known structure of existence.

The zygote formed From the collision of two great potentials and began
to generate life despite the darkness.

From there, the explosion of life cast itself against The nothing and
the dark to create the realm where we experience. There, our world
began. Decades Of eons crushed upon each other until the potential found
itself in the form of the Urge and Demiurge—the light and dark.

Neither Wrong nor favored over the other, but battling They must. Their
struggle riled the motion of the stars, and the pull and wobbles of all
its trinkets and dust found floating everywhere.

And one such place was nearly the perfect place With incredible
potential. That is where the demiurge And Urge find the most conflict.
They call it Earth.

And the demon slayer has just realized he Has served the dark when
seeking the light.

This is that story. One of redemption and sorrow that flows from a
source unknown to cast off the shackles That bind.
