---
title: Club Dragon
---

True evil never dies. What you are about to read is an account of what
happens to the disappeared. The dictators. The monsters of our
totalitarian fears. Just behind the official history and often a
gruesome “public” death is the real history. Stalin. Hitler. Pinochet.
Lenin. Che. These names are but a small sample of the club membership.
To be admitted they were forced to give up everything. But they are not
victims. Rather they were saved from their victims only to find that
membership into the Dragon Club was more than they bargained for.
