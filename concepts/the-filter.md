# The FILTER / Secure-Link

The **FILTER** is an advanced cybernetic communications and intelligence implant placed behind the user's right eye, next to the cerebellum. 

## Functionality
- **Instant Data Overlays:** Displays real-time operational briefs, data-streams, and communication feeds directly onto the user's retina.
- **Secure Mesh Network:** Creates an encrypted, continuous connection between government officials, military commanders, and civilian directors.
- **Sensory Projection:** Capable of projecting sensory stimulation, auditory prompts, and data visualizations directly to the user's brain.

## Side Effects & Pitfalls
- **The Rattle:** Users describe a constant, physical "buzzing" or "pinging" next to their brains, causing extreme nerve rattle and sleep deprivation.
- **Focus Deficit:** Over-reliance on the stream leads to deficits in organic focus and physical coordination.
- **Escapism & Addiction:** Users struggle to unplug, feeling a profound sense of isolation and sensory disorientation when the link is disconnected.
