Buried in the Veins

Synopsis: An aimless man submits his DNA to an ancestry service. He is
intrigued to discover he had ancestors in places he never knew before.
Then he discovers a disturbing pattern of death and misfortune in the
veins of his family tree. Patterns so apparent this leads him to
question: Were his forefathers' serial killers?

More so, this discovery unlocks something sinister that he can not
ignore.
