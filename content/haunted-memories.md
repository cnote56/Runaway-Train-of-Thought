He called again this week. He just keeps calling. He talks for about ten
minutes and then hangs up. Why does he haunt me? Why won’t he just stop it!
He never cared about me when I lived up the street or even in the same
house! My poor children are shocked. They found out yesterday. I am going
to die soon. We cried and cried. Sometimes we will take a night and spend
it together. I’m trying so hard to give them just one last memory. But, the
pain of time burns so hot. My husband has done all he can and so much more.
But, now even he is weakening. I tell my children I love them and I tell
them behave. I look into their eyes, the both of them. A boy and a girl,
twins at birth. They’ve grown so fast. Soon they will grow without me.

The doctors didn’t believe me. I wouldn’t them what was hurting and they
wouldn’t even listen. For my own doctors not to care nearly broke me. But
my husband whom has always been there for me he cared he fired those
doctors and found some that believed me.
