God Dies From Cancer...

Or how I learned to love the end of everything.

You are not long for this kind of treatment. It will kill you. But so
will everything that you desire. It's not the things, it's the want.
That is the killer. We can embrace all the elevations of all the
substance in this realm only to forget how we are nothing. Love that
base materials folded and stuffed into leaky packaging.

The malformed bones of my mother have plagued her existence for ages.
Pinching and grinding across the tendons and connective tissues of her
frail body. She's given so much and yet it's taking more. There is no
logic to this. There is not rhyme to it. There is no reason in
suffering.

The actions of the day and what it should make are the mysteries of why
we are where we are today.

God Dies of Cancer. And we gave him the gift.
