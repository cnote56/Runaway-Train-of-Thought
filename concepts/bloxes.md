# Bloxes

## Overview
Bloxes are autonomous, flying drone secret police developed and deployed by [[the-fatherhood]]. They act as omniscient, overhead sentinels, enforcing absolute compliance through real-time biometric tracking, curfew enforcement, and brutal chemical suppression. ^[raw/drafts/the-peanut-butter-underground.md]

## Characteristics & Design
- **Motto/Operating Directive:** "Sempre Supra. Always Above." ^[raw/drafts/the-peanut-butter-underground.md]
- **Artificial Intelligence:** Programmed by the power-mad elite, these "Thinking-Drones" were trained on decades of smartphone, dashcam, and webcamera data. ^[raw/drafts/the-peanut-butter-underground.md]
- **Physical Forms:**
  - **Standard Patrols:** Floating, rectangular flying boxes. Some are larger than others but share the same blocky utility. ^[raw/drafts/the-peanut-butter-underground.md]
  - **Bear-Sized Bloxes:** Heavy-duty drones designed to handle cargo, logistics, and heavy transport throughout the city. ^[raw/drafts/the-peanut-butter-underground.md]
  - **Collection Bloxes:** Floating dumpsters and clean-up drones that descend to clear debris and salvage valuable materials. ^[raw/drafts/the-peanut-butter-underground.md]

## Capabilities & Behaviors
- **Patrol and Curfew:** Drones patrol blocks all night. Anything larger than a cat that is caught outdoors after hours is immediately targeted, sprayed unconscious, and dragged into magistrate chambers. ^[raw/drafts/the-peanut-butter-underground.md]
- **Wall-Clinging and Charging:** When wind conditions are too extreme for flight, standard Bloxes adhere directly to building walls and sidewalks, recharging their internal batteries via "power-taps" vampired from electrical couplings. Even while charging, their optical sensors remain active and vigilant. ^[raw/drafts/the-peanut-butter-underground.md]
- **Chemical Suppression:** Equipped with a chemical dispersal system that sprays a "noxious red liquid." The liquid is highly toxic, causing intense respiratory burning and immediate unconsciousness, making resistance impossible. ^[raw/drafts/the-peanut-butter-underground.md]
- **Identification & Scanning:** Drones emit a blue ultraviolet light to scan faces. When directed at "leaders" or key targets, the light turns their faces an unnatural shade of lemon yellow to flag them for immediate detention by gray-clad ground squads. ^[raw/drafts/the-peanut-butter-underground.md]
- **Voice Capabilities:** Broadcast digitized, metallic voices to issue commands and state legal violations (e.g., "This is the Blox! Stop Resisting! You are in violation of Judgment 34J-426!"). ^[raw/drafts/the-peanut-butter-underground.md]
