He smiled at me and tipped his old gray face downward. The straw hat was
worn and sun beaten. His teeth all three colors of them smiled brightly and
surely at me.

I didn’t know him. He was just some old man in an old truck with a rusty
trailer hooked to the back of it.

In just one smile, we had an entire conversation. In just one moment not
even a tick on my pocket watch that old gray-yellow-black toothed man and
me. We had our moment.

By the way have you ever wondered why pocket watches have seemed to drop
from sight and market? Could be no one wants to take the time to find the
time? They just want it strapped to their wrist.

Ticking right along with their pulse. Tick…tick

Uh oh better find something to occupy you time , cause the pulse on your
wrist is slowing down a little too much.
