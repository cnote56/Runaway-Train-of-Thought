# Amaymon (King of the South)

## Overview
Amaymon is the King of the South, commanding all spirits of the southern quarter. Extremely perilous to summon, he is a great and mighty king who acts as an executive administrative portal for higher philosophy, the *Ars Notoria* (speed-learning), and the acquisition of high social standing.^[[raw/drafts/mythos-and-magic-backup-repository.md]]

## Appearance & Traits
- **Venerable and Wild:** Appears in the likeness of an old man with long hair hanging over his eyes and a great beard. His ears are distinctively horse-like, and he wears a shining bright crown.^[[raw/drafts/mythos-and-magic-backup-repository.md]]
- **Royal Procession:** Rides upon a fierce, ramping lion that roars at his first appearance. He holds a rod (or dart) in his right hand, preceded by dancers and a full band of musicians.^[[raw/drafts/mythos-and-magic-backup-repository.md]]
- **Sacrificial Preference:** Unlike other air kings, he gladly accepts sacrifices, burnt offerings, and gifts from the operator.^[[raw/drafts/mythos-and-magic-backup-repository.md]]

## Biography & History
- **The Perilous Philosopher:** He represents the apex of philosophical and intellectual expansion within the southern network grid, though his extreme volatility makes him one of the most dangerous spirits to deal with. He can be safely kept in obedience for only one hour.^[[raw/drafts/mythos-and-magic-backup-repository.md]]

## Relationships
- **Satan:** Co-manages the atmospheric matrix under Satan's oversight.^[[raw/drafts/mythos-and-magic-backup-repository.md]]
- **Asmoday:** The first of the twelve noble spirits under his command, who teaches arithmetic, astronomy, geometry, and music.^[[raw/drafts/mythos-and-magic-backup-repository.md]]
- **Messengers:** Assisted by his three chief messengers: **Emlon** (or Emelon), **Ocarbidaton** (or Ocar/Cydaton), and **Maditon** (or Madycon).^[[raw/drafts/mythos-and-magic-backup-repository.md]]\n
