# Satan

## Overview
Satan is the third of the Three Devils bound in the art of Necromancy, originally belonging to the **Throne of Cherubim**. Uniquely, Satan resides in the "obscure air" rather than being cast directly into Hell, as he did not fall entirely of his own will. He is a destructive force of chaos, failure, and spiritual blindness, possessing a lingering desire to possess his former heavenly throne.^[[raw/drafts/mythos-and-magic-backup-repository.md]]

## Appearance & Traits
- **Obsidian Air:** Exists within a dense, hazy, and obscure atmospheric suspension rather than a physical infernal shape, though he can be conjured to appear in a basin of clean water.^[[raw/drafts/mythos-and-magic-backup-repository.md]]
- **Deceptive and Covetous:** Persistently seeks to regain his lost throne, attempting to manipulate operators into praying for his restoration—a request the Master must strictly ignore.^[[raw/drafts/mythos-and-magic-backup-repository.md]]
- **Vast Destructive Power:** Governs the power to fail projects, destroy, make blind, and enact widespread mischief.^[[raw/drafts/mythos-and-magic-backup-repository.md]]

## Biography & History
- **The Wilderness Tempter:** Famous for tempting human ancestors in the desert or wilderness, instigating disobedience against the divine will.^[[raw/drafts/mythos-and-magic-backup-repository.md]]
- **The Water Basin Invention:** A specific protocol to conjure Satan in a basin of water was documented by the friar William Bacon, allowing daily scrying (excluding holy days) without the need for a child medium.^[[raw/drafts/mythos-and-magic-backup-repository.md]]

## Relationships
- **Lucifer:** Operates within the overarching hierarchy of Hell, despite his unique suspension in the "obscure air."^[[raw/drafts/mythos-and-magic-backup-repository.md]]
- **The Four Kings:** Satan is well-acquainted with [[orions-of-the-east]], [[paymon-of-the-west]], [[amaymon-of-the-south]], and [[egine-of-the-north]], whose domains span the lands, seas, and trees of the mortal plane.^[[raw/drafts/mythos-and-magic-backup-repository.md]]\n
