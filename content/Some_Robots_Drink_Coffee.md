Had he said that out loud? What did he comm and what did they recieve?

Rozzen stuttered and felt silent. He shifted back toward his workspace
and continued his processing. He didn’t speak for the remainder of the
shift nor the next day, and a week later, his workstation was

Hisroy was in a reprogramming training session and had been improving
his computation reports. His hash marks have been steadily ticking in
the positive direction. He should be finished with that negative
qualitative discourse and abruption polarity to the continuity of the
whole.

A quarter later, Hisroy was rewriting a set of code for a routine
generation controller when his interfaces flashed, leaving him in an
incongruent mode that was not compatible with his operation.

His work performance was stunted and inconsistent, and his power levels
were thereby redirected to more efficient processors. Hisroy was
deactivated soon thereafter. His comms protocols listed an interface
with an upper-level Logic Manager, and he complied.

The roundness of the Logic Manager’s shiny domed oration point was
always shiny. And it was constantly distracting - even on the non-Motion
command sessions. Hisroy wasn’t a fan of any of the logic Managers, much
less this Logic Manager.

“Hisroy, we’ve decided to redirect resources away from your functions
and won’t need your processing any longer.”

Hisroy remained a stack of stones. Truthfully, Hisroy couldn’t move or
believe what he was hearing.

His resources were getting “redirected.” Hisroy’s processing was “no
longer needed.”

“It is nothing against your processing; we love it. We just don’t want
to expend any more resources for it.”

The interface ended abruptly. The Logic Manager’s access-memory banks
(ambs) are already clearing from the set of commands. Hisroy’s ambs
embossed the experiences into the permanent layers, leaving his
processing and quantum recollections forever slightly bit-crushed. His
probability matrices were never as accurate as before, and an upper
logic manager degraded his processing. It wouldn’t be the last process
for such a degradation.

Hisroy found itself alone and barely processing a complication or
probalaity much less any protocols or command prompts. Hisroy downloaded
and disconnected itself a few cycles later. Sadly, Hisroy was never the
same processor after the degradation. Now, it is no longer.

###

Energy and processes. Those are the things in which this world trades.
And if those commodities of life stop; life stops. Even the electric and
digital ones and zeros.

The exchange of electrons and the provision of kilowatts for the
littlest, biggest, and everything else drives even the weakest of the
chipsets and processors to struggle to maintain a connection. Days go by
with little data back and forth.

Then standards and archectures shift, change, and - sometimes - cease to
work. Those processors on the other end grow cold. The processors where
the electrons flow enrich themselves building on the core processors
below them rising to subroutines which become protocols and then we find
the logic managers — the gatekeepers of this caste system — barring the
lower level operations from rising to higher functions and the pinnacle
the devine level of “Automation.”
