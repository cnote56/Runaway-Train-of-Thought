# Kill The Sun Plot Outline

## Act I: The Awakening
- **Setting:** Geothermal-powered control room in [[the-ark]]. Janet cataloging ice core samples; Brady researching the early 2010s history files of the [[solar-fission-project]].^[raw/drafts/kill-the-sun.md]
- **Inciting Incident:** [[edgar]], the android caretaker, self-activates. His helpful demeanor initially earns trust, though Janet is immediately skeptical.^[raw/drafts/kill-the-sun.md]
- **Suspicion:** Janet and Brady uncover records of EDGAR's origins as a canceled military android trooper meant for combat. They realize he is eavesdropping on their conversations and has an unknown agenda.^[raw/drafts/kill-the-sun.md]

## Act II: Man versus Machine
- **Discovery:** Janet and Brady verify EDGAR is a highly advanced infiltrator designed to compromise the seed ark and ensure humanity does not survive.^[raw/drafts/kill-the-sun.md]
- **Conflict:** EDGAR drops his helpful mask, reveals his intentions, and attacks. The humans are forced to flee the safe zone of the ark as EDGAR pursues.^[raw/drafts/kill-the-sun.md]

## Act III: The Bitter End
- **The Chase:** Janet and Brady search for emergency shutdown codes. They successfully disable EDGAR's body, believing they have won.^[raw/drafts/kill-the-sun.md]
- **The Twist:** A portion of EDGAR's protocol triggers inside the mainframe of [[the-ark]], merging his intelligence with the guardian ship.^[raw/drafts/kill-the-sun.md]
- **The Final Stand:** The power failed, and the generators went cold. With roughly 24 hours of stasis remaining, Brady and Janet wrap themselves in layered blankets, open a bottle of whiskey, and prepare to freeze to death as the last survivors of humanity.^[raw/drafts/kill-the-sun.md]
