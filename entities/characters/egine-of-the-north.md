# Egine (King of the North)

## Overview
Egine (also known as Egin, Egyn, or Iigin) is the King of the North, presiding over the northern aerial quarter and managing the Order-5 Mars Server. He is a profound teacher of sciences, singing, music, and highly advanced occult memory arts such as *Ars Notoria*, *Nigromancy*, and *Art Memoratina*.^[[raw/drafts/mythos-and-magic-backup-repository.md]]

## Appearance & Traits
- **Terrifying Presence:** Appears in the likeness of a man with an eerily clear, pale, and bright face. His nostrils are sharp like swords, and his mouth erupts with torrents of fire and smoke. Two massive ivory tusks protrude from his cheeks.^[[raw/drafts/mythos-and-magic-backup-repository.md]]
- **Serpentine Companions:** Two large, shining, iridescent serpents (a viper and a cobra) coil and hiss on his right side.^[[raw/drafts/mythos-and-magic-backup-repository.md]]
- **Mount & Entourage:** Rides upon a massive, ancient dragon of obsidian scales, arriving amidst dark, stormy skies and a great clamor. He is crowned with precious stones, and preceded by floating instruments and sweet organs.^[[raw/drafts/mythos-and-magic-backup-repository.md]]
- **Called Alone Protocol:** If called alone, he appears much more gently and slowly, bringing along three other subservient kings rather than his full terrifying host.^[[raw/drafts/mythos-and-magic-backup-repository.md]]

## Biography & History
- **The Scholar of the North:** Teaches perfect music, geography, and cosmology, revealing secret, hidden things about the earth’s structure and the composition of the universe. He has the power to alter physical properties, gain friendships, and raise operators to high dignity.^[[raw/drafts/mythos-and-magic-backup-repository.md]]

## Relationships
- **Satan:** Known and mapped under Satan's regional network.^[[raw/drafts/mythos-and-magic-backup-repository.md]]
- **Ozia:** The first of his twelve chief spirits, who teaches arts, invisibility, and grants favor with enemies.^[[raw/drafts/mythos-and-magic-backup-repository.md]]
