Single bullet theory

It only took one. Really didn’t feel like I thought it would. The slam to
the chest was like all the times I’ve heard a survivor talk about it. Like
a sledge hammer leveled against your sternum.  How the bullet would slam
into their Kevlar vest and knock them to the ground and the wind out of
their lungs. Like Tyson took a gauged punch at their chests. But I was
never punched. I never saw it coming like you see in the movies. I just got
this hell of a pain cut into the center of my chest. It got hard to
breathe, my throat filled with blood and that was it. Not even enough time
to shout out a prayer. I guess that’s why I’m a believer in the single
bullet theory.
