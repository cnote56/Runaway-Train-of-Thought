# The Fatherhood

## Overview
The Fatherhood is the supreme, totalitarian technocratic regime that commands the remnants of the Americas after a devastating global war. They are composed of an elite class of idealists, logicians, chaos calculators, and pontificators of theory. They exercise absolute control over citizens' lives, movements, and labor. ^[raw/drafts/the-peanut-butter-underground.md]

## Core Beliefs & Structure
- **Motto/Philosophy:** "All things serve the people. The people serve the Fatherhood. All things serve the Fatherhood." ^[raw/drafts/the-peanut-butter-underground.md]
- **The Elite:** "Fathers of Society"—the central planners, logicians, and "Chaos Calcutists" who translate theory directly into law. They maintain a stilted "socialized credit score" that determines rations, housing, and social privileges. ^[raw/drafts/the-peanut-butter-underground.md]
- **The Working Class:** Relegated to strict "work requirements" for basic rations and shelter, performing grueling tasks like reclaiming old city infrastructures under the constant threat of violent punishment. ^[raw/drafts/the-peanut-butter-underground.md]

## Mechanisms of Control
- **[[bloxes]]:** Autonomous, flying drone secret police trained on decades of smartphone, dash, and webcamera data. They monitor every movement, enforce curfew, and spray non-conformers unconscious. ^[raw/drafts/the-peanut-butter-underground.md]
- **The Pedwalls:** Physical steel or concrete walls that rise and divide the city's residential and industrial blocks, strictly controlling traffic and isolating citizens into designated HomeBlocks. ^[raw/drafts/the-peanut-butter-underground.md]
- **Re-education Centers:** Facilities where non-conformers are sent to be beaten, broken, and reformed. The regime's stance is absolute: "Reform or death; there is no other exit than death for the non-conformers." ^[raw/drafts/the-peanut-butter-underground.md]
- **Resource Control:** Rations, chocolate, and "numbjuice" are heavily controlled and distributed based on compliance, socialized credit scores, and pairing rituals. ^[raw/drafts/the-peanut-butter-underground.md]

## Historical Background
- The technocracy mutated out of the decline, failure, and destruction of the United States of America.
- Subsequent wars carved the Americas into feudalistic, desperate fiefdoms and unstable alliances.
- Long-term climatic shifts and famines decimated the population, allowing the Fatherhood to sell "solidarity" and "technological safety" in exchange for the absolute erosion of human liberty. ^[raw/drafts/the-peanut-butter-underground.md]
