# Willie

## Overview
Willie (or Willy) is a contact and organizer within [[the-underground]]. He acts as an intermediary who acquires [[peanut-butter]] and other resources to distribute among the subterranean refugees who have fled [[the-fatherhood]]. ^[raw/drafts/the-peanut-butter-underground.md]

## Appearance & Traits
- **Key traits:** Shifty, pragmatic, and community-minded. He knows the back tunnels, sewage systems, and alleyways of [[the-capital-city]] and is highly familiar with evading [[bloxes]]. ^[raw/drafts/the-peanut-butter-underground.md]

## Biography & History
- Willie operates within the shadows of the city's alleyways and sewage tunnels. He has established connections with suppliers like [[lucinda]] and works to keep the aching, laboring population fed and medicated with "butter" to help them endure the harsh manual work requirements. ^[raw/drafts/the-peanut-butter-underground.md]

## Narrative Arc & Progression
- **Core Motivation:** Supporting the subterranean resistance and ensuring survival of the masses in [[the-underground]].
- **Current Goal:** Sourcing multiple jars of [[peanut-butter]] from [[lucinda]]'s shop, utilizing [[tony]] as a runner, and building up stockpiles for the growing refugee population. ^[raw/drafts/the-peanut-butter-underground.md]

## Relationships
- **[[tony]]:** Willie is Tony's primary link to the subterranean trade. He sends Tony on dangerous runs to Lucinda's store, providing him with bags of trade goods. ^[raw/drafts/the-peanut-butter-underground.md]
- **[[lucinda]]:** Trades valuables (gold, smiths) with Lucinda for jars of her sweet, white peanut butter. ^[raw/drafts/the-peanut-butter-underground.md]
- **[[toro]]:** An associate in the subterranean tunnels of the resistance. ^[raw/drafts/the-peanut-butter-underground.md]
