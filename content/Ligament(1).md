---
title: Ligament(1)
---

You never get used to the sound.

The release.

The relax.

The absence of resistance.

It happens right when the one that gives away right before the bone
does.

Everyone fears a broken bone. But they never think about the ties that
bind.

Bits of elastic coil connect every moving bit of ourselves.

You don't move without them,even if your bones remain intact.

I learned this at a young age.

I learned that my joints had that extra give that others didn't.

You never forget the sound.

Is it a pop? Or a tear? I hear it so often in my line of work that
honestly, I try not think about it. But I admit, it is satisfying.

They give up so quickly afterwards. No fight left. Still life but no
fight. But I count them dead, because there is no fight without life.

My name is Billy. I like to fight. I don't lose.

They call me, “ Pension,” because guys tend to retire after matching up
with me.
