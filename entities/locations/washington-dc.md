# Washington D.C.

Washington D.C. is the political epicenter of the United States. In the universe of Project FILTER, the entire city, especially Capitol Hill and executive offices, is fully integrated into a continuous digital communications mesh.

## Key Areas
- **Capitol Hill & Speaker's Offices:** The high-stress congressional halls where Speaker Baker, Reggie Watts, and Jessica Powell manage legislative battles.
- **Congressional SCIFs:** Sealed, heavily shielded Sensitive Compartmented Information Facilities where General Robinson, Clemens, and Senator Ammerstand debate top-secret intelligence warrants in closed-door sessions.
