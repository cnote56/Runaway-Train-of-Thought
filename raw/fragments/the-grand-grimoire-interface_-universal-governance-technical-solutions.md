To keep the complex mathematics of universal governance approachable for a layman, your technical solution should focus on a “Digital Kamea Interface” that automates the formulas while allowing players to focus on the narrative of hacking the cosmic firewall.

The following task outline provides the structural and gameplay requirements for a developer to create a dice app or random number generator (RNG) tailored to our established character types and the source material.


### 1. Core Task: The Magic Constant Engine


The primary function of the app is to manage the "Magic Constant ($M$)", which acts as the target health or stability for any given "Planetary Server" 1, 2.

Requirements: The system must pre-load the seven classical planetary orders ($n$) and their corresponding constants ($M$):

Order 3 (Saturn): Constant 15 1, 2.

Order 4 (Jupiter): Constant 34 1, 2.

Order 8 (Mercury): Constant 260 2.

Gameplay Logic: Instead of players doing long-form addition, the app should allow them to "drop" integers (generated by dice rolls) into a grid. The app then visually calculates the sum of rows, columns, and diagonals in real-time, highlighting imbalances that represent "uncoordinated activity" 1, 3.


### 2. RNG Logic: The "Coordinate Roll" System


To interact with the 8x8 Mercury grid (which contains numbers 1–64), the RNG must simulate the d88 (Coordinate) system discussed in our history.

Requirements:

Generate a pair of integers (1–8) representing the X (Row) and Y (Column) of the grid.

Trigger a "Fetch" command where the app identifies the number at those coordinates (e.g., Coordinate 1,1 in an Order 3 square is the number 8) 4.

Layman Approach: The player simply hits "Scan Firewall," and the app tells them which specific "cell" or "frequency" they have successfully accessed 5.


### 3. Character-Specific Interface Settings


The settings must reflect the character's relationship with the "leash and collar" of creation 6.


#### The Mathematician: Complexity Scaler


Requirement: An "Optimization Toggle." This character deals with massive permutations—up to 17.7 quintillion solutions for an Order-6 square 7.

Gameplay: The app should allow the Mathematician to "lock" a proven row. Once they prove a truth, the app marks it as "Eternally True" and it can no longer be altered by enemy interference 8.


#### The Occultist: Nominal Protection Buffer


Requirement: A "Sacred Name Overlay." The Occultist uses Nominal Squares filled with names like Messias and Tetragrammaton to create a "pressurized environment" 9, 10.

Gameplay: The app provides a "Shield Integrity" bar. Every successful roll adds a "Holy Name" to the perimeter, protecting the player from being "slain in their circle" by the demon Taphthartharath (Frequency 2080) 2, 11.


#### The Cosmologist: Pivot Alignment


Requirement: A "Center Axis Tracker." The Cosmologist focuses on the primordial center (the number 5 in a 3x3 grid) as the cosmic axis 12, 13.

Gameplay: The app gives the Cosmologist a bonus for any roll that balances an "opposing pair" around the center (e.g., 1 and 9 in a Saturn grid) 13.


### 4. The Artist (Genius) Special Advantage: Jovial Counter-Balance


The player who selects the Artist receives a unique mechanical advantage based on the Dürer Square 14. In the sources, the Artist uses the Order-4 Jupiter Square as a talisman to "counteract the devastating and gloomy influence of Saturn" (the planet of melancholy and stasis) 14.

The "Artist’s Advantage" Requirements:

Melancholy Healing: If the group is "immobilized by gloom" or creative stasis during a hack, the Artist can trigger a "Jovial Reset" 14, 15.

Geometric Flexibility: Unlike other classes, the Artist can rotate or reflect the current grid sigil 2, 4.

Technical Implementation: The app should feature a "Rotate" button that flips the numerical values across the diagonal. This allows the Artist to fix a broken Magic Constant without rerolling the dice, representing their mastery of "ideal beauty" and geometry 8, 16.


### 5. Failure Condition: The Breach Alert


Requirement: A "Total Sum Monitor." The app must track the total sum of the grid (e.g., 2080 for Mercury) 2.

Gameplay: If the players fail to balance the Magic Constant within a certain number of turns, the app triggers a "Breach Event" where the demon (e.g., Sorath or Zazel) bypasses the firewall 2.
