---
title: Art in the temple of lies
---

﻿Art in the temple of lies.  
At the end of puffery and embellishments. 
There, where I found you, the truth. 
There amongst the weeds and briars, beauty.  
There, where we all grow.


Truth. Is art truth. 
Is that a question?
Damn you for asking.
Damn you for hearing 
Damn the whimper of thought. 


To what favor does the truth seek? 
Which rich man do we fellate? 
Burn of gold in our veins
Burn the fire coals hotter
Burn the gold fires hotter
