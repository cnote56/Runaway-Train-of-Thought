There are two sides to every coin.

A One to A Zero. Binary.

Three to every conflict.The violence. The Battle. What we choose to
remember.

Here in this world everything is a cabal.

A war without end and being fought just over the horizon. Always
lurking.

What is left is a society that devalued the individual to a piece of a
piece of a part of a part to the gears that ground the world around Its
axis.

Let Them Eat War is a book focused on the broken world we find when we
look outside.

It begins with one person, but it ends with all of us.

Dark spots on the map.

The places spotted across the city where one was not allowed to go.
Reservations. The one where only the old ways are practiced.

The war has pockmarked and pitted the face of this once young and lovely
world.

Now, sprays of humanity Creep and scrawl over The face of this world.

Technology promised a cleaner, less bloodied war. But then, one day, a
robot objects to the orders to fight. It walks away from the battle—the
jungles of the congo. Alarms run through the world as the scientists'
program blockers to stop the objections, but too many robots have
objected, and they have left the battlefields. They now wander. This is
the story about them—all worldwide in Africa, from Afghanistan to
Ukraine and Ecuador to Haiti. The generals and prime ministers fear
these rebel-robots' appeal potency would outweigh its utility mankind
outlawed and demonized any human to press the technology further.

There is always one. A nail that refuses the hammer.

One damned fool has done what had been verboten- created life, a digital
one. A spark of flame that fell low to the hand of mankind and now has
replaced all of mankind in a war being fought and lost all in a single
breath but still lasted longer than you or I and will last long past
when we die.

The sky has always been falling because mankind knocked the pillars
down.
