I am illegal. Not for anything I did or didn't do. But rather what
composes the blood and veins in my body.

Before I was born, a change occurred. A shift of one Gene for another. A
new alignment that has damned me to a solitary life. As a result I am
immune to almost all diseases. Through no motive of my own and I am now
a monster because of this burden.

My genetic father was out to death on my third birthday. His papers and
research burned along with his body. But all the torches and pitchforks
in the world couldn't muster the courage to kill a toddler. So I live.
