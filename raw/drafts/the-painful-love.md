Just forget me

Some days it isn’t worth breathing
Just taking a breath hurts
It hurts as if I were inhaling death itself
There isn’t a cure
There isn’t a disease
Love kills
Love sucks
There isn’t a reason for me to snort something
Or to smoke some other substance
I am dead
To the ones who hate me
To the ones that look at me
To the world
Even to the ones that understand
I don’t feel anything
I feel nothing
I feel nothing but pain
I feel nothing but pain and sorrow
Sorrow and pain
Alone and crowed
Loved and hated
Alive and dead
Here and far, far away
All of it and none, too
Just for get me
