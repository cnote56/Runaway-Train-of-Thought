# Dr. Vance Aldrich

## Overview
**Dr. Vance Aldrich** was a brilliant but utterly ruthless Cold War psychiatrist and lead research coordinator for [[mk-ultra-division]]. He is historically recognized as the "Grandfather of Cybernetic Conditioning," having bridged the gap between raw psychological brainwashing and systematic neurological programming.

## Biography
During the 1950s and 1960s, Dr. Aldrich operated out of the highly secure [[cia-safehouse-baltimore]]. Commissioned by the joint intelligence-military apparatus, Aldrich was obsessed with discovering a "permanent key" to human memory. He believed that the human mind was essentially a biological machine that could be programmed, wiped, and re-coded like an early mainframe computer.

Aldrich spent years conducting clinical trials using a rare, highly potent soporific compound coupled with high-frequency auditory prompts. He was the principal author of *The Brainwashing Manual*, a classified ledger that outlined the systematic erosion of conscious ego through sensory isolation.

## Technical Legacy
While Dr. Aldrich's physical body has long since decayed, his research lives on within the simulation's architectural framework. His notes on "sensory-feedback capture" served as the core blueprints when the first [[the-filter]] secure-links were developed. His conceptualization of the "Sleeper Hack" is the direct ancestor of the modern "Stasis-Lock" protocols used by [[the-fatherhood]].

## Relationships
- **[[general-robinson]]:** Robinson's predecessor in the Air Force intelligence branch was Aldrich's primary military liaison.
- **[[reggie-watts]]:** Reggie's modern struggle with his optic-implant "rattle" is a direct, lingering descendant of the sensory-overload side effects first documented in Aldrich's Baltimore lab reports.
