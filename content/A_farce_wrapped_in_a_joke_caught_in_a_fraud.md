title: A farce wrapped in a joke caught in a fraud
---

A farce wrapped in a joke caught in a fraud

I admit. I am not what I picture in my mind.

I am not what I am.

I am not who I am.

I am not anything,

Nothing of import

Nothing to offer.

Nothing.

A belch gone in a breeze is all I’ll ever be to this world.

It’s okay; I don’t mind being the filler between the seams.

I won’t haunt you. I won’t bother.

There’s nothing after this. Like there was nothing before.

Just a cruel joke of sudden existence and change forced upon the feels
and holes I feel inside.

There’s nothing there.

Never has been.

Always empty.

Lost as much as I knew where I was going.

Or at least I thought I did.

Past too far and just as many times beyond the horizon.

Too far to go back and say anything.

Other than what I said before.

Not even second place. Sometimes not even third.

Honorable mention.

Yeah. Thanks.

That paid so many bills.

Maybe I have to face it. I am not what I think I am.

I am not talented, not as much as I think I am.

I am not gifted. Never gifted anything in this life. I’ve had to scrape
and fight for it all.

It may be true that I am not the magnificent beast I see in my
reflection.

I’m but a failure and fraud of what I think I am

I could never write.

I can barely think.

I can’t even do what makes me feel good.

And even then, when I do... I am a farce wrapped in a joke caught in a
fraud.

Laughable. A burnout. A loser. A fool.

Walked away from a state job. All I had to do was swallow my pride and
sit there and be a warm body.

I couldn’t do that.

I couldn’t handle that. Not even that.

What a weak fucker I am.

I used to be strong.

I used to have confidence.

I used to know what, who, or even the lie that I was.

I used up all my nerves, fighting myself while the world roared around
my ears.

All the skills and things I’ve done.

All the strength in my arms and back

Gone.

I wish I knew what to do.

I wish I knew what I should think about and how to make something worth
the effort.

That's the trouble now.

I have to ration myself too much these days.

A short jog with the dog means the next day, my low back and hips are
racked.

Suppose I take the time to try and play something. I wear out before
I’ve started.

Every day I walk to the void and shout into it.

I hear nothing

Why have I felt so empty for so long?

Why have I felt alone?
