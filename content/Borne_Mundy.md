Borne Mundy

I've been dead since before I was born.

The day after the Lord's day; a Monday.

Or so I've been told.

Yeah 'tis true. I was born at one o'clock.

I’d like to think there was a thunder clapping

As I torn my momma's womb.

Them thunderheads barreling in from the west.

They coming for me, coming for you, me and all them too

But I'm lying. Or so I've been told.

Been a good liar, all my life, so been told.

I've been told I'm a good liar, even on Christmas day.

So I've been told.

The punches always land. Hard and fast.

Hard and on target. That’s how I get paid.

So I've been told.

Oh, and the fact that it doesn't hurt when I get hit. Or at least it
didn't.

I've taken punches before. But I remember when they started to hurt.

Henry “Chunk” McClain is a punching bag. A human punching bag that takes
everything you throw at him for the sack of cash waiting for him in his
locker after the fight. At this point in his career he was a human
tenderized steak warmed by the sun then slapped into the ring to dance
around for five rounds and then take the payday.

It was $25K in a brown paper bag. It's always in a brown paper bag. It's
a game of margins. Those margins don't include canvas or leather gym
bag. There's nothing romantic about a payoff. It's money on the dresser
after the deed is done.
