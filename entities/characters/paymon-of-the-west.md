# Paymon (King of the West)

## Overview
Paymon is the King of the West, ruling the western aerial region and overseeing the Order-8 Mercury Server. He is uniquely noted for being more obedient to the will of [[lucifer]] than any of the other three Kings of the Air, though he remains argumentative and difficult with the summoner unless constrained by divine power.^[[raw/drafts/mythos-and-magic-backup-repository.md]]

## Appearance & Traits
- **Ethereal Souldier:** When compelled by divine power, he takes the form of a soldier. He rides upon a dromedary (camel) crowned with a bright crown, and possesses the delicate countenance of a woman.^[[raw/drafts/mythos-and-magic-backup-repository.md]]
- **Royal Fanfare:** Preceded by a band of men playing trumpets and various other instruments with great volume. He speaks with a hoarse voice.^[[raw/drafts/mythos-and-magic-backup-repository.md]]
- **The Written-Paper Protocol:** Speaks naturally in his own native tongue, which is incomprehensible to the operator. The Master must cast a written paper to him, ordering him to "speak plainly and definitely," after which he will comply.^[[raw/drafts/mythos-and-magic-backup-repository.md]]

## Biography & History
- **The Western Controller:** Commands vast western legions and serves as a major node for secret communication, languages, and diplomatic relations. He has the power to make men invisible.^[[raw/drafts/mythos-and-magic-backup-repository.md]]

## Relationships
- **Lucifer:** Exceptionally loyal to Lucifer's administrative commands.^[[raw/drafts/mythos-and-magic-backup-repository.md]]
- **Satan:** Co-rules the aerial quarters under Satan's regional mapping.^[[raw/drafts/mythos-and-magic-backup-repository.md]]
- **Kings Under Him:** Directly commands **Belial** and **Baasan**, who assist in invisibility spells.^[[raw/drafts/mythos-and-magic-backup-repository.md]]
- **Messengers & Enforcers:** Guided by his messenger **Belferth** and enforcer **Rombalence** (or Rombalane), who operate from the 3rd to the 12th hour of summons.^[[raw/drafts/mythos-and-magic-backup-repository.md]]\n
