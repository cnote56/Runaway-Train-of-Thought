\*   \*\*Source Logic:\*\* The sources highlight that Dürer likely \*\*reversed or reflected\*\* Agrippa's square to create his own masterpiece, and that these sigils can be "freely rotated for aesthetic purposes" \[2, 5\].

#### \*\*3. Gnomon Shielding (2x2 Corner Buffer)\*\*
\*   \*\*Source Logic:\*\* Dürer’s square is \*\*"gnomon,"\*\* meaning each 2x2 corner within the larger grid sums to the magic constant of 34 \[3\].
\*   \*\*Gameplay Effect:\*\* The Artist can focus their repair on the corners of a firewall. If they balance any 2x2 corner to sum to 34, that section of the grid becomes \*\*"Eternally True"\*\* and immune to further demonic corruption for the rest of the trial \[3, 7\].

#### \*\*4. Date-Stamp Locking (The 1514 Signature)\*\*
\*   \*\*Source Logic:\*\* The numbers 15 and 14 in the bottom row of Dürer's square denote the date of the work \[3\].
\*   \*\*Gameplay Effect:\*\* The Artist can "sign" the firewall.
