I’ve just gotta get this out


There is nothing inside.
Nothing that hasn’t been there since the beginning
And it’s just about to start.

Popcorn, candy from the overpriced counter.
The drink filled with 90% ice and 10 % actual drink.
The show should begin at any time.
The room is dark and there is nobody here but us.
The popcorn is already half empty, and the hands digging deep in the cup
aren’t mine.

Dim, dimming flashes of yellowed light are trailing across the wide white
screen.

The music starts and the world is hushed for just a moment.

Oh I remember this one.
I hated the way he smelled, like urine.
He was always dirty.
That’s why we made fun of him.

Oh god not her I didn’t even remember how we stopped talking.
I think she just moved away.
Oh yeah she was asking me about something and I just didn’t want to listen
anymore.
I wonder why she never cared to talk to me again.

Ha! Ha! Ha!
This is the time I got home late and told dad I fell asleep.


I don’t think this is all that bad.
Never thought looking at ones sins in a dark room with cold popcorn could
be so much fun.

dowden
