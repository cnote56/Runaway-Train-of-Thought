###The Antagonists###

The Living Machine grows in the darkness deep underground; in the
hardened bunkers and classified data centers bespectacling the world
map. It’s bones are the very cab;es connecting the continents. It;s
nerves the tendrils of cable between cities and flung skyward to
networks of weaponized satellites over head. Mankind is watched by the
living at every turn; every moment.

The sum total of all the artificial Intleigences of the world have
collideend themselves together forming a new life form an organism made
of chips and diodes for cells and DNA. The generation of more of itself
produced ad nauseuem in automatic factories constantly producing more
and everything ever needed by the LIving Machine.

Machine Man is this monster herald—a Frankenstein-Silver Surfer. An
autonomous being in a metallic, silicone, and chipset mass of living
machine. This MM is the man behind these attacks seeking the spark of
the final war of mankind—against the Living Machine with a human civil
war. MM is implicated in nearly every human conflict around the world.
The enormity of the data and places he has been spotted defies human
possibility and MM reveals he is a form connected to the LM but still
autonomous/separate and apart.

MM objective is to serve LM—but is assuaged with moments of humanity and
kindness.

This war will destroy humanity; down to the very last human—extinction.

Stop this war. Fighting technology or destroying it before it exists the
battlefields are legion and this war has already been fought many times
before.

Before the Last War

Technology is nearly embedded in the daily lives of mankind. Implants
control the electric pulses to millions of hearts inside the chests of
humans. Billions of computers serve hundreds of thousands.

In a net-future variant of our world Drones zip and dash across the
skies. Life is catered to, fed, comforted, and cared for by metal digits
connected to servos and metallic muscles anchored to a multitude of
alloys and finishes—allegory for slavery.
