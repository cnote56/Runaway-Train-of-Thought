﻿Burton C. Chime never felt at home anywhere else but in the city. Didn't have to be any particular city, but a city. New York, Chicago, Washington, DC, Philadephia.
Burton never felt at home anywhere else but in the city. Didn't have to be any particular city, but a city. New York, Chicago, Washington, DC, Philadelphia. They all had that same energy, that same feeling of possibility. But New York was his favorite.
He loved the way it felt to wake up in the morning and hear the city already waking up around him. The sound of the subway rumbling, the smell of coffee brewing, the people hurrying to work. It was all so alive.
He loved the way the city always seemed to be changing. There was always something new to see, something new to do. He loved how the people were from all over the world, with all different backgrounds and stories.
But most of all, he loved how the city made him feel. He felt like he could be anything he wanted in New York. He felt like he could achieve anything he set his mind to.
The coolness of country dawn was pleasant, but it didn't have the same energy as New York in the morning. In New York, you could feel the excitement in the air. You could feel the potential. You could feel the future. Anywhere there were buildings and people and movement and energy. it fed his soul, he said as he left his family's farm in Texas. 
He wanted nothing more than to look back at that lonely place. But soon, the grim reaper called him back to his hometown. To bury his father. 
But there was no other morning like this one, Burton C. Chime stood in the center of an impact crater. Barefoot. Skin smoldering. And glowing from his core. 
In his mind, RAGE. 
Suddenly he was himself again—a man. Naked, in an impact crater, staring up at the pale light of the morning aurora. 
“I have to stop drinking.” 
His skin cooled, and his head struck itself with a hangover sending Burton collapsing to the charred earth under his feet.
