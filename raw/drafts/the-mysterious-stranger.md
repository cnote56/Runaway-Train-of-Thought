The Mysterious Stranger

It was a cold, wet night in the Capital City. The rising pedwalls had locked down sector 4, leaving the streets empty save for the silent hover of bloxes patrol drones.

Inside the rusted steel doorway of the subterranean bar, Vance shook the rain from his heavy leather trench coat. His eyes, sharp and hyper-focused from a bootleg cybernetic iris, scanned the dimly lit room. He wasn't here to drink. He was here to find Willie.

Vance was desperate. His younger sister, Clara, was burning up with nerve fever in a basement hideout, and the only thing that could soothe the rattle in her spine was a ration of white peanut butter. He had three silver tokens ready, all he had left, to trade for that sweet-smelling grease.
