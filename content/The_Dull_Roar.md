﻿The Dull Roar


A sound based novel about self-destruction. Welcome to the end of a glorious decay. 


A man is tortured with survivors guilt and the wounds which constantly haunt him as he pioneers a field of science and technology. 


###


Sweat beaded on his neck. Breath shot and receded from his nostrils and mouth. 


Operator's were discouraged from sweating. 


Only a pencil-necked bureaucrat would have ever written down that rule. A suit never stepped foot in a sandbox. They never once soiled their wingtips with the dirt and scum required in planting a device on a targets possessions. Section D was not as glamorous as advertised, but it paid the bills. Enough to take care of an apartment this operative never got to sleep a whole week in, and a nice split-level house in Olney, Maryland that since the divorce this operative never got to see anymore. 


It was not a life you would ever see in a brochure in the local Msribe recruiters office. But this life was his. And he was named, Operative 24. Mo Christia, Muslim, or Jewish name. A number. Cold and calculated. 


But he was not as cold as he used to be. He was sweating and frustrated with life. 


The sticky glue adhered to the wooden side table. The red light flashed to green then blinked off after ten seconds. It was armed. The mission was complete. Operative 24/was to extract. Then he heard giggling on the opposite side of the hotel door. 


From what little Russian Operative 24 understood he comprehended the double-entandra of the word key and intended word dick. 


This hotel room only had one other room in it. A bathroom. The whore would likely want to freshen up and use it only to find him huddled in the bathroom. What use would be the bug he just planted next to the bed then? So Operative 24/made the final independent decision he had of his career. And it needed poorly. 


As the blood soaked into the padding if the carpet, Operative 24 sat in the foot and of the bed and gripped his K-Bar knife. This was not going to end well. 


The suit entered the scene and began barking and orders and shouting at Operative 24 about what a "fuck up" he was. 


The evening ended with Operative 24's K-bar knife buried in the thin drywall of the cheap motel wall and the soiled suit pants of the suit glistening in the headlights of the cleanup van.
