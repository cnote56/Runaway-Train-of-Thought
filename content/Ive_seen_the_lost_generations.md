---
title: Ive seen the lost generations
---

I’ve seen the lost generations.

Ghosts of the potential we’ve traded for these heavier burdens.

I’ve seen the regret in barren eyes.

Quiet and cold tenements.

I’ve seen older generations, hold accountable the younger.

For lost opportunities never afforded to the now living.

Fuck the road. It has crumbled underfoot.

Fuck the highway. It demands a toll.

Fuck the factory. It has rusted shut.

Fuck the school. It has demanded too high a price.

Fuck the things you lost. You never had them.

Fuck the hell you find today. It was always there.

Fuck the relief you succle. It just masks the pain.

Fuck the future you look to. It lies and beckons you over the edge.

Fuck the paycheck. It is just paper you’ve traded your blood for.

Fuck the roof over your head. It is just a box larger than the one that
waits for your corpse.

Fuck the food you eat. It is never enough or really what you want.

Fuck the dream you dreamt. It lies.
