# Clemens

Clemens is a high-level civilian intelligence director or national security coordinator who oversees active surveillance and tactical operations.

## Biography
Clemens is a highly frantic, fast-paced administrator who wears a grey suit. He is constantly in motion, tapping his feet, pacing, and checking operation debriefs directly inside his secure-link. He works closely with **[[general-robinson]]** to monitor tactical intercepts and rogue targets. 

Clemens represents the bureaucratic and impatient face of the surveillance state. He is heavily reliant on the secure-link overlays but lacks the technical understanding of code and server protocols, often demanding summaries and drone footage in plain language.

## Characteristics
- **Appearance:** Grey suit, sharp, frantic demeanor.
- **Implant:** Fitted with a high-level secure-link.
- **Habits:** Constant foot tapping and arm folding.
