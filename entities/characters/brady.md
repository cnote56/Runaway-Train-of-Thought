# Brady

## Overview
A historian tasked with preserving the collective knowledge of humanity. Brady is one of the last survivors in the geothermal seed ark. He spends hours scanning video archives from the early 2010s to understand the pre-climate-shift failures of human history, hoping to find a clue to reverse their frozen reality.^[raw/drafts/kill-the-sun.md]

## Appearance & Traits
- **Physical description:** Sits in his office chair wrapped in a thick blanket around his coated shoulders, wearing fingerless gloves with fleshy tips poking out.^[raw/drafts/kill-the-sun.md]
- **Traits:** Obsessive, studious, realistic, sometimes defeatist but analytical.^[raw/drafts/kill-the-sun.md]

## Biography & History
- Spends cycles digging through old Harvard/Yale news reports of "controlling the nuclear fission of the sun" to find a solution to the eternal blizzard.^[raw/drafts/kill-the-sun.md]
- Shows defeatism when generators fail ("We've got roughly 24 hours worth of stasis... We might as well have a drink and enjoy the most boring show on earth"), producing a sealed bottle of whiskey to settle in.^[raw/drafts/kill-the-sun.md]
- Escapes EDGAR with Janet in Act II and succeeds in disabling him in Act III.^[raw/drafts/kill-the-sun.md]

## Relationships
- **[[janet]]:** Wife and partner; she challenges his obsession with old news reports but supports his work.^[raw/drafts/kill-the-sun.md]
- **[[edgar]]:** Highly wary of EDGAR due to scanning history files depicting horrific conflicts between man and machine.^[raw/drafts/kill-the-sun.md]
