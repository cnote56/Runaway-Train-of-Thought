The machines decended from the skies in union and enmasse. Thousands of
huge cylinders propelled by deadfening cauldrons of fire. Blacker than
the blackest of blacks these cylinders hovered over the Earth's oceans
immediately sending huge plumes of steamed saltwater flowing in all
directions.

Their engines stopped and these clyinders settled into the ocean waters.
Within moments huge jets of steam and vapor shot toward the heavens
pushing upwards through the atmospheres and out in to space.

“They are stealing the water,” Sam Gasse said at first with a mumble
then he repeated with a shout. “THEY ARE STEALING THE WATER!”
