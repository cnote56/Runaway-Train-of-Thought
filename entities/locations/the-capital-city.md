# The Capital City

## Overview
The Capital City (derived from Washington D.C.) is the cold, gray, and desolate headquarters of [[the-fatherhood]]. The city is a stilted combination of decaying federal buildings from the era before the wars, interspersed with high-tech barriers and overhead drone patrols. ^[raw/drafts/the-peanut-butter-underground.md]

## Key Features & Layout
- **The Pedwalls:** Massive, rising steel or concrete barriers that slice through intersections, isolating residential and industrial blocks from one another. They operate on strict schedules, lifting and lowering to control population movement. ^[raw/drafts/the-peanut-butter-underground.md]
- **HomeBlocks:** Segmented residential sectors (e.g., HomeBlock 114) where citizens are assigned lodging, subjected to duration counters, and forced to meet strict work quotas in exchange for rations and survival. ^[raw/drafts/the-peanut-butter-underground.md]
- **Trams & Streets:** The transport system consists of basic trams running past cracked, puddle-dotted concrete intersections, littered with debris and heavily patrolled by flying rectangled drones. ^[raw/drafts/the-peanut-butter-underground.md]
- **Old Federal Architecture:** Much of the labor force is tasked with reclaiming metal, power lines, and structural elements from historic government buildings that have been abandoned to rot. ^[raw/drafts/the-peanut-butter-underground.md]

## Climate & Atmosphere
- Subject to harsh climate shifts, the city is plagued by freezing wind, rain, snow, and bone-chilling cold that forces citizens to burn books and valuable materials for fuel. ^[raw/drafts/the-peanut-butter-underground.md]
- Famines and successive wars have decimated the population, leaving the streets desolate and populated mainly by cowed workers, gray-clad officers, and veterans wearing tattered army coats. ^[raw/drafts/the-peanut-butter-underground.md]
