From all sight


I must drug my eyes.
To safeguard my sheep.
From the dreams of past lives.
I must do this to protect.
To protect my mind from my soul.
For in no other state is mind and soul;.
So complete than in sleep.
To complete the wall for one more night.
I must drug these eyes.
From all sight.
