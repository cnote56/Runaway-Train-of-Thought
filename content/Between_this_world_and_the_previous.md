---
title: Between this world and the previous
---

I stand on the event horizon between this world and the previous.
Staring into the abyss from which I climbed and clawed. It is not for
me, this world and I long for my previous self. He was brave. He was
genuine. He was dead long before he knew he was.

I am now but a shadow on the wall of his domain. And his world turns
without him. Won't they all? Turn on when we are dust.
