# General Robinson

General Robinson is a high-ranking black Air Force general dressed in dress blues, heavily involved in the execution, legal justification, and defense of the **[[the-filter]]** secure-link program.

## Biography
A decorated military commander with a chest full of medals and colored ribbons, General Robinson manages the deployment and tactical applications of the cybernetic secure-links. He acts as the military liaison to civilian intelligence and congressional oversight. 

During closed-door briefings with congressional committees, Robinson frequently clashes with **[[chuck-ammerstand]]**, a senator demanding civilian and independent oversight of the FILTER program. Robinson steadfastly defends the program as legally compliant and essential for national defense.

## Characteristics
- **Appearance:** Dignified, black-skinned, clad in Air Force dress blues with service medals.
- **Affiliation:** Joint intelligence-military apparatus.
- **Rivals:** [[chuck-ammerstand]] (congressional watchdog).
