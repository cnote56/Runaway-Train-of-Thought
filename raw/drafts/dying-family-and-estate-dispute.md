I’m dying, that is all that needs to be said. That is all I wanted said.
Yet my loving family wanted to exploit my passing. My body will not even
grow cold and they all ready plan the separation of my estate. They are
bloodthirsty wolves – craving my death to better their own lives. They will
try to receive more than they ever gave. My doctor’s lie they say I will
heal. Not so.
