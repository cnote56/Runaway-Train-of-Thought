# The Harp

A machine gifted to humanity by the "gods" in the sky. It serves as an anchor for [[levators]] and a frequency engine to maintain the planetary balance.

## Mechanics
- **Function:** Projects frequency intonations to maintain planetary rotation and stability.
- **Components:** Rotating circles holding cups filled with oils and waters of varying density, heat, and measure. 
- **Operation:** Requires precise manual balancing by a [[harp-keeper]]. Shifts in density cause circles to rotate and gears to dance.
- **Failure:** Agitation leads to frequency instability, causing planetary-level damage or decoupling from the "gods'" palaces.

## Provenance
- Described in [[13-8-billion-years]] and the journals of [[dobly]].
