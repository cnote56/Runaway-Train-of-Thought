Speaking in salt the curses swirl like quicksand at our feet.

Before the cataclysm and after the fall.

Before the walls fall and the seas crash

Something else more murderous than before.

The catch between the set and the spring.

All the mad bastards everywhere here and there between life and death

The world turned a harsh gunmetal grey very fast.
