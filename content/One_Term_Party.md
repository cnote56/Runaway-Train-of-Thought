The Assassination

Caught in the glow of a spotlight on a riser braced stage amongst a
crowd massed inside a rundown stadium long ignored. There were 30,000
people - children, families, whole communities - cheering. Not for the
glory of sports or the flash of spectacle. It was for one woman standing
in a spotlight.

ALEX MORGAN projected against a fluttering backdrop.

The booming chants of supporters washed over the name of the person
caught in the spotlight.

Alex Morgan stood on a starkly empty stage.

A woman.

An American.

A veteran.

A writer.

A person praying for a brighter future than what was forced at birth.

Alex is almost finished with a speech that should be ignored, but it is
not. It is being broadcast on nearly every major network and streaming
service across the world. In real time.

Alex never thought of herself as a public speaker, but she spoke. She
shuddered a few times here and there; as speaking truth to power often
does.

The words fell across the world and while half derided her manish chin
more than the content of her words; Alex spoke and nearly half the world
heard her.

"This is our moment! Together, we can rebuild what’s broken and build a
future that works for everyone!"

The cheering covered the crack of gunpowder and projectile.

A rifle shot thundered the arena. A people accustomed to weapons knew
the sound of a gun. A shocked silence covered the vastness of the space.
There was a moment as Alex had tried to speak. Then she could not
inhale. She clutched her chest to catch her breath and then felt the
rush of blood down from the hole torn through her throat.

Alex’s eyes dimmed and lost focus. Her eyes faded to black before her
head struck the stage landing. She now lay in her blood, dying.

Aides and security rushed the stage in vain. A woman reached Alex’s body
first, sliding herself into the growing pool of Alex’s blood and
desperately trying to cradle the body and catch the spurts of blood.

"No! Alex, NO! NO! NO!"

A gurgle slipped from Alex’s throat and ushered her to her next
incarnation.

The world watched Alex Morgan die.

Plot Ideas and Themes

Reckoning Against Inequality: 

The protagonist, a charismatic third-party presidential candidate,
proposes radical reforms to address student loans and UBI while facing
corporate sabotage. The compelling platform resonates with young voters
who seek change, dramatizing the struggle between grassroots democracy
and established corporate powers.

Technological Hope: 

The candidate unveils revolutionary blockchain technology for tracking
tax contributions, ensuring transparency in wealth distribution. This
could attract public support but also anger wealthy elites who want to
stop him. The technology also acts as a target for cyber attacks that
threaten to undermine the campaign.

In-Media Res with an Assassination: 

The story opens with the chaotic aftermath of the candidate's
assassination at a rally, interspersed with flashbacks revealing
campaign events, key endorsements, and public reactions. This creates a
gripping narrative that draws the reader into the urgency of the
election season.

The Shadowy Assassin: 

The antagonist is revealed to be a disillusioned former tech billionaire
enlisted by hidden corporate interests to eliminate the candidate. As
the story unfolds, his training, motivations, and moral struggles come
into play, exploring the fine line between idyllic reform and corporate
greed.

Dissolution of Party: 

The candidate's promise to dissolve the party if elected serves as a
double-edged sword. It attracts those tired of the two-party system
while alarming traditional political allies, leading to unexpected
alliances and betrayals that escalate during the campaign.

Empowered Youth Movement: 

The campaign sparks a youth movement, with college campuses becoming
hubs of activism. This subplot focuses on young supporters, highlighting
their grassroots efforts, raising funds, mobilizing voters, and
showcasing their struggles against entrenched societal norms.
