Tax Advice For Bad People

 

Trouble wasn't a blood clot floating in my mind’s eye before this all
happened. I had a career -- gone now, blasted to ash by a nuclear bomb
called “professional malpractice.”

I was in a land farthest from anything that could remind me of the life
I used to enjoy. I was now in the sandy desert somewhere between Vegas
and the rest of the outer darkness known on a map as “Nevada.” Don’t ask
how to pronounce it.

A car lay nearby, rolled upside down and burning; its fire warmed my
backside as I crouched down and scrubbed my hands with the sand of the
road to clean off some of the blood. There was no water to be found. The
car was engulfed in flames, slowly becoming a charred hulk of its former
self.

Desert, dark, night. Somewhere in the blackness was a road I had been
run off from into the brush and sand. Now, the fire is behind me.
Somewhere out there between the darkness and me, there’s a car idling
and at least two men trying to kill me.

The sudden feeling of a bullet buzzing my ear threw me back on my ass
and to the sand. My heart raced as my mind screamed, Get away from the
flames!

The car's trunk lay gapped open by the blast of the gas tanks. A lug
wrench glinted in the light. Long with a curved portion with the socket
end. I flattened myself to sand and crotch and crawled toward the only
thing worth calling a weapon.

Another gunshot rang out; near-instant, the bullet spat sand near my
head and darted into the night.

“Don’t move, buddy.” A disembodied voice from the darkness yelled. “The
next one will be in your knee. He wants you alive.”

“Then I’ve got the advantage,” I said. “Because I don’t care if I have
to kill you and all your friends back in Baltimore.”

I lay pronated on the ground—how was I going to run away? Panic took
hold of my fight-flight response, and with strange dexterity, I
push-up/jumped myself to a stumbling run and then blindly dashed away
from the burning car into the darkness.

“How the…” One voice studdered.

The other didn’t care to comment, and gunshots rang out, sending sand
and sagebrush flying in the wreckage’s firelight.

Somehow, my feet found flat ground and then a dead tree's twisted root.
I flew tumbling to the ground and struggled to keep myself from
screaming and grunting my position away from my pursuers.

I found stillness again and waited to hear if I was fucked or still had
some time yet to get fucked.

Gunshots. Not at me.

Two voices yelling at each other in disbelief that “the fucking guy just
fucking ran the fuck away!”.

Still grounded, my leg found the dropoff to a ditch - I slid, retreating
into it. Finding a stone to hold in each hand in the dark, just
something to knock, whichever I saw first in the head.

My left hand felt a smooth, oblong stone come into its grip. Then, my
right hand found a round, chunky one with just enough curve to be held.
At the other end was a jagged paw, perfect for blunt trauma.

Shouting and curses. One voice from closer than the other. I shielded my
eyes with my hand and blinked quickly to adjust to the darkness better.

I sensed a shape near me.

Breathing.

Both he and I.

I could almost smell him.

Then, the moonlight glinted off the barrel, and I lunged at him. I
missed him but caught him with my left hand, and I felt it hit something
soft and metal.

The gun fired in the darkness.

He grunted and cursed, “My hand! You broke my hand!”

I swung toward the voice.

The rock in my hand felt something connect and then crunch.

My right hand felt the report, and then I heard life release from lungs
that didn’t need it anymore. I exhaled as a body slumped to the ground.

Moonlight glinted off the barrel. I grabbed it, dropping both my rocks
and feeling the barrel slip and become a little sticky. It was the man’s
blood from the rock in my right hand.

It felt like a revolver. I had no chance to check the chambers, so I
crouched and felt the grip in my hand and the stickiness all over.

The other guy heard. No doubt. Run.

I ran.

And collided face-first into the chest of the other guy. Both of us were
stunned to find the only other moron running into and out of the
darkness was also directly in front of the other one.

The hit shocked us both as we clamored for grip and leverage on the
other.

My left hand felt a shirt, a collar of something, and pulled hard as his
mass tumbled onto me. I knee’d his gut, and we tumbled.

Like a bear’s paw, one of his hands clutched my neck. I wouldn’t hold
out from him choking me. Then, the gun surfaced between us and into my
hand. The barrel blast whistled the fight to a close.

My shirt was on fire! Did I shoot myself?!

I slapped the flames off my chest as the ringing in my ears rattled my
brain, shooting jolts of white-hot pain through my brain. I stumbled and
rolled; dust kicked up around me.

Quickly, I clamored to my feet for the next attack, but there wasn’t
one.

Coughing blood, he gasped and spat a bloody mass from his bleeding
mouth.

I saw his form more clearly in the low flames from the wreckage now.

“Just take it easy,” I said. “I’m sorry.”

I recognized his face. I had seen him before, on one of my trips to DC
for signatures when I was someone. I remember having him sign his tax
returns; he laughed as he was getting a “tax return” for all the pimping
and punching. I remembered who I was before I dragged myself to hell.

“Fuck off,” he grimaced and gasped for air - bubbles ginned from a
pinkish and bloodied chest wound.

I saw his face more clearly then. He was one of the Capos. One of the
ones I saw regularly at the social club—the inner circle of the gaggle
of bastards that made my life hell.

His arm stretched for a gun near his right hand. But I kicked it away.

He gargled death.

I sat and watched a monster die.

This was the second person I had watched die.

The first that I directly caused, but for my damned stupid ideas about
justice and freedom.

It was quiet now. His eyes cast off in a forgotten gaze; he was gone.

I watched the dimming firelight cast shadows on the nearby sand for some
time afterward. I remained staring at my memories amid the firelight and
the bodies of two former clients.

Plot Threads

The Escalation of Violence 

After witnessing a brutal beating of a prostitute by a mob capo, the
disbarred tax lawyer, Alex, becomes increasingly enraged. This event
catalyzes Alex, sparking thoughts of revenge against the cabal that has
entangled him in a life of crime. As he attempts to gather evidence and
find allies among other victims, he faces the harsh realities of mob
power and the risks of going against them.

Duality

Alex leads a double life, working as a financial advisor for the mob
while secretly assisting law enforcement as an informant. The tension
between the two identities creates a web of lies that leads to betrayal
and moral dilemmas. The beatdown of the prostitute intensifies his
internal conflict as he struggles between self-preservation and justice.

Corruption and Fallout 

The story could explore the deep-rooted corruption within the legal
system itself, with the protagonist discovering that the justice he once
upheld may be just as flawed as the criminals he now serves. This
realization drives Alex to seek not only revenge but also a way to
expose the hypocrisy surrounding him, leading to a high-stakes maneuver
against both the mob and corrupt officials.

Unlikely Alliances 

As Alex searches for redemption, he forms a bond with the very victim of
the crime that ignited his rage. Their shared goal of justice leads to a
deeper exploration of their pasts, highlighting the impact of trauma and
the resilience of the human spirit. Together, they plot to take down the
mobster by utilizing his own tactics against him, creating an unexpected
twist.

The Price of Revenge 

While seeking retribution against the mob, Alex discovers he is not the
only one they’ve harmed. He encounters other victims trying to navigate
their own tapestry of despair and seeks to rally them in a stand against
their oppressors. However, as he gets deeper in, the line between being
the avenger and becoming a monster blurs.

embrace this new monstrous identity or destroy what he has created,

pondering human nature's complexities.

Character Profiles

**1. Alex Morgan (Protagonist)**

- **Age**: 38

- **Appearance**: Tall and lean with dark hair that is slowly going
gray. He dresses in ill-fitting suits due to his financial troubles,
reflecting his past life.

- **Background**: Once a respected tax lawyer with a golden reputation,
Alex's downfall came after he took a calculated risk that spiraled out
of control, leading to his disbarment.

- **Personality**: Intelligent and resourceful but plagued with guilt
and shame. He is driven by a deep-seated desire for redemption, making
him impulsive in the face of injustice.

- **Motivation**: His witness of the mob's cruelty pushes him towards
revenge against those who have wronged him and the innocent.

- **Fear**: Becoming the monster he is hunting; losing his humanity in
pursuit of revenge.

**2. Tony "The Knife" Romano (Antagonist)**

- **Age**: 45

- **Appearance**: Stocky with a shaved head, sharp features, and a
collection of tattoos that narrate his violent history. Always seen in
lavish clothing.

- **Background**: A feared capo within the Baltimore mob, known for his
ruthless tactics and enjoyment of power. Grew up in a criminal family,
normalized violence from a young age.

- **Personality**: Charismatic yet sadistic. Finds pleasure in asserting
dominance over others and thrives on fear and loyalty.

- **Motivation**: Maintaining control over his territory and wealth.
Willing to destroy anyone who opposes him, including Alex.

- **Fear**: Losing power and respect within the mob; being exposed for
his crimes.

**3. Mia (Ally/Resistance)**

- **Age**: 28

- **Appearance**: With long, curly brown hair and bright blue eyes, Mia
has a fierce disposition and tends to wear street style that signifies
her rebellious nature.

- **Background**: A former prostitute who escaped the yoke of the mob
after suffering from their abuses. She harbors deep resentment towards
men who misuse women.

- **Personality**: Strong-willed and resilient, she is not afraid to
speak her mind. She embodies a fierce sense of justice and loyalty.

- **Motivation**: Seeking to expose the mob's brutality and protect
other vulnerable women from similar fates.

- **Fear**: Falling back into the grasp of the mob; being unable to stop
more victims from suffering.

Story Synopsis

The wealth inequality is palpable in the opulent yet deceptive
atmosphere of Dupont Circle, Washington D.C.. Luxurious buildings stand
as proud guards to the secrets they harbor, while shadows of decay loom
in their wake, a compelling contrast to the affluence surrounding them.
Here walks Alex Morgan, a disbarred tax lawyer haunted by his past
mistakes. Once esteemed, he now thrives on the fringes, blackmailed into
submission by those he cannot confront.

As he navigates his dismal reality, everything plunges into chaos when
he witnesses a mob capo, Tony Romano, unleash a merciless beating on an
unsuspecting prostitute—an act of brutality so cold and wanton it
ignites something deep within him. Overwhelmed with rage, Alex
recognizes this as a turning point, compelling him to seek a path of
vengeance against the very forces that imprison him. Torn between
life-altering anger and moral grounding, his resolve hardens.

In the shadows of Baltimore, Alex meets Mia—a resilient woman with a
past of her own, one marred by the mob's cruelty. Together, they forge
an unlikely alliance, drawn not only by pain but also by a shared
purpose: to unearth and dismantle the mob's stronghold. Their midnight
conversations spark plans that oscillate between preposterous and
undeniable. Amid their plight, Alex faces the encroaching danger of
becoming the very monster he despises.

Tension rises as the duo maneuvers through the web of crime, deception,
and heartbreaking loyalty—an intricate dance where each wrong move could
seal their fates. The climax builds as Alex finally decides to confront
Tony and the monstrous world he embodies. In the dusty confines of an
abandoned warehouse, with tension thick enough to cut, Alex realizes
that this showdown will define whether he emerges as a hero or succumbs
to the thirst for revenge.

With fists raised anger pulsing with each heartbeat, Alex charges
against Tony's crew. The fight is fierce, a violent testament to what
desperation drives a man to become. As the dust settles, Alex stands,
suspended between victory and an existential crisis. Did he find
justice, or did he unearth something monstrous within himself?

In the end, the conflict resolves, not with clear conclusions but with
haunting ambiguity. Alex learns that true redemption intertwines with
acceptance of loss and confronting his actions, shaping the identity he
has yet to fully grasp. Shadows of the past may follow him, but the path
to reclaiming his life lies ahead.

Through his journey, Alex discovers that the monsters we face can often
lead us to rediscover the humanity we thought lost—yet the struggle
remains ensconced in the grayness of morality.

I ignored it. Not my job.

“You fucking cunt!” A man’s voice roared through the walls after I

Baltimore, MD. One Year And One Day Ago.

The numbers he left scratched in columns and rows on a yellow legal pad

No extensions allowed.

“That’s how they get you in their audit trap.” My tax law professor, a
