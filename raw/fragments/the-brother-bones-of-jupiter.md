The Brother Bones of Jupiter

Cole.dowden@gmail.com; 817-343-3718

Skulls and bones floated in the vacuum of space with the gentle tug of gravity pulling them into rings around the massive cloud-gas planet.

The nebula’s purple hue colored everything in this system a lie. Nothing was trustworthy here. All the gravity in the world could not keep you from spinning yourself out into the untethered space of this broken system. This was the nature of this realm. We were not in the normal sectors of space. Everything here was familiar as a lover’s touch, but twice as deadly.

It is here where they find our screaming bones floating amongst its brothers. Not your brothers, but the Brother Bones of Jupiter.

This wasteland has not felt the touch of life for over a hundred thousand cycles. But the bones float and spin around the dead planet Jupiter.

This giant once demanded all the wonder found in the hearts of men as their yes trespassed the dark night sky. The call of Jupiter sowed seeds of desire in the hearts of men. Spurred by their desire to know more of the thing they strained their eyes toward, they developed thought and think. These terrible twins have brought to us all of the pleasures and pains we find in our realms today.

But here, in the shadow of Jupiter, this is where the universes began to die.

It is a pity, true. This young system held so much promise, so much resources to be mined and capitalized on. Let’s trace the dead back to their deathbeds. There we may uncover the reasons for this broken sandbox of one star, nine planets, one-hundred and eighty-one moons, and thousands of satellites. Strange they cluster around the cracked and smoldering planet just three steps from the dying star trapping all of these souls to their doom.

This poor shell of its former self. We see the cracked surface of the planet cutting to the very center of this planet. Once teeming with life and energy. It is no longer. We find its hot core cracked like an egg and its yolk flung out to form a half ring around this planet.

On these fragmented rings we find crystal and ore. All the elements that caused the destruction of this planet lay open, naked, exposed, like the curves of pornographic centerfold. The soft glow of the nebula-sun cause sparks and reflections of light from these rings. This is where we find the last few hardened survivors all scraping at the riches under their feet.

Moving away from our space graveyard we travel closer to this cracked-egg planet.

Along the cast of the ore rings the small clusters of structures pepper and draw order from the rough ridges of the ore rings. Like long circuit boards we seen the tracked pathways running with small boxes from structure to structure. Some of the structures are boxes others round terminals and then we see the domes. Large Geometric domes lined with highly reflective foil shells keep our regular sight form peeking inside when the blaze of the nebula-star find the full force of its radiation on these  domes. And in the cold dark shade these foiled shells turn transparent and let us see inside where these survivors survive.

Under the foil-skin of the habitats we see green terrariums lined with tall trees, viney jungled morasses, and then the clean lines of organized and cultivated crops. Some green and yellow other golden strands stretching skyward to feed on the remnants of radiation from the nebula-star.

Small floating drones pepper the fields as they hover over the crops tending to them. Small creatures dart and meander around the fields and in and out of the tunnels feeding the cast of characters in these geometric structures.

Closer still. Let us see more detail on these creatures. They are similar to each other but different still. Some skin and hair opposite of the others, but all of them appear to be of the same genus. Nothing alien - to the inhabitants at least - in these geometric structures. They pick and scrape at the soil under their feet and at the roots of the crops. They appear to be tending these plants and not destroying them.
