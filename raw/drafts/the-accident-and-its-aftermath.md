Scene: an apartment of a couple

Tony
I’ll take care of the laundry later, after I get home from work.

Emily
Sure just like always, hon.

Tony
Honey, don’t worry  about dinner either. I’ll be taking care of that as
well. I love you, bye.
Exit tony

Emily
Love you too...
Emily starts to tidy things up in the apartment and grabs her backpack to
go to school.

Lights down
Lights shift across the apartment setting to simulate the sun going across
the sky.
Sounds of a car accident and police sirens.

The telephone in the apartment begins to ring.
It rings three times and the answering machine picks up.

Voice on answering machine
Yes, hello this message is for Tony Williams.  I’m sorry to inform you but
your wife has been in an accident and I’m afraid it’s quite serious.  We
currently have her under care at St. Peters Hospital. Please call 555-4360
and speak to the nurse on duty and we’ll try to tell you as much as
possible.

Tony enters with a boquet of flowers. Lights remain dim.
Honey, I’m home...Emmy?  Anybody home?

Tony searches for the light switch and lights come up when he finds it.

Telephone rings.

Tony
Hello? Yeah it’s me mom.  Do you know where Emily is?  Where? What message?
 How? Oh my god...no...  oh god no!  I’ll be right there!
Tony slams the phone down and flies out the door.


Lights dim on  the apartment.  Lights up on a hospital bed opposite side of
stage.
Emily is lying in the bed, in a coma.  Tony’s mother is pacing back and
forth up stage.

Tony enters.

Tony
Oh God...Emmy?  Emmy?!?!

Patrica
She’s in a coma...It’s no use son.  She can’t hear you.

Tony
How did it happen?  What happened?

Patrica
Evidently as she was going to her morning classes at the college, she was
walking like she always does and was hit by a car running a red light.
Son, I’m sorry, but the doctor doesn’t think she’s going to wake up.

Tony remains silent and in shock.

Doctor enters.

Doctor
You must be Tony.  I’m Dr. Tolar. Son, it’s really hard to say now but one
things for sure,  no matter if she wakes up or not there’s too much damage
done  to the brain to excpect a full recovery.

Tony
Could I be alone with Emily, please?

Patrica
Son, are you...

Tony interupting
Just for a minute. Please, Mom. Just a minute.

Patrica and the doctor exit stage left.

Tony
Well Hon...You look tired.  Like you always said, “Bad things happen to
good people.”
They say you’re not gonna wake up, and come back to me. Well, (tears begin
to form in Tony’s eyes)  I say you’re too much of a fighter to let them be
right.  I say you’re still too strong to just give up. But, I don’t know
what to think anymore ...it seems as though everything’s turned upside down
now.  I was gonna surprise you with dinner at your favorite resturaunt.
Dancing at the Wilson room inside the Hilton Hotel... I had even gotten us
the honeymoon suite.   Happy anniversary, baby...

Tony burst into tears and his mother Patrica enters.

Patrica
Tony?  Honey... I...

Tony
Oh mom... what can you do, really? what could you say?

EXIT TONY CRYING UNCONTROLLABLY

Lights down on hospital bed.

Tony appears opposite side on stage in a chair with his hands covering his
face...weeping.
Why God? Why?  Why did you have to do this to my Emily?  Where’s the just
cause in striking down such a beautiful creature...She’s all I have and you
just wiped her out!!!

Janitor stepping from off stage
Can’t tell you how many times I’ve heard dat.  From both men and women.

Tony
What ?  Do you mind!?

Janitor
What you just said...all that about why God has picked your sweetheart
instead of some other shmucks’.  Now the way I see it is you can either go
on in there and take a good long look at what you have and either do one of
two tings.  You can go in there and pull up a blanket and wait the storm
out or cut the cords where they lay and try an walk away with your head
held high.  I’ll tell you I’ve seen both of them done. ‘Cept for the last
one.  I never seen one walk away with their head up.  Dey walked away, but
never with they head held up.  Nope never with they head held up.

But hey, I’m just a dumb old janitor what do I know ‘bout the innerards of
a man’s soul. That be up to da man himself ta find out.


EXIT THE JANITOR

Tony
Dumb Ol’ bastard. What does he know about my life and my needs and my
problems.  I don’t see his wife laid up in a hospital bed breathing off
some godamn machine.
Ignorant fool.

EXIT TONY STAGE LEFT





LIGHTS UP ON HOSPITAL BED
Patrica is sitting by the bed holding Emily’s hand and running her fingers
through her hair.

ENTER TONY

Tony
How is she?

Patrica
Son, I’m sorry.  (She gets up to hug him. And they embrace.)

Tony
Was it this hard when dad died? I mean, I was so young. I don’t remember
much of it.
