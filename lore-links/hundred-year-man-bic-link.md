# Lore Link: The One-Hundred-Year Man and the BIC

- **The Boy in the Alley (1916):** Confirmed as the nascent BIC progenitor. His observation of the "Flash" (Henry's departure from the alley) is the primary "Initial Render Error" mentioned in the *Allegiance* archives.
- **The BIC Connection:** The child, seeing Henry's "skin-suit" and barcode, subconsciously encoded that aesthetic into the early BIC operational protocols. The "Kavendolt" word Henry accidentally slipped becomes a forbidden frequency in BIC's later history.
- **The Atomizer Link:** The Fricative Tumble (the transition through the Fold) is powered by the same resonance as the Atomizer—they are two sides of the same causal coin. The Atomizer destroys matter; the Fricative Tumble denatures matter into energy and re-assembles it at a lower entropy point.
- **Fibonacci Calibration:** The Tumble occurs along the Fibonacci sequence (1, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13...). Each number represents a "stress fracture" in the causal field. The jump is always 1, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13 years into the past—a recursive, violent folding of space-time.
