God’s country.

Muffled tones.
Blue vision.
A sensation.

In god’s country
Alone, together in god’s country
Footprints of giants in god’s country

Neither foot, nor hand shall set down
Not one item to be stolen
Nothing touched, in god’s country

Only respect for his mighty hand.
