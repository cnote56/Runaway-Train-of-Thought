You Can’t Park That Here

Genre: Sci-Fi Thriller

Theme: Government Conspiracy, Extraterrestrial Life, Technological
Advancement

Conflict: The U.S. government discovers alien technology after a crash
on the National Mall. They must decide whether to reveal the existence
of extraterrestrial life to the public or keep it a secret.

Setting: Washington D.C., 1947. The Cold War is escalating, and the
government is focused on national security.

Key Events:

- A mysterious flying object crashes on the National Mall.

- The government forms a secret task force to investigate the incident
  as an outbreak occurs of a strange flu.

- The task force discovers advanced alien technology as suspicion
  spreads.

- A government official leaks the information (by way of blackmail) to
  the press, sparking a national debate.

- A foreign power attempts to acquire alien technology while the
  mysterious flu spreads across the US and surfaces in the capitals of
  adversaries, indicative of espionage.

Storyline:

- Beginning: A mysterious flying object crashes into the National Mall
  in Washington, DC. The U.S. government immediately classified the
  incident and formed a secret investigative task force. A plague
  quietly spreads from the initial crash - a nasty flu that ultimately
  turns a person catatonic.

- Middle: The task force investigating the mysterious plane crash
  uncovers advanced alien technology and evidence of international
  espionage. Among the wreckage lies a sophisticated communication
  device and fragments of a spacecraft, suggesting a secret alliance
  between extraterrestrial beings and a foreign power. As the government
  grapples with the dilemma of whether to reveal the existence of
  extraterrestrial life to the public, unaware of a far more pressing
  threat, the plague spreads rapidly. The clandestine theft of alien
  technology by foreign agents unleashes the virus upon unsuspecting
  cities like Moscow, Beijing, and Tehran overnight, while the true
  nature of the outbreak remains concealed.

- End: The government decides to keep the alien technology a secret,
  fearing the consequences of widespread panic and international
  instability. The task force successfully thwarts the foreign power's
  attempt to steal the technology. However, the secret eventually leaks
  to the press, sparking a national conspiracy theory.A catastrophic
  turn of events unfolds as the alien spacecraft crashes, bringing forth
  a deadly plague that escapes its containment. The sinister virus,
  unleashed upon the world, spreads rapidly, wreaking havoc on the human
  nervous and electrical systems. This technology-based contagion
  disrupts the core of human biology, causing unprecedented symptoms and
  unimaginable suffering.

  In a bid to maintain control and prevent mass panic, the government
  decides to conceal the existence of the alien technology. However,
  their efforts are in vain as the truth seeps through the cracks,
  sparking a national conspiracy theory. The world is plunged into chaos
  as people grapple with the terrifying reality of a virus that defies
  understanding and challenges the limits of human endurance.

Protagonist:

- Name: Agent Alex Carter

- Backstory: A former military pilot with a deep-seated belief in the
  existence of extraterrestrial life.

- Motivated to protect humanity and uncover the truth about alien
  technology, a dedicated and idealistic agent with a strong sense of
  duty.

Secondary Characters:

- Dr. Sarah Miller: A brilliant astrophysicist recruited by the
  government to study alien technology; a brilliant but cautious
  scientist skeptical of government secrecy.

- General Thomas Stone: The head of the secret task force, a hardened
  military man prioritizing national security, a pragmatic leader who
  believes that the government must protect the nation at all costs.

- Agent Anya Petrova: A Russian intelligence officer sent to Washington
  D.C. to acquire the alien technology; a skilled and ruthless agent
  willing to do anything to achieve her goals.
