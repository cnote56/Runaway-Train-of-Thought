# SHOW RUNNER BRIEFING: THE ALLEGIANCE

## LOGLINE
In a world where reality is a refinery managed by frequency-tuning "Harps" and haunted by warring celestial Titans, an indifferent protagonist—the "One-Hundred-Year Man"—must choose between stabilizing a decaying simulation or triggering the final debt-release.

## NARRATIVE PILLARS
1. THE REFINERY: Earth is not a planet; it is an industrial facility for "Lightning Bringers" residing in a bubble reality (The Pocket).
2. THE HARP: Frequencies stabilize existence. Humanity's purpose is to manage these machines.
3. THE TITAN'S WAR: Celestial entities fight in orbit, raining mutagenic "coolant" (Titan Blood) that poisons the planet.
4. THE DEBT: The simulation is powered by debt-density. Every human sweat/blood drop is a dividend for the invisible creditors.

## VOICE SIGNATURES
- ARCHITECT (The System): Cold, clinical, procedural, technical. Use to describe mechanics, logs, and security protocols.
- HUMANIST (The Witness): Introspective, rhythmic, cynical, traumatized. Use to describe the human cost, the "Titan blood" weather, and the existential weight of the Tumble.

## AESTHETIC & AURA
- Brutalist: Concrete, gears, metallic surfaces, ionized air.
- Synthetic: "The Render" (holographic/manipulated) vs "The Real" (decaying/filthy).
- No Em-dashes. No AI-isms. Blunt, efficient, technical language.

## THE AUDITOR'S JUDGMENT (Synthesis)
The system is failing. The "Harp" mechanics are the only thing preventing the world from cracking apart. The "Titans' War" creates continuous entropic pressure. We are in the final stage of a simulation-debt event. The protagonist's trajectory is the only variable that matters.
