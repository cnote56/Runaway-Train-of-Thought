# 13.8 Billion Years

The collection of stories exploring the timeline and galaxy of the universe since the Big Bang. Defines the cosmological and systemic structure of reality as an "ever-burning" experiment harvested by non-human entities.

## Critical Concepts
- [[the-harp]]: A frequency-based machine gifted by "gods" to anchor planetary levators and harvest resources. Managed by [[harp-keepers]].
- [[the-bang]]: The ignition point of our reality, perceived by overseers as a random experiment or flash of flame.
- [[harvest-economy]]: The conversion of biological labor, human genius, and trauma into debt-density assets for off-world extraction.

## Entities
- [[dobly]]: A Harp Keeper who committed sabotage and suicide to break the frequency cycle.
- [[the-majestir]]: Leader of the university sciences, colleague to Dobly.
- [[the-ambassador]]: Targeted for treaty negotiations regarding "special metals" for Harp upgrades.

## Narrative Voice Signatures
- [[architect]]: Clinical, cynical, focuses on systemic mechanics (the Warden, the Harp mechanics).
- [[humanist]]: Introspective, rhythmic, centers on the human cost of the harvest (Dobly's sacrifice, the trauma of the "under-class").
