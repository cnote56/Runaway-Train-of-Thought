When the Aliens Take Over...

Required Reading:

https://www.yahoo.com/news/time-traveling-drunk-man-says-155541478.html

This morning is brought to you by Led Zeppelin’s, Babe I’m Gonna Leave
You.

When the aliens take over, are they going to be like Lizard Nazi’s form
Space or are they going to be the floaty light creatures with the cool
synthesizer music. Either way, I’m in for a shitburger of a time because
I prefer my alien overlords to wear their skin masks all the time and
keep the semblance of a representative democracy somewhat in tact. At
least as best it can with the social media plague we have all
contracted. I also prefer Punk Rock over Synthpop and Blues to Jazz. So
If i’m going to have to build the light machine synthesizer to
communicate with the floaty ones this isn’t going to be easy.

Besides the Lizard-Fuckers out there are going to be troublesome. The
cosmetic industry is going to see a lot of growth; they’ll need
realistic skin masks and lots of make-up. I’m just worried about how to
survive in the desert with all the MadMax-Kid-Touchers and their
self-congratulating hordes of canned foods and shotguns. The I told you
so’s are going to get annoying after the first week and half. Then I
know I’m going to punch someone’s cousin-husband and I’ll get put in the
tree-cage and I give another 48 hours before they either reenact the
3-PO Golden God scene from Return of the Jedi and try to cook and eat
me. I’m chubby so there will likely be some good marbling in there, but
I’m also likely very stringy in certain spots. And frankly if I’m going
to go out like that, I’d prefer to at least be worth the bonfire.

I hate to assuage you of any preconceived notions, but I’m an indoor
cat. I don’t do the survivalist routine very well. I hated camping when
I was a kid. I almost died on a family camping trip once. I don’t
remember but I was told I stopped breathing once.

I spent much of the morning yesterday feeling good about my prospects. I
even found some time to write and arrange the Sex Pistols Anarchy in the
U.K. into a country-folk version on my acoustic guitar.

I figured rearranging a Punk song into a folk ballad is a really “punk”
thing to do, so I tossed two fingers in the air and started to finger
pick my way into the open chords and pentatonic key.

Now, today I face two daunting tasks. A haircut and beard trim then a
job interview downtown. One must be done before the other and preferably
with the option of washing the trimmings.

But I’m having a better morning than most. Especially the
time-travellers out there. According to a story published on Yahoo, a
man was arrested yesterday for being drunk in public in Wyoming. Not a
huge deal, but his story is what compels me this morning. He said he was
form 2048 and had travelled back in time to prevent the aliens from
invading next year. He was there to warn us. When asked why he was
three-sheets to the wind in public he answered that was the only the
aliens from the future could make him time travel. He had to get smashed
in order to flash back on the time travelling pad.

Yes, it all makes sense now. I don’t like to to take a airplane flight
without a few beers in me. I can’t imagine blasting back 30 years sober.

A friend said, “hey at least there’s a future.” That’s only mildly
reassuring. So this is where we go from here.
