# MK-Ultra Sub-Project 1624

## Overview
**Sub-Project 1624** was a highly classified, covert operations branch within the United States chemical-intelligence division, specifically tasked with exploring "extreme behavioral control" during the Cold War. Operating in a series of hidden cells across the Eastern Seaboard, the division specialized in combining psychological trauma with hypnotic suggestibility.

## Core Directives & Operations
*   **The Soporific Handshake:** The division perfected chemical and hypnotic techniques to induce deep, unrecallable trance-states in test subjects.
*   **The Blank Slate Protocol:** By utilizing sensory deprivation, the division aimed to erase a subject's conscious memory entirely, leaving a clean "slate" upon which a new, programmed identity could be written.
*   **The Corporate Liaison:** Sub-Project 1624 maintained discrete ties to the early banking and industrial conglomerates that would eventually form [[the-fatherhood]]. They treated the human mind as an asset class—a resource to be managed, consolidated, and liquidated.

## The Dreyfuss Connection
Decrypted archives from [[grandfather-audit-73]] and the [[gilded-occult-lore]] confirm that **Asset 1624-B** (the Orphan) is a direct collateral descendant of the Dreyfuss bloodline, held under confinement in the "Foundation Pit" of Halcyon House. Sub-Project 1624 was the original division that identified the genetic and psychological markers of this bloodline, earmarking them for long-term "render" experiments.

## Key Facilities & Assets
- **[[cia-safehouse-baltimore]]**: The principal active field terminal where subjects were processed and hypnotized under the supervision of [[dr-vance-aldrich]].
- **[[raw/drafts/the-brainwashing-manual-experiment]]**: The primary operational ledger documenting the successes and catastrophic failures of the division's clinical trials.
