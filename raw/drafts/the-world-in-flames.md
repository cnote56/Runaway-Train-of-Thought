I see…

I see the suit wearing man preach about god, bombs and oil.
…The rough hands breaking knuckles on wrenches and bolts
…Laws as words on paper written long ago
…Potholes in the streets schools collapsing in on themselves
…Dirty faced children staring back at me
…Trouble finding victims on any morning
…Evil crying foul when tallies are marked
…Hands dipped in soapiness trying to make clean
…Blood covering borders and maps painted red
…Us and them fighting for the art of fighting
…Tears in the eyes of a dark-skinned man
…The hate moistened by the blood on the ground
…The world still turning chasing the sun
…The church doors swing both ways
…The art burning in the alleyways
…The spectacle of being alive
…A mother crying praying for a sons lost soul
…Missiles replacing swords
…Death coming for those who don’t even see
…Pillars of light blinding god
…Ghosts of things that once were
…Praises to Buddha, Allah, God and Jehovah
…Broken backs under skin covered numbers
…Speeches given ‘bout god, bombs and oil
