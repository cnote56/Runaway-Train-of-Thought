Work History

By Cole Dowden

A man named Henry is caught between worlds that exist and do not. Spurs
of time-space hurled off the chaos of the known universe of this
timeline. Henry will die penniless. But he also will be broke and
suffering in the future. A man caught out of the loop and weighed
wanting in luck - potential, and first in being a failed promise of a
man. Life’s pitfalls seemed to find his shoulders at every turn. Born to
junkies and dropped in a box at a fire department in a nameless town
crossed off a map that never was for all the purposes he has in his
life. Crippled with depression, he bounced from place and city after a
tumultuous childhood; Henry gains a grip on adult life. He finds a
meaningless job and a partner to love him.

Then, she dies in childbirth.

Twins survive. Henry's thoughts are bleak with the Job’s-life he’s been
dealt with if we accept luck as a controlling force in our world.

Henry is suddenly hit with a debilitating and terminal nerve syndrome.

Your blood carries a terrible fate. The computer-generated voice of the
AI Doctor droned. Henry faced the prognosis that he would die suddenly
and soon. Three years maximum. It was inevitable that the degeneration
of his nerves would be sudden, and its onset would be debilitating.
Medications would help extend his time, but he couldn’t afford them.
Worse yet, it was genetic. His children would carry the same fate --
just twenty to thirty years later than he would. They might get to
remember him on his deathbed for their first memory. But for the rest of
their lives, they’d be alone.

They lay swaddled safely in the robotic arms of the nursery’s massive
Cradlebot. Oblivious to the stunted life forced in their veins, the
newborns were recharging their little lungs to bellow without thought
and furious little hearts that stubbornly refused to give up.

Each child had its cradle basket and was being softly rocked to sleep
with twelve other newborns, delivered automatically with Medbots and
Able-Drone's assistance.

Each new life brings a fresh new life into a new world. When their
mother died in childbirth, their father, Henry, had just received very
unpleasant news regarding the newly widowed father of two.

They’d live a life as long as his, and then, about the age he is now,
would face sudden and painful organ failure, system after system would
drop out and age suddenly.

They will be dead in a few months, sometime after their 25th birthday,
respectively.

The AI Doctor conveyed the news and switched off. Its form remained
inert, as the software and energy were elsewhere now.

Driven to the second most extreme measure a man can take. Henry stands
on the roof of the hospital. He just wandered after the doctor read him
the news. He is staring at the horizon of the nation’s capital, the
spike of the Washington Monument off in the distance, and the round dome
of the Capitol building to his left. His right foot steps to the ledge.
His other foot won’t join. Then, a voice nearly stopped his heart cold.

Mr. Henry.

His body was frozen, his heart raced, and his mind aflame. His eyes
widened as his head whipped to the voice, and he found a man standing a
few feet from him—strangely close to him.

I have a proposition for you.

Henry’s thoughts fired; where the fuck did you come from? I’ve been up
here for an hour thinking about this shit, right here! How the fuck?!
Henry’s voice was in his head - he couldn’t speak if he wanted.

The man was clad in a dark blue uniform of a shining matte-infused woven
material wrapped around his robust frame.

Who the fuck?

His broad shoulders carried emblems and braids of a military panache.

Where the fuck?!

He stood feet comfortably shoulder-width apart. His arms were wrapped
behind him, his left arm crossed over his right, and each hand gripped
its respective forearm.

Who the fuck?!

A small cube clung to the stranger’s side with no noticeable means of
attachment—like a belt or loop—but it seemed to both adhere to his suit
and float just next to it. It glowed with a rolling, soft, multicolored
selection of lights, giving the scene a soft offset light from the
orange burn of the street lights below.

The lights swirled in Henry’s eyes, and he was instantly emotionally
disarmed—suddenly relaxed. The snap of the hypnotist's fingers and the
combination of patterns and tones filled Henry’s vision. His ears buzzed
with a whining pitch like a frequently dying scream. And then, Henry was
fine and completely normal—nothing like the wreck of himself he was a
moment ago—the one who had thought the express elevator down was his
only fate.

I wanted to make you a job offer. Now seemed the most opportune time to
discuss terms.

Time travel is possible. 

A select few humans hold a unique protein identifier in the nooks and
crannies of their DNA. They hold a key that unlocks the human body's
potential that is currently latched tight.

The Kozyrev Mirror was once a hysterical farce scribbled on the napkin
of a half-drunk Russian who feared a gulag sentence if he could not
satisfy the madman in Moscow.

It was not originally called a mirror, but once the prototype was built,
the shine of the thorium-infused metal suddenly charged itself from the
air around it and flashed out of existence.

The second Kosorev’s Mirrors was built in stages and kept apart until
the final construction required it to encircle the traveler. The
first-time traveler was a welder who flicked out of existence just as he
finished the molten bead.

At first glance, you might find it captivating—a cylindrical spiral of
polished aluminum, perhaps glistening in the light like a beacon of
scientific hope. But let’s not kid ourselves; this is no breakthrough in
modern physics; it's a pseudoscientific contraption born from the
fertile imagination of a few too many vodka-soaked late nights in the
1990s.

Imagine this: a device supposedly capable of focusing psychic energy or
even bending time itself and, yes, bending time. Who wouldn't fall for
that at first? It's all too enticing—a shiny toy for anyone with a flair
for the dramatic or an affinity for half-baked theories. The allure of
peering into the past or channeling your inner telepath through a piece
of twisted aluminum seems like a jackpot for anyone craving adventure
beyond the mundanity of reality.

But as the excitement grows, one must step back and question the
absurdity. Here, we have a mirror (and let’s spare the use of the term
"scientific device") that claims the ability to neutralize Earth’s
electromagnetic field while interacting with "types of radiation" akin
to a magnifying glass. Indeed, anyone with a basic understanding of
optics would raise an eyebrow. Are we expected to believe that a few
metal sheets can transcend time and space?

Yes. Just as Kosorev was resigning himself to a noose rather than a
firing squad nearby or a prolonged death in Siberia, he faced a man with
broad shoulders wearing a dark blue suit. He stood, hands crossed behind
him, a glowing cube on his hip.

The man spoke directly while ignoring the noose around Kosorev’s neck.

He has Kosorev’s complete attention.

Henry faces a unique plan wrapped in an impossibility.

In the sweltering heat of mid-July 1916, Henry stood on the windswept
docks of Baltimore, the salty air mingling with the acrid scent of coal
and steam. Time had alienated him in two dimensions: he was a wanderer,
displaced not only from the physical world of 1916 but also from the
future he knew in 2036—one plagued by the suffocating grip of terminal

Amid the scorching heat of mid-July 1916, Henry found himself standing
atop the windswept docks of Baltimore. The salty air intertwined with
the spicy scent of coal and steam. He was a wanderer, trapped in an
impossible situation—alienated from both the physical world of 1916 and
the familiar future of 2036, plagued by a terminal illness that would
extinguish his family lineage. Yet, paradoxically, he was 124 years in
the past.

Yet, a flicker of hope emerged among the thrumming heartbeat of the
bustling harbor. Soon, Henry was certain of it - on Friday, July 15,
1916, the arrival of the U-boat Deutschland heralded a lucrative
opportunity that could grant his children a better future despite his
bleak fate.

The Great War’s shadow cast whole nations into darkness. A marketplace
abhors a vacuum, and the demand for effective dyes and inks surged.
Henry understood the complexities of the market, having immersed himself
in supply and demand from a future perspective. The war effort needed
resources to support the military machinery, leading to a skyrocketing
demand for vibrant inks that could produce propaganda materials—posters
calling for enlistment, pamphlets outlining the virtues of sacrifice,
and banners to rally the spirit of the American populace. The raw
materials, controlled tightly by a few suppliers, were now on the brink
of a pivotal shortage as European shipping routes became dangerous from
the British blockade of German ships - and their exports - to American
markets.

The anticipation rose as the Deutschland docked, casting ripples of
change across the ink and dye marketplace. With each crate of
German-processed aniline dyes that hit the Baltimore docks, the tides
shifted, and Henry had been anticipating this moment for the past 15
days.

The scarcity of substitutes during the war created a perfect storm for
entering the market, a rare chance for a time traveler like him to
capitalize on the converging timelines. He envisioned cornering the
market, distributing supplies in discreet channels, and watching as
prices soared with each passing week of conflict.

As he approached the U-boat, a flurry of dockworkers unloaded crates
filled with brightly colored chemicals—reds, blues, and greens that
could transform paper into vibrant statements. This was his moment.
Henry's plan was simple yet effective: to buy and store large quantities
of these precious dyes and inks before selling them at a premium.
Historical focus groups in future texts had echoed how a sudden influx
in demand paired with limited supply could yield fortunes for the
discerning investor.

Yet this venture was not solely for wealth accumulation. It was a
desperate attempt to fund a future for his children in 2034, to provide
them with opportunities denied to him due to his illness. Henry had
signed a contract with the enigmatic time travel company; their strict
rules bound him to the past, forbidding any attempt to return, unless he
should affect the lineage that would follow. Every ink-filled drop of
his plan resonated with the hope of his children's lives bettering
through his temporal sacrifice.

But unbeknownst to Henry, destiny had woven a twist into his narrative.
As he meticulously plotted his financial ascent, the arrangement with
the time travel company came at a significant cost. His body had been
swapped unknowingly with that of a wealthy magnate from the past who had
mysteriously vanished from time itself, leaving behind a void filled
with riches in the future where they would thrive under circumstances
far more favorable than Henry could have ever imagined.

Caught in the crossfire of ambition and the unseen consequences of his
journey, Henry had set in motion a plan that would carve out a legacy
for his children, yet he was not the knight in shining armor he
imagined; rather, he unwittingly became an architect of fate, entwined
in the enigmatic dance of time. His heart raced with anticipation as he
carried the weight of his intended prosperity—one that would fortify not
only his lineage but also hew the unexpected pathways of wealth far into
the future.

Time-Value of Money 

This story concept revolves around time travel's implications on
investments and the time value of money, thrusting characters into a
future where franchising and ethical dilemmas of profit-making
intertwine. Using theories of time travel that avoid paradoxes, the
story can explore complex investment strategies while emphasizing the
emotional stakes involved in making choices that benefit future
generations.

###

The story's universe is set in a future where a powerful corporation,
Crohaus-Nexxon GmbH-MNPLLC, holds a monopoly on time travel technology.

Known as ChroNex, they utilize this technology not just for exploration
but also for financial gain, establishing franchises in various
historical periods.

Time Travel Mechanics

ChroNex employs a system where traveling to the past tears the fabric of
time—`a branching timeline theory. This allows individuals to interact
with the past without creating paradoxes; any significant alterations
result in the birth of a new timeline while leaving the original intact.

The cost of time travel is determined by the potential impact on
subsequent timelines:

- **Closer to the Present:** Less expensive and lower risk, as changes
affect fewer systems.

- **Further Back in Time:** More costly; the higher risk is correlated
with the ripple effects these actions create within the overarching
timeline.

In this narrative, the time traveler must make investment decisions
using the advanced knowledge of future market behavior while ensuring
that none of the money they create or earn benefits them directly.

###

The Time-Value of Money and Investments

The principle of the time value of money states that a sum of money has
greater value now than it will at some future date because of its
earning potential. In ChroNex's universe, travelers are instructed to
consider this principle avidly:

- **Investment Strategies**: The protagonist will choose investments
with high long-term potential, leveraging knowledge of future economic
trends. They can select assets expected to gain significant value due to
historical occurrences.

- **Inflation Considerations**: The traveler must also account for
inflation rates in the timeframe they are visiting, ensuring their
investments far exceed potential inflation costs. This aspect can be
tied to ChroNex's sophisticated data analysis systems that predict
inflation trends based on historical events.

###

Creative Liberties With Known Time Travel Theories

While the story aligns closely with known time travel theories, some
creative liberties will be taken to introduce unique stakes and ethical
dilemmas.

- **Branching Timelines**: By defining actions in the past as creating
branching timelines, the story sidesteps known paradoxes such as the
notorious grandfather paradox. This allows for a coherent exploration of
investment decisions without risking self-contradiction.

- **Ethical Dilemmas**: The reality that every financial decision made
in the past will alter the present affirms that while personal gain is
prohibited, any wealth generated will benefit future generations through
indirect means, like gifts to offspring or funding social projects back
in ChroNex's timeline.

Black Tom Island, New York Harbor, Late-July 1916.

Late July 1916, Black Tom Island functioned as a pivotal munitions depot
for the United States, playing a crucial role in supplying arms to
Allied forces in World War I. Daily operations included the storage,
preparation, and shipping of vast quantities of ammunition and
explosives, which were often subjected to logistical challenges and
security vulnerabilities. This environment ultimately culminated in a
catastrophic explosion, instigated by sabotage from German agents.

### Daily Operations

The daily operations on Black Tom Island revolved around the management
and distribution of munitions. The island served as a major depot where
large quantities of arms, including ammunition and explosives, were
stored in warehouses, railcars, and barges awaiting shipment to Allied
forces ((19)) ((22)). At that time, approximately 2 million pounds of
small arms and artillery ammunition were housed on the island, along
with around 100,000 pounds of TNT ((23)).

### Shipping and Logistics

Black Tom Island was strategically connected to the mainland via a
causeway and was accessible by rail, allowing for the efficient
transport of munitions ((4)). Daily tasks involved coordinating
shipments, which required meticulous planning and organization.
Munitions were prepared and loaded onto ships for delivery to locations
in Europe, primarily directed toward the Allies, as American arms
dealers sold to any warring nation despite the U.S. being officially
neutral at that time ((3)) ((22)).

### Security Challenges

Despite its importance, Black Tom Island faced significant security
challenges due to its open and easily accessible layout. The perimeter
was virtually unguarded, and there were no effective security measures
in place to prevent sabotage or unauthorized access ((68)). This
vulnerability was exploited by German agents who aimed to disrupt U.S.
munitions supply efforts to their adversaries in the war ((49)) ((50)).

### Incidents Leading to the Explosion

In the days leading up to the catastrophic events of July 30, 1916,
small fires were reported around the docking area, which were early
signs of an impending disaster. Although these were initially addressed,
the fires remained a concern as they indicated negligence pertaining to
safety and enforcement protocols ((24)).

### The Day of the Explosion

On the night of July 29, the atmosphere on Black Tom Island was tense
but operational, with personnel actively engaged in managing the
munitions awaiting shipment ((40)). However, the situation escalated
when German saboteurs ignited explosives at the munitions depot, leading
to a series of massive explosions commencing at 2:08 a.m. on July 30,
causing widespread devastation ((19)) ((75)).

### Aftermath of Daily Operations

The aftermath of the explosion drastically altered the perception of
operations at Black Tom Island. Investigations revealed the extent of
negligence and security failures, ultimately prompting significant
changes in how munitions were managed and protected in the United States
((56)). The catastrophic event also reshaped public opinion, leading to
heightened tensions and the eventual U.S. entry into World War I—a
direct consequence of the operations conducted in the lead-up to the
explosion ((33)).

### Summary

In summary, the daily operations on Black Tom Island in late July 1916
involved managing an extensive munitions depot that was crucial for
supporting the Allied war effort. Despite its strategic importance, the
island was rife with logistical and security challenges, ultimately
culminating in a devastating act of sabotage that would resonate through
U.S. history.
