Kacy,



      I pray this finds you well...if ever, this will find you.  I haven’t
forgotten you. In fact I can still hear your voice at times, telling me
nothing in particular, just words.  I remember the last time I saw you the
most.  Your house in Washington terrace.  I shouldn’t have let you go...I
know that all too much now.  I still hope to hold you again, even for just
a moment.  The world would stop, the mountains crumble, and the sun would
never set again...for me just holding you again would be enough fuel to
burn as many torches as there are stars in the sky.

      Now I realize that it’s been three years, almost.  People change and
grow, but there has always been the constant in everything I’ve put my hand
to...you.  Although we’ve been apart, I’ve kept the thought alive.  I’ll be
turning Twenty this November. That means, if memory serves, you’ll be
Twenty-one.  Remember the Songs I wrote to you...I was such a fool.  Too
much to soon, I guess.  I still play the guitar and I have even picked up a
few more instruments.  But it means nothing without you to hear it. There
are times when I’ll be writing a new piece I’ll think of you and the piece
will write itself. Not to sound too weird...but your memory, just the
memory, serves as my personal muse.

      You were my best friend.  Someday I hope to see my best friend again.
If nothing more than just a tomato, turkey, and mayo sandwich to share
between us...just to be near you again...your still in my thoughts,
everyday.



love,

        Cole
