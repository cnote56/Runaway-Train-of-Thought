"I was thinking of changing my..." The blast tore apart the very words
in the air with shards of fire and shrapnel. Blood and sinew. Bone and
soul.

In the shockwave of the blast God is the first to cry.

We'll never know of what the victim was thinking of changing. We'll
never know the undreamt dreams incurred while sleeping victims slept
into their deaths. May the soul find its way to heaven or whatever may
be next.

In the maelstrom of fire and evil, the devil embraces his victims with
both arms while wrapping his tail around their necks.
