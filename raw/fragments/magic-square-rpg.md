# The Magic Square Tabletop RPG

> **The Magic Square** is a tabletop roleplaying game of cosmic management and mathematical sorcery. In this system, players take on the role of **Operators** who balance geometric grids, bind entities, and manipulate numerical matrices to rewrite the underlying source code of the universe.

---

## 🌌 Overview

The game operates on the principal of **Mathematical Hermeticism**, where numerical balances correspond directly to cosmic structures. Operators utilize specialized grid sheets called **The Blueprint** to perform calculations, align dimensions, and control the flow of metaphysical energy.


## ⚙️ Core Mechanics

### 1. The Blueprint (Grid Balance)
The primary gameplay loop revolves around filling a square grid of order $N$ (typically $3 \times 3$ or $4 \times 4$) with sequential integers such that the sums of all rows, columns, and diagonals equal the **Magic Constant** ($M$).

The Magic Constant is calculated using the formula:
$$M = \frac{n(n^2 + 1)}{2}$$

*   **Order-3 (Saturn Square):** Constant is $15$.
*   **Order-4 (Jupiter Square):** Constant is $34$.

When an Operator's grid is perfectly balanced, they achieve **Resonance**, which allows them to cast spells or stabilize anomalies.

### 2. Entity Summoning & Binding
Operators can summon high-level entities (demons or angels associated with planetary spheres) to execute complex cosmic computations.

*   **Orience** (King of the East)
*   **Paymon** (King of the West)
*   **Amaymon** (King of the South)
*   **Egine** (King of the North)
*   **Lucifer** (The Light Bringer)

Summoning an entity requires a sacrifice or specific alignment. Once summoned, the entity must be **bound** using a balanced grid. If the Operator’s grid is unbalanced during a binding attempt, the entity escapes, causing a **Code Collapse** or mental trauma to the Operator.

### 3. Operator Archetypes
Players choose from specialized roles that dictate how they interact with the cosmic grid:

| Archetype | Specialization | Key Ability |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Mathematician** | Grid Optimization | Can swap adjacent cells once per turn |
| **Occultist** | Entity Binding | Gains bonus willpower when binding spirits |
| **Cosmologist** | Matrix Alignment | Can alter the Magic Constant by $\pm 1$ |

### 4. Grimoire Inventory
Operators gather mystical herbs and geometric relics to enhance their arithmetic rituals:

*   🌿 **Rosemary** - Used to clarify thoughts and prevent grid corruption.
*   💍 **Ring of Solomon** - Grants absolute protection against unbound spirits.
*   🪄 **Verge Foudroyante** - A lightning-struck rod used to jumpstart stagnant grids.
*   🪵 **Peony** - A root that stabilizes Order-4 matrices.

## 📜 In-Universe Lore

According to the central texts of **The Cosmic Calculus**, the universe was constructed using a series of nested mathematical equations. Over eons, the "source code" has decayed, leading to physical anomalies, temporal leaks, and supernatural incursions. 

Those who discover the grid mathematics are known as **Operators**. Armed with chalk, parchment, and a sharp mind, they venture into unstable regions of reality to rewrite local parameters. Some work in service of the central **Fatherhood** administration, while others operate as underground heretics, rewriting the universe's code for personal liberation.
