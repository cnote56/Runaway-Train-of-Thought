# Project FILTER Plot Outline

An act-by-act breakdown of the novel *Project FILTER*, tracking Reggie Watts' escape from the surveillance mesh and the political battlefield surrounding cybernetic control.

## Act I: Protocol Wildfire
- **The Collapse:** Speaker of the House Baker dies of health complications. His Chief of Staff, Reggie Watts, has taken a weekend off to unplug his secure-link implant, leaving him completely blind to the political chaos and Cover-up unfolding on Capitol Hill.
- **The Rattle:** Reggie struggles with the constant physical buzz of the implant and resists re-engaging with the network. He heads to the office to meet Jessica Powell, his brilliant tech advisor.
- **The Escape:** Realizing he is being tracked and cornered by civilian coordinators like Clemens, Reggie is intercepted by Trevor, a rogue driver with a metallic eye, who drives him to a secure basement hideout behind New Hampshire Avenue.

## Act II: Closed-Door Battles
- **The Congressional Hearing:** Senator Chuck Ammerstand grills General Robinson in a closed-door briefing, demanding independent civilian oversight of FILTER surveillance warrants, exposing cracks in the legal foundation of the secure-link program.
- **The Safehouse:** Inside the basement SCIF of the Digital Underground, Reggie meets OpOne, a sarcastic operator who shows him the true scale of the data-monitoring network (including programs like YELLOWBOUNCE and CHILLYWATER).

## Act III: The Unplugging
- **Lockdown:** The intelligence apparatus initiates a complete digital lockdown to flush out Reggie and secure Speaker Baker's leaked data.
- **The Choice:** Reggie must decide whether to permanently sever his secure-link implant—risking brain damage and complete isolation—or use his unique clearance to leak the FILTER project's secrets to Senator Ammerstand's reformers.
