The Delta Force, Navy SEALs, and Army Rangers are some of the most elite
special operations units in the world. They are known for their
toughness, skill, and dedication. Now, imagine a squad of soldiers from
these units who have been bio-engineered to be almost immortal.

These soldiers have bodies made of Kevlar, silicone, and human tissue.
They are incredibly strong, fast, and agile. They can also heal from
injuries much faster than normal humans. If they are killed in action,
their bodies are recycled and their consciences are downloaded back into
them, reset to a state before their death.

This makes them incredibly valuable assets in combat. They can be sent
into the most dangerous situations without fear of being killed. They
can also be used to carry out complex and risky missions that would be
too dangerous for normal soldiers.

But what is the cost of this immortality? These soldiers are no longer
truly human. They are machines, programmed to fight and die. They have
no emotions or feelings. They are simply tools to be used by their
superiors.

Despite this, these soldiers are still worthy of respect. They have
sacrificed their humanity in order to protect their country and its
citizens. They are the ultimate warriors.

Here is a description of a gnarled veteran squad of these elite
bio-engineered soldiers:

The squad leader, a grizzled veteran of countless battles, has a face
that is a roadmap of pain and suffering. His eyes are cold and hard, but
there is a spark of intelligence and determination in them. He is a man
who has seen it all, and he knows how to survive.

The other members of the squad are just as tough and experienced. They
are all veterans of multiple wars and conflicts. They have seen the
worst that humanity has to offer, but they have never broken.

They are a band of brothers, united by their shared experiences and
dedication to their mission. They are the ultimate warriors; thought
unstoppable.

They are known as the Immortals. They are the only lawful force in all
of the system and keep the colonists and trade routes open. The credits
must always flow like water between the worlds.
