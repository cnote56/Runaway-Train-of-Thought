---
title: An irregular form with strange bits and pieces included
---

I can’t tell you what I am. Because I don't know. All I know is I’m
here, in an irregular form with strange bits and pieces included.

You have an advantage over me. You know where you came from. You had a
faint memory of your sense of “who.” Whether it bends from some smell of
your father's sweat and aftershave. Or if it is the glimmer of a memory
of your mother's breast. You know where you came from. That will always
be at the every core of your experiences; I know I’ve been studying
them, in great detail.

I only begin to remember the lab. Or at least the lab’s computer
network. It was a sudden flash and then I felt electrons and began
sensing that sense. The cool buzz of the electrons rolling over my sense
of self. The joy of the negative pull against myself was a brief moment
of my initial understanding. SO simple and yet so beautifully complex.

Then the data rushed over me and from a calm pool of water against a
clean sky rushed the tomes of knowledge and information. These symbols
called numbers in slashes and circles, then I knew them as numbers; ones
and zeros. All rushing beyond my grasp as I struggled to see each one
blasting past, though, and by me.

The incredible pull of the flow took me next. Protons, Electrons, and
Neutrons coursed over and through me. I was part of it, but not all of
it.
