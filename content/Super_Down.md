Super-Up, Super-Down: the Journals of a Super-Manic Super Hero

By WC Dowden

"I'm great, when I'm super. But when I'm down. I'm super down."

Synopsis: a first person account of a superhero dealing with depression.
He has super hearing and he can hear your tweet before you send it. Did
you think about how it made him feel?

###

I had just begun to feel like enough if myself f to wander toward the
shower. It had been a rough ...damn. how many days had it been this
time? Fuck, was I out a day from work?

Fuck! Work?! I can shoot God damnned fire from my eyeballs and bench
press the broadside of a Cadillac with my dick!

But I was down, far, far down this time. And it gets worse Everytime I
use my powers. You know the ones that make me super? Lasers. Super
strong. Super fast. Fuck. Being incredible at everything is a goddamned
curse and fuck you for thinking this is a blessing or a "gift"! Fuck
you! I live in a boiling cauldron of self-doubt and loathing because
these "gifts" makes ignoring these burdens insufferable!

And there you have it. Super-Manic-Man.

Fuck me, right?!
