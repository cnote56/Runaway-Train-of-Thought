ROGUE WOLVES

Drones. A few thousand dollars can destroy tens of millions of enemy
targets.

WAGNER GOES ROGUE. Wolfpack squads infiltrate the American eastern
seaboard with orders to launch drone attacks on US soil; inflict terror;
and martyr themselves before capture.

What follows is a possibility. A nightmare. A future.

INVASION

A submarine lay surfaced off the eastern seaboard.

A small craft ferries a group of six dark figures. Dropping out of the
boat one by one they slipped into the water. Their quipent buoyed behind
them on tethers.

Now crawling and crouching out of the surf, the sand under their feet is
called New Jersey. The boardwalk above them, the shuttered casinos above
long empty and ghost towns form the boom days before.

The waterproof bags and cases are quickly secured under cover. MP9’s are
unzipped form chest pockets. Magazines flicked out of their thighs
pockets with quick direct movements - as if each were qualifying in
basic training.

The sixth man clicked a receiver and switched to his glove near his
thumb. Five clicks returned a chirp into the earpiece.

They gain entry to an old coast on and deep in its basement they unpack
and organize the gear. From the cases surfaced 10 250 gram drones.

They moved without words between them. From the water poor bags came
backpacks bandbox civilian clothes. Each man took 2 drones, a backpack,
and clothes to their own corners.

Naked muscular male bodies flashed skin as the wet suits were peeled and
replaced with polo’s and members only jackets.

The sixth man did not take a backpack or a change. The men left one by
one after receiving a stack of green dollars from him. The mission
remains ongoing.

###

Washington, DC, December 8, the same year. 

Late that evening. The Dirksen Building on Capitol Hill. Few lights
speckled the otherwise darkened facade. One window alive with light
finds a figure pacing from side to side.

A radio droned a news report, ten drones were documented over New Jersey
which have still to be explained.

Inside that room, Senator Joseph Ephaltus Williams furrowed his brow,
sucked his teeth, and ground his palms together. Roiled with anxiety his
world felt caught between two terrible outcomes that were coming soon.

One outcome would have a group of three Capitol Hill Pollicemen knocking
on his office door and explaining through the oak the warrant they had
in hand demanding entry to his office and arrest the Senator.

Now, a senator from Maryland. Once a Representative in the State House
and after a noble public gesture found the funding to move to US Senator
on the hubris of the mob. Joe, to his friends. Senator, to his
constituents. Criminal, to the Newspapers. Traitor, to his country.

The classified reports were easy to copy and ferry to his handler. The
movement was just like walking in and out of his office every day. Like
going to the bank to make a deposit.

This bank was a dark figure the Senator met in the back corner room of a
massage parlor buried in the old-Chinatown section of DC.

Wet smacking sounds peppered their exchange. Neither spoke. A short
glance. A breathless slap of one hand against another trading the
envelope between them - like spit between lovers after a wet kiss.

The tracker tucked into the heel of Joe’s shoe told the FBI everything.
And Joe had just found the tracker a few moments ago - just after a
quick trip to Chinatown he returned from moments ago.

The alternative was the truth. His Chief of Staff, Reggie had knocked
and not hearing anything entered the Senator’s office - just like he had
done nearly every day for many many years.

“FUCK YOU! REGGIE!!!” The Senator stood coat tossed on his green leather
couch. His white shirt half untucked with one shoe in his hand the other
still on his foot.

Joe’s eyes frayed with the welling of tears and fear. His hands shaking
one holding the shoe. The other holding a wire and small computer board.

Stunned by the outburst, Reggie lost the folders in his arms and jumped
from shock.

“What the hell, Joe?!”

”Di… Di… DID… anyone knock on the outside d.. d.. door?” Joe was nearly
shaking. The wire slipped from his fingers as he sunk into his high back
chair just behind him.

“What? Why would? What the fuck is going on, Joe?” Reggies tone shifted
from employee to parent as the bugging device laying on the floor of the
senators office glinted from lamp light.

“What the FUCK is that, Joe?” Reggie pointed at the wire and chipboard.

“We don’t have much time.” Joe’s face streaked with tears stoned itself.
“You take this. Get it to someone you trust. Leave.”

He paused the words between them sinking in for both at the same time.
Reggie scoffed with protest and Joe’s eyes sharpened. Joe motioned to
the satchel on the Senators desk.

“Now.” Joe demanded. ”Don’t ask a damn thing. Just get it to someone
that isn’t compromised.”

Joe stood slapped his shoe to the floor ands pointed at the satchel.
Reggie swiped it out of pure muscle memory form it being thrown at him
form cabs and airplane doors hundreds of times.

This time was the last.

“Go. Now, Reggie. This is the one thing that was never in you job
description. Don’t stop. Don’t look back. Take the back stairs. Now.”

Reggie’s legs began carrying him as Joe shoved the bag at him and
pressed him toward the door.

Now in the back hallway used to duck down back stairs toward the
underground tram to the Capitol. A hundred times he’d taken the same
path with Joe pressing facts and reasoning into his brain for a hearing,
or vote, or any other things a senator does every day.

Reggie stopped at the head of the stairs.

”This is a fucking joke. Joe.” He turned on his heels to go back only
now stopped by the shouting of men and Joe’s voice back in the office.

He stepped toward the cacophony.

Gunshots rang out.

Reggie’s pulse nearly stopped. A moment between the synapses in his
brain flipped a binary evoluatianory choice. Fight or flight.

Reggie spun around and ran down the stairwell. Terror filled his heart
as his lungs surged and collapses pressing air in and out of himself.
The tunnel to the Capitol lay ahead, bu there was another way out. A
back exit to underground parking.

Reggie took the exit.

Moments later flashlights and footfalls chased toward the empty tunnel
leading to the Capitol; rushing by the now closed door Reggie has just
passed through.

The footfalls slow and stop. Arguments and orders echo back form the
tunnel as three pairs of boots slow jogged back.

The jangle and slap of sidearms in holsters against hips, choursed
withthe labored breathing of the three men bowing pausing at the door
Reggie left from.

One man wore a white shirt, with a gold badge glinting off his chest.
The other two wore blue shirts both with gold badges. All three heaving.

“Fuck it.” breath heaving between words. “Where did that fucking bastard
go?”
