I remember it clearly now
I wanted to kill them
All of them I had the plan and it consisted on a series of bombs. I had the
design and I did it. I finally did it. the club was empty and I was finally
king.

Then I opened my eyes and inhaled the smoke of my own cigarette. I was
coated in a film of rotating lights and all I saw were idiots raving
lunatics. With repeating rhythms and ignorant gestures.

I was still king.
