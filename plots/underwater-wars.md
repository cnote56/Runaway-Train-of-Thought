# The Underwater Wars: Battle for the Sunken Lands

A narrative of survival set in 2077, detailing the conflict for resources on the ocean floor following nuclear devastation.

## Core Plot
- After nuclear war ravages the surface, humanity clusters in underwater settlements.
- The primary resource for survival and power is [[helium-3]], extracted from the seabed.
- The [[defender]]—a hybrid submarine-tank—is the central weapon of the human survivors against AI-driven machines.

## Key Themes
- [[the-sunken-lands]]: The final frontier for human resource extraction and combat.
- [[technological-existentialism]]: The threat posed by AI machines, which are also desperate for energy resources.
- [[sabotage-and-infiltrator]]: The discovery of cyborg infiltrators among human crews, signaling the total corruption of the human fleet.

## Technical Mechanics
- [[defender-sub]]: A submarine equipped with a repurposed space-program gravity engine (neutron engine) channeling fusion reactions into subatomic powertrains.

## Entities
- [[commander-anya]]: Decorated captain of the [[defender]].
- [[the-lieutenant]]: The protagonist, an investigator of systemic sabotage within the fleet.
