RAW CHAPTER NOTES - EPISODE 101 ADAPTATION STAGE
Title: Through a Glass, Darkly (First Draft Scenes)

Scene 1: Craigh na Dun - Dawn
- Claire touches the stone. Sound design: high-pitched buzzing, wind howling, the sound of traditional drumming under a heartbeat.
- Transition: abrupt cut to Claire falling onto the forest floor. The trees are different. The light is colder. The dirt is damp and rich.
- Visual: A redcoat soldier appears, pointing a flintlock musket. She thinks it's a costume or a film set. The real threat of the bullet whizzing past her ear breaks her post-WWII security.

Scene 2: Encounter with Captain Jonathan "Black Jack" Randall
- First reaction: She thinks it's Frank (her husband) because of the identical facial structure.
- Contrast: The coldness in Black Jack's eyes. The lack of Frank's warmth and intellectual kindness.
- Action: Jamie's clan members rescue her just in time.
