# Jessica Powell

Jessica Powell is an exceptionally brilliant former Air Force technical specialist who serves as a key tech advisor in Representative Baker's office on Capitol Hill.

## Biography
Recognized as one of the most gifted programmers and technical specialists the Air Force ever produced, Jessica transitioned to a civilian advisory role on Capitol Hill. She was tasked with the handholding and implementation of **[[the-filter]]** secure-link systems for senior congressional representatives, specifically guiding the stubborn and grumbling Speaker Baker. 

Reggie Watts holds a deep respect and soft spot for her due to her stunning athletic presence, vibrant red hair, and near-wizardry technical skills. When Speaker Baker's health collapses, Jessica becomes a critical anchor of technical support and intelligence.

## Characteristics
- **Appearance:** Tall, athletic, red-haired.
- **Skills:** Expert in network protocols, hardware interfaces, and cybernetics.
- **Relations:** Trusted colleague and ally of **[[reggie-watts]]**.
