# CIA Safehouse (Baltimore)

## Overview
The **CIA Safehouse (Baltimore)** was a covert, heavily shielded field terminal located in a non-descript brick rowhouse in Baltimore, Maryland. During the 1950s and 1960s, it functioned as the primary laboratory for [[mk-ultra-division]] research.

## Features & Security
*   **The Isolation Chamber:** A soundproof, lightproof basement room designed to simulate an absolute sensory void (The Void Interface). This room contained an early, highly unrefined version of the sensory-deprivation tanks.
*   **Biochemical Lockers:** Heavily secured storage lockers containing rare, highly restricted soporifics and psychoactive agents sourced from global pharmaceutical networks.
*   **Signals Intercept Terminal:** A room outfitted with early, vacuum-tube signal receivers and recording equipment to monitor local communications, functioning as the primitive ancestor to modern [[the-filter]] operator bays.

## Historical Significance
The safehouse was the site of the infamous "Lazarus Experiments" conducted by [[dr-vance-aldrich]]. These experiments aimed to find a method to permanently "rewrite" human memory and volition through hypnosis and sensory capture.

The data gathered in this facility was later used by [[the-fatherhood]] planners to design the original blueprints for [[the-capital-city]]'s re-education centers and the standard [[bloxes]]' chemical dispersal units.
