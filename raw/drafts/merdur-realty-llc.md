Chapter One

“It’s M! E! R! NOT! M! U! R!” exasperated and constant, this was David Merdur’s usual introduction to a stranger. The shouting was this time because it was a loud bar, and he was trying to shake off the evening. “Not like…well y, you know. But also like a gathering of crows!”

“Uh huh,” the girl wasn’t paying him any attention. “That’s cool!” She sipped the drink and scanned the dancefloor, looking for someone…anyone other than the guy that was yammering on about the spelling of his name and buying buildings.

“I’m a realtor!” David was trying desperately to earn her attention. “I‘ve bought and sold a lot of the southwest revitalization project! The tax breaks were good, but they missed the potential there!”

“Thanks for the drink!” The girl kissed the air in David’s general direction – her breasts jiggling toward David, his eyes locking in on the enticing display – and slipped away into the crowd of the deafening bar.

“And I kill people to make money!” Honesty came easiest for David when he knew no one was listening. Pounding his vodka tonic back, he sucked his teeth, disgusted at the rebuke he’d just suffered by the dumb bimbo now laughing and getting groped by some filthy fuck boy on the dancefloor.

He flicked his wrist it was 10:45 pm. Too early.

But David was getting too old for these numbers, especially with the body parts currently duct taped in plastic and twice bagged and dismembered in suitcases in the trunk of his Tesla. They’d wait until morning, but it’s a long drive to the desert; to make the overnight deposit before dawn.

Slapping the empty glass on the bar, his hand whipped out a money clip and flicked out green bills. Two bills of some denomination got pinned to the bar with the empty glass in a grand satisfying gesture and an audible frat-boy grunt. He whipped around and headed toward the door.

Eyeing the blonde that had ignored him as he loomed on the periphery of the club toward the exit. He locked eyes with the bouncer at the door.

“Charly,” David cast a warm smile – starkly contrasting his demeanor a second ago. He got close enough to say aloud, “That big titty broad back there?”

“Yeah?! What about her!?” Charly looked and then answered into David’s ear.

“Give her the number for the VIP room next weekend.” David slapped Charly’s shoulder assuring and stabbed a wide knowing grin to punctuate the command.

“Oh…” Charly caught another look at the bouncing set of breasts and the laughing blonde gyrating to the beat of the music. “I got ya, Boss!”

And turning away and out the steel door, David dropped the fake grin, and an icy mood settled over him. His Tesla parked immediately outside the front door with a line of people waiting for Charly’s approval to enter the bar grunted and whined, wishing David wasn’t David, but a pile of drunk frat boys pouring out some at least a handful of them might stand a chance at getting in the bar.

“Dude, the guys in there are fucking old!” A girl tried to say quietly, but she misunderstood the carry of her voice.

“Old?!” David shot a piercing glance in the direction of the muttering. “Did I hear someone say Old over there?”

Quiet. An Eire mood settled over the scene. Even the rowdy bros toward the back of the line didn’t dare buck up against the authority asserted by David just then.

The beep-beep of David’s Tesla waking up from his presence broke the silence. The door popped open to reveal a black-on-black cockpit with clean red, green, and yellow lights like a fighter plane. The flat black of the outside carried clean straight lines of high gloss black-like pinstriped racing stripes at various angles and stretched over the body of the magnificent car. If Musk had sought to design sex into the steel of a mode of transportation, but David found a way to make you lust after the steel and the lighting within.

The crunch and tap of his Italian dress shoes sounded as David glided into the cockpit, and the door followed behind him automatically. A slow, quiet roll away followed as the line of people watched the blacked-out Tesla crawl down the street. The crowd was oblivious to the fact the very ground under their feet was owned fee simply, all by David and for many, many years.

The last glimpse before turning to the left was the license plate: M3RDR-INC.

David Merder felt little for anything, much anything living. But he saw value in one thing, power. Power over anything, but especially over things. Some things David played with were buildings, and some other playthings bled.

A psychopath, but one bred for this life. His role in society; is like an undertaker but for a particular, affluent clientele. Who also share his affinity for wealth and power and all the freedoms it allows the holder.

Beyond property and the power being a landlord brings its owner, but also the strength to hold onto the power one enjoys from it.

Here’ the price of a building for sale to the public is a sum beyond the means of many generations. Still, one can enter into the ownership group for a specific clientele, for a particular obligation, or a series of discrete acts.

There’s a reason the old money fears new money. High-value real estate is a cut-throat business where David Merder has carved himself a lucrative niche.

Act Two

His empire in doubt, Merder must toe a fine line not to reveal the hidden bloody world of ultra-rich killers and demonists.

Merder and the Upstart engage in a battle of wits and power, with Merder using every dirty trick in the book to maintain his position.

As the conflict heats up, Merder begins to lose control and must confront his demons and the price of his power - before he is betrayed by a comrade in the trade, which opens the door to a new darker layer of this underworld.

Act Three

In a showdown, Merder and his rival face off; although David is wounded and weakened, he is victorious in the duel between him and the Upstart Antagonist.

Using every occult tool, war spell, and dark magic hex at their disposal to come out on top costs them both dearly - leading to the Upstarts' demise.

Wounded but victorious, the new reality of the battle invoked by his necromancy has cast a terrible prophecy to pivot toward coming true.

Can David prevent the fabric of space and time from unraveling? Or will his bloodlust destroy a world thinly veiled from ours?
