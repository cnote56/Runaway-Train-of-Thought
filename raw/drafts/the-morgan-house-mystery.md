I fell asleep one night….

A novel wondering the questions some of us ponder.

By Cole Dowden

JP Morgan.

I fell asleep one night. Woke up in the home of JP Morgan.

J.P. Morgan had several residences in New York. One of them was the
Morgan House, situated on the corner of 37th Street and Madison Avenue¹.
This house was one of the most impressive residences of its day, with
forty-five rooms, including twelve bathrooms¹. The Morgan family lived
in this house until Jack Morgan, J.P. Morgan’s son, died in 1943¹.

Another residence was a townhome on East 35th Street, which is now one
of the buildings in the Morgan Library². Additionally, J.P. Morgan owned
a carriage house on East 83rd St., which was built around 1907³. This
house featured a hidden pool underneath the kitchen’s oak floor³.

While there are no specific accounts of J.P. Morgan’s daily life at
home, we can imagine that he would have spent his time managing his
business affairs, entertaining guests, and enjoying the comforts of his
luxurious homes. As one of the most influential financiers in history,
his days would have been filled with meetings, correspondence, and
decision-making. However, he also likely enjoyed quieter moments of
relaxation and contemplation in the privacy of his residences.

I poured his throat out over his breakfast with a swift swipe of a razor
I pulled from his bathroom down the hall.

Then I watched his eyes streak for someone’s help. He only found my
eyes. Watching him shed his only value…liters of blood soaked the line
and silk he fed every day over.

The food spoiled with his blood, not laid speckled and ark with the last
of JP Morgan's worthless soul.

I dosed off in the wine cellar after enjoying a few bottles to wash my
hands.

I awoke again in my timeline. Hungry and still satisfied.
