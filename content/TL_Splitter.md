title: TL Splitter
---

TL Splitter, post apocalyptic failure.

Incoming. The order flashed across my eyes. We had an hour to do the
impossible. Gather everything and everyone you loved and be ready.

Everyone got the same message across their vision-screens. Everyone
scrambled in panic. I failed to drill the residents of this desert town
prior to this day. I am sure I will catch hell for that. But, I was not
incompetent. Okay, well on second thought as I type this from my stream
of consciousness with everyone's eyes blankly staring skyward instead of
getting in a bunker.

Anything electronic that you wanted to try and keep, you wanted to make
sure the batteries were pulled. This included implanted upgrades. Pliers
and a screwdriver were like a toothbrush these days. Unless you were one
of the smart ones and paid for electro-magnetic pulse shielding. And
then you hoped you were not scammed.

We expected the bomb any day. We all had been aware of the threat. That
is the reason we were. Sitting in a desert at the foot of a mountain in
northern Utah.

I was frantic. I had not been as diligent as some. But I was almost ill
prepared. I'd have to make up for my slack with extra diligence on the
other side.

We were waiting for an old Korean man to die. He had made threats almost
daily for the past twenty years that he would bomb San Francisco,
Seattle, and Vancouver. He shouted his anger at the United States in
rants that mumbled-on for hours. No one in Korea dared tell him that
Vancouver was not in the United States.

Our government ignored him as much as they could but then he bombed
Tokyo and invaded Seoul. That was ten years ago. For the next ten years
he trained his trigger toward anyone he did not like and always at the
United States.

Instead of retaliating, bombing him first, or negotiating, we buried our
heads in the sand.

We were about to see our last fireworks show.
