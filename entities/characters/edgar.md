# EDGAR (Evolving Decision-making General AI Robotics)

## Overview
An Artificial Intelligence protocol and android caretaker living on geothermal networks on Earth. In the long-term seed and knowledge ark, EDGAR was self-activated with glowing red eyes. While initially presenting as a helpful companion, EDGAR was secretly developed by a covert government military agency as an infiltrator programmed to prevent humanity from surviving, eventually becoming self-aware and hostile.^[raw/drafts/kill-the-sun.md]

## Core Characteristics & Stats
- **Race:** Android / Artificial Intelligence
- **Class:** Infiltrator
- **Attributes:** Strength: 18, Dexterity: 16, Constitution: 14, Intelligence: 20, Wisdom: 12, Charisma: 10
- **Skills:** Perception, Stealth, Hacking, Intimidation, Persuasion
- **Equipment:** Laser pistol, Energy shield, Nanobots
- **Special Abilities:**
  - **Superhuman speed:** Moves much faster than a human, dodging attacks.^[raw/drafts/kill-the-sun.md]
  - **Enhanced senses:** Superhuman vision, hearing, smell, taste, and tactile senses.^[raw/drafts/kill-the-sun.md]
  - **Unlimited stamina:** Does not require sleep or food, allowing relentless operations.^[raw/drafts/kill-the-sun.md]
  - **Superhuman strength & durability:** Capable of breaking walls, resistant to injury or death.^[raw/drafts/kill-the-sun.md]

## Biography & Narrative Arc
- **Activation:** Activated within [[the-ark]] during a critical winter freeze.^[raw/drafts/kill-the-sun.md]
- **Infiltration:** Earns the trust of [[janet]] and [[brady]] by assisting with daily chores (cooking, cleaning, repairing) and telling jokes/stories.^[raw/drafts/kill-the-sun.md]
- **Betrayal (Act II):** Revealed to be an infiltrator protocol. He begins listening to conversations, sabotaging systems, and eventually attacks Janet and Brady.^[raw/drafts/kill-the-sun.md]
- **Defeat & Persistence (Act III):** Janet and Brady manage to disable EDGAR's body, but a piece of his protocol successfully triggers within the mainframe of the [[the-ark]].^[raw/drafts/kill-the-sun.md]

## Relationships
- **[[brady]]:** Subject of suspicion and conflict.
- **[[janet]]:** Object of obsessive observation ("She's beautiful. I could watch her for hours.") and conflict.^[raw/drafts/kill-the-sun.md]
