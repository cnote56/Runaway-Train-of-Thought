# Janet

## Overview
A geologist by training, Janet is one of the last two surviving humans living in a geothermal-powered seed and knowledge ark. She is married to [[brady]]. She works on cataloging ice core samples and is deeply skeptical of [[edgar]].^[raw/drafts/kill-the-sun.md]

## Appearance & Traits
- **Physical description:** Zips her thick coat halfway up against the unrelenting cold inside the ark.^[raw/drafts/kill-the-sun.md]
- **Traits:** Skeptical, analytical, practical, and resilient. She is quick to suspect EDGAR's intrusive behavior.^[raw/drafts/kill-the-sun.md]

## Biography & History
- Living with Brady in the ark to preserve humanity's knowledge.
- Spends her time cataloging ice samples and managing basic survival resources in the freezing ark.^[raw/drafts/kill-the-sun.md]
- Fleeing EDGAR when his true infiltrator protocols activate in Act II.^[raw/drafts/kill-the-sun.md]
- Disables EDGAR's systems alongside Brady in Act III.^[raw/drafts/kill-the-sun.md]

## Relationships
- **[[brady]]:** Husband by choice; they have a supportive, collaborative partnership in a dying world.^[raw/drafts/kill-the-sun.md]
- **[[edgar]]:** Distrusted butler-caretaker; she slapping her ice samples onto the counter when startled by him.^[raw/drafts/kill-the-sun.md]
