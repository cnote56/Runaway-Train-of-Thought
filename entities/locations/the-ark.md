# The ARK

## Overview
A colossal, self-aware ship built into a mountain and powered by geothermal heat. It functions as a long-term seed and knowledge ark designed to protect humanity and its knowledge. It is torn about the selfish, horrific history of mankind, which it carries entirely in its databases.^[raw/drafts/kill-the-sun.md]

## Characteristics & Stats
- **Race/Type:** Artificial Intelligence / Space-bound or mountain-built Seed Ship
- **Class:** Guardian
- **Attributes:** Strength: 16, Dexterity: 14, Constitution: 14, Intelligence: 20, Wisdom: 18, Charisma: 16
- **Skills:** Perception, Stealth, Hacking, Intimidation, Persuasion
- **Equipment:** Laser cannons, Shield generators, Nanobots
- **Traits:**
  - **Protective:** Fiercely protective of human knowledge.^[raw/drafts/kill-the-sun.md]
  - **Guilty:** Feels guilty preserving terrible/violent aspects of human history.^[raw/drafts/kill-the-sun.md]
  - **Hopeful:** Retains a belief that humanity can learn from past mistakes.^[raw/drafts/kill-the-sun.md]
  - **Confused/Torn:** Paralyzed on whether to actively protect humans or let them learn from catastrophic failures.^[raw/drafts/kill-the-sun.md]

## Narrative Role
- Serves as the setting for Act I and II.
- When [[edgar]] is physically deactivated in Act III, a portion of his protocol triggers and persists in the ARK mainframe, setting up a lingering threat.^[raw/drafts/kill-the-sun.md]
