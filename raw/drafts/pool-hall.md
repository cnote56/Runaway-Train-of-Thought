The Pool Hall.
By William C. Dowden III

      I felt like Dostoyevsky writing my own “Notes From the Underground”
for all those years of my life.
      Here it was culminating, all right here right now! It was a smoky pool
hall hidden somewhere in the world. These few souls had found their way to
it, like me, to drink and smoke away the demons even if for only a few
hours till the beer wore off. She was a friend. At least that is what I was
to her. I’ve always had a difficulty with communicating with them, my
friends. I always laced my words with a joke to lighten the viciousness
that I truly intended.
      I loved to rattle off verbal atrocities–inexcusable jokes about Jews,
Blacks, unborn babies and even women as I looked deep into their minority
eyes. I was looking for that change in their eyes the moment when the tint
or the glaze would gloss, stiffen and glow with the same hate that savagely
fueled my words. It was like I wanted to be misunderstood. The differences
between them and me. That is all that mattered when others looked at our
party. Are we so blind and stupid that we can’t realize our differences and
allow them to divide us against ourselves?
      I envisioned my self laid naked on the alter and exhumed while still
living so I could describe what made me tick.
      “Yes, yes this is my liver. It’s the one organ I continually abuse if
not for my pleasure then for yours. Next my lungs. It’s from here where the
energy comes to scream at you with such violence that I do. Oh yes, the
stomach my prized possession look you see I lied it’s not cast iron in fact
it’s full of holes after all those troubling times I stayed awake at night
feverishly cursing all the things done wrong to and by me. What’s that you
say? A heart? What heart? Why should I show you my heart? Do you deserve to
see it’s hardened state? Could you understand why I keep it locked not in
my chest but in a safer place? No, I still have it even thought it has been
stolen so many times over the years. But I fought like hell to get it back
piece by piece, from all you deceivers and liars and toilers alike. All the
revolutions in the mindscape and under the running boards; no you people
will not see my heart. I’d rather eat it my self without you cruel fools
ready at the table with fork and knife in hand.”
      I would rehearse great plays of the evening’s events in my head.
Puffing up my will and id to let loose upon some poor creature all my
insanity, all my wildness, all my humanity. So I could sleep late the next
day and continue on as a sober man.
      I’d like to be considered wise, someday. So I read books as they call
to me from the dusty shelf. This week it’s Dostoyevsky. Next some other
lost soul. No I’m not reading to better understand this human condition.
I’m writing for that. I read the works to pause my brain from shaking-yes
shaking-to it’s very foundation, to my neck. You see my heart has never
been to good at the workings of life. My brain has always had to
compensate. Now that my heart tends to make all the wrong decisions my
brain tends to strike and picket when the moment are most heated. But I
love the argument. I love the three o’clock arguments that ones mind can
only have with ones soul, not while laying in the gentle abyss of some
lavish dream. But, rather in the open and debating court of a room fully
lit – burning hot on a summer’s eve. A man taking center stage with a
single attendee of a personal riot cursing even the air for making me
breathe it… so damn much.  The sleep would only weigh heavy on my mind when
my debate would be at it’s most potent. When my mind would calm enough for
my insomnia to loosen its grip. My eyes would grow heavy and my sight dim.
I would yawn. And my breathing would slow. The perspiration on my chest and
back would actually cool me almost to point of being comfortable. I would
cease fire the debates oppression that I labored under only to allow myself
the rest of sleep. But, like a thunderclap, after all my energies had
cooled and seemed dormant, my rage would return in the sense of some poetic
phrase that would spit itself from my lips and curse a sheet of paper not
unlike this one.
      I refused to spit out my statements like some half chewed meat and
wait for the rebuttal or conformation. This is why I never really took to a
drunken rant with a cigarette in one hand and drink in the other. Only to
display myself like some show for some other depraved soul. I am not
entertainment. Famous last words of an entertainer.
      My actions and words all scripted and merely a puppet then while
whoring my humanity for you! Give me strength. I speak these words on the
face of my character, lord whomever you maybe give me strength. It’s for my
self I write these words. I think in my own device of creativity. I have no
divinity in the explanation of the world I see. I am not above the words I
use-the ideas I think or the actions I describe. The world but a stage and
I a single player.
      I believe in the common man, if a common man were in this liberal
world attacking hi character and his life. His family disturbed to some
strange portrait not even including his face. The wife he once knew and
loved now lost on a dream of knowing herself better and “experiencing
life.” Her excursion to find the life that some book, dream, or marketing
agenda gave her credence to a new world that does not tolerate the common
manhood, because lies are all that’s to be believed when the truth is
silent.
      The real world where…
            …His daughter hate him for what he can only be…her father.
      The real world where…
            …His son doesn’t know him because of all the mud in the eyes of
the youth.
      Both believing the communistic new lie that if only the “man” would
die then the whole world could be a paradise. They do not know that the
“man” is the lie and not their father that they wish to die.
      Nothing can repair what has been done. No amount of tort justice can
keep the pain from being revisited every time the mind transverses the
crevices of remembrance. But the popular pastime is to better understand
and accept the occupations that have occurred to the occludes. To charge
out at the bleak surroundings that corners us with every new understanding
of our feelings. Is this to be our emotion ruled reality or is this to be
the moment when the smoke chokes at our throats and wreck the broken
machine that is causing all this disbelief and torture to us? I’m speaking
of the bleeding half dead animal that is this country. This is the nation
once great and powerful enough to warrant the accreditation of the greatest
nation under god. How bleak are these days that we question is this still
that nation. This is still the American soil my grandfather built homes,
banks, offices, and schools on. America is still in my heart. My father
placed it there every time he spoke of work, love and family. I saw those
tears in his eyes and now they’ve seeded in mine. This is my America. Yes I
call it my own. I do so because I alone care for it by giving a damn when
talking heads flap their well-oiled jaws and spit on the flag, constitution
and bill of rights. That’s my constitution. Every word rings true. Those
are my rights how ever old fashioned they may be. I write these words for
the philosophy of me. Dedicated them all in the heart of true reality.
                   But all that is suspect to inspection.
      The fact may be, that I am a crazy-angry-white man. But I am the one
dying in the fields, colorblind in respect of skin to my brothers. And to
the lost souls in this room even this woman on my lap. She’s an angel. How
fallen from the highest seat. She’s but an angel amidst the blue red neon
lights. Amongst my brothers few, trying to only lighten our shoulders from
this workers paradise. The new creeping socialism I see. It’s camouflaging
itself in the anti-Marxist rhetoric of modern mans plight. Out there they
scream when economies go south but it was them that signed those jobs away.
“Giving peace a chance.” They shout when war drums begin in the chorus.
They will plead screaming not to risk the death sentence of sacrifice to be
visited on their sons/daughters. But they forget wars have to be fought
before that bloody tide reaches our fair soil.
      The real story.
      What is this place?
      What’s the score?
      It’s not just some pool hall. It’s an event. It’s a title fight. Round
two. The introductions and pleasantries are long gone. The blood has yet to
be spilt but that is more in align with the happenings of round four or
five.
      Now its time for the real gauging to begin, the point in the evening
for the two combatants-her and me-to begin the dance. I’m speaking of the
dancing that only one can understand when it’s been a month since a real
title fight. There is a state, an effect, where the softness takes over
when your mind has been relaxed a little too much. She’s dancing around
ducking the issue the fist and the hammer, only proverbially not really in
the literal sense.
      This woman sitting on my lap, her blonde hair finding it’s way into my
face, there is a sweet scent to it. What ever it is or what ever it could
be. It’s like a drug and it’s in my veins cause she’s still sitting on my
lap, oozing its way though my skin and poisoning me. Flirting has never
been my strongest suit to wear. But I was enjoying the pain of her weight
on me, the gentle pressure of skin as she pushed closer to me. Above the
smoke and scent of the pool hall–I could still smell her hair and the aroma
of womankind. That’s the uppercut, right on the square of the chin. It
burns all the way into the brain when it hits you and you’ll believe you
just got your head taken off before you realize that it is still attached.
Your dizzy from the pain, but you got to keep you head on straight so you
shrug like it isn’t anything special, and your still smelling that scent in
your nose and its running a race deep into your brain.
       Now it’s her shot…on the table. Now I refuse to expound on the subtle
nuances of the pool shot. Although if there could be a simpler way to knock
a ball into a hole then there is not a sacred cross to bear nor is anyone
really listening right now. The pool shot is a misunderstood metaphor…think
of it as a heart attack but only it is not bad. In fact you’re trying to
suffer a cardiac arrest that is the whole point. The aphrension, the slow
decay into the impact and the wait! The wait, a slow collapse of time, till
you see if your ball drops where you shot it.
      But a pool shot is only a pool shot, a violent gentle act that sends
something- somewhere. All cause and effect gives of all it’s own cause and
effects. But the pool shot is only a metaphor.
      Forget the look in the eye…it means nothing to the one that saw it.
It’s only the aftermath - the whitewash – the noise. The look in the eyes
after the shot’s been made can’t tell you a goddamn thing. Don’t trust it.
Don’t try to guess its philosophy. Cause its all wrong.
      Now the walk back from the pool table…can mean many things but none of
these mean anything. She can confuse you with clever mouthings of the word
to a song that’s being played. This reminds you of the sex smell and
thoughts that you had just previous to the game played.  But that’s only a
drug. Don’t trust it cause there’s nothing in there but a song that is
played right at the moment that makes you believe it’s your turn to make
your shot… but it isn’t.
      You see she’s just walking back to hand you the cue. Don’t hallucinate
yet…I know …I know it’s a rush. You see it not everyday that the cue
actually gets pushed into your hand but after she’s given you the cue you
got to find your shot.
      Line up the white ball.
      Remember your geometry.
      Cause the pool hall isn’t always going to be open for you to practice
your shot.
      Now a poor suffering fool, I am and you are. We are stuck here in this
sufferable pleasure place. We are waiting for the trouble to leave us and
the night to begin with the ending we are dreaming for. I talk of not this
pool game or the turmoil of the shot we take, but rather the fruition of
this excursion. Perhaps it will be a simple kiss? Perhaps the sun warming a
naked leg motionless in a bed? Is this the end of the dreaming and the
beginning of the existing? Or ware we still and quiet while another reaches
for that prize and wins the day. What are we fools waiting for the gray to
grow thick on our chins or the strength to leave us for some other wild
buck?
      I do not pretend to know what this shot will bring. Perhaps the ball
will drop where I please. Perhaps I will fault and grin at the evidence of
my impotence. Either way at least I am shooting.
      Enjoy the pool hall. Enjoy the rooms where the last legal sins are
praised and cherished, because life is too short for second thought and
bridge burning.
