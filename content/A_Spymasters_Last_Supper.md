A Spymasters Last Supper

Synopsis: a spy ring is winding down its operations in a country of the
past 15 years. The chief ring leader is congratulated and rewarded with
one final mission, killing his team members.

The spy ring meets at the Spymasters house for their final debriefing.
They do know this is their final mission...to die.

One of the spies realises the purpose of the evening and tries to stop
the Spymasters last mission. Will he succeed?

The story unfolds with a look back on the rings’ missions as told
through the dinner prep and evening with a country devouring itself in
turmoil and strife - all the outcome of the spyrings success.

Will anyone survive the Spymasters Last Supper?

Chapter notes.

A candidate for governor suddenly dies of cardiac arrest. He was healthy
and with no know health problems but his death throws the political race
I to turmoil.

Then a congressman has a stroke after attending the funeral, his ability
to retain his seat is questioned and the turmoil spreads from the state
races to the midterms.

The cardiac arrest was by use of a special nerve toxin tailored to the
victim.

The stroke was caused by a similar toxin designed to affect the
congressman and based on his dna.

Both were part of the spy rings mission to disrupt the elections for
that state.
