# Terson

## Overview
Terson is a powerful King within the offices and orders of spirits, commanding a vast army of 72 legions. He is an omniscient entity who holds absolute knowledge over the past, present, and future, and is a primary guardian of hidden treasures, which he willingly reveals to operators who follow his specific sacrificial protocols.^[[raw/drafts/mythos-and-magic-backup-repository.md]]

## Appearance & Traits
- **The Lion King:** Appears in the likeness of a man but with the fierce, majestic face of a lion.^[[raw/drafts/mythos-and-magic-backup-repository.md]]
- **Royal Court:** Preceded by a grand court of 40 musicians playing trumpets and other instruments.^[[raw/drafts/mythos-and-magic-backup-repository.md]]
- **Sacrificial Demands:** Demands a physical sacrifice in the specific form of a brazen (bronze) image.^[[raw/drafts/mythos-and-magic-backup-repository.md]]

## Biography & History
- **The Omniscient Treasure Holder:** Commands 72 legions of spirits. His portfolio is centered on economic retrieval and temporal omniscience. He is noted within the techno-occult protocols as an essential agent in Kabbalistic server siege operations.^[[raw/drafts/mythos-and-magic-backup-repository.md]]

## Relationships
- **Infernal Hierarchy:** Operates as an independent King under the general Necromantic umbrella, highly sought after by operators seeking hidden data vaults (treasures).^[[raw/drafts/mythos-and-magic-backup-repository.md]]\n
