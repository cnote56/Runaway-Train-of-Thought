# Test Story Draft

Once upon a time, in the distant digital space of the Creative Wiki, there was a background watcher process that monitored the inbox directory for incoming documents.
It was a loyal and diligent process, sleeping for only two seconds at a time.
One day, a new test draft appeared.
"Aha!" said the watcher. "A new document has arrived!"
And so it processed it cleanly.
