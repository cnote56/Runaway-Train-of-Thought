[]

The year is 2042. The world is in the grip of a deadly virus that has no
known cure. The virus is spread through the internet, and it is killing
people at an alarming rate.

The only hope for humanity lies in the hands of a group of hackers who
are trying to find a way to stop the virus. They have discovered that
the virus was created by a powerful witch who was buried centuries ago.

The hackers believe that if they can find the witch's grave and drill
through it, they will be able to stop the virus. But they soon realize
that they are not alone in the cemetery.

The witch's spirit is guarding her grave, and she is not happy that the
hackers are there. She attacks them, and they are forced to flee.

The hackers know that they cannot defeat the witch on their own. They
need to find a way to weaken her. They decide to use the internet to
their advantage.

They hack into the witch's computer and upload a virus that will weaken
her. The witch is furious, and she attacks the hackers again.

This time, the hackers are ready for her. They have developed a new type
of virus that will destroy her. They upload the virus into the witch's
computer, and she is destroyed.

The virus is stopped, and humanity is saved. But the hackers know that
the witch's spirit is still out there, and she will be back.

The next time she comes, she will be stronger than ever. And the hackers
will be ready for her.
