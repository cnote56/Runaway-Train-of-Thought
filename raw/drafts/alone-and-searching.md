Fresh drippings.


It’s a little after midnight and I am alone.
No real difference in my situation here.
There has been no real revolution in the air about me.
I’m simply alone in my solitude.
I’m tired.
I’m tired of being so tired.
Why can’t I find that one?
That one gem to call my own.
I’m not asking for much.
I’m not asking for the world on a platter.
I’m not asking for anything that doesn’t want to be had.
I’m not asking for much.
Just to have that one.

Why? Why? Why?

I’m not asking any new questions here.
I’m not breaking any new ground.
I’d just like to know… why?
Life’s a good cook off in my eyes, to see what falls to the flames and what
clings to the bone.
And in the end everything is consumed.
Everything is a mistake for the one with no one to tell them otherwise.
It’s just not right.
This light that I am sitting in.

Goodnight…goodnight.
