---
title: Remembered
---

I don't remember getting out of the MRI machine. I remember being int eh
damn thing, but I don't remember getting out of it. I've had an MRI
before. I've put my clothes in a locker and put on the little gown and
getting shoved into the machine. the knocking and beeps and claxons. But
I don't remember getting out of it.

I don't remember the technician telling me it was done and the doctor
will have the report in a few days. I don't even remember putting back
on my clothes from before.

I just remember being in the dressing room - fully dressed. I walked
out, all the signs said no need to talk to anyone at the front desk.

I barely remember driving my car home.

I don't remember putting the key in the lock. I always remember the key
in the lock.

I don't remember the alarm. I always turn the alarm off.

I always turn the alarm off.

I do, however, remember when my wife finally came home that evening. She
was in tears and ignored me the whole night.

Then I remembered the wake.

And the tears.

Then I remembered being dead.
