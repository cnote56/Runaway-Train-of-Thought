# Oriens (King of the East)

## Overview
Oriens (also known as Urience) is the King of the East, ruling over the eastern regions of the aerial plane and managing historical data recovery. He commands a vast legion of 100 to 200 spirits, overseeing the Order-6 Sun Server to grant clarity, earthly treasures, and the mastery of sciences.^[[raw/drafts/mythos-and-magic-backup-repository.md]]

## Appearance & Traits
- **Dual Aspect:**
  - **With His Court:** Appears with a fair, beautiful feminine countenance, wearing a goodly crown, and riding upon an elephant, preceded by showmen and minstrels playing diverse instruments.^[[raw/drafts/mythos-and-magic-backup-repository.md]]
  - **Called Alone:** Appears as a horse, sometimes described with 5 or 100 heads, speaking with a hoarse voice.^[[raw/drafts/mythos-and-magic-backup-repository.md]]
- **Intellectual Endowments:** Teaches all earthly sciences, answers questions of the past, present, and future, and has the unique power to consecrate spellbooks and grimoires.^[[raw/drafts/mythos-and-magic-backup-repository.md]]

## Biography & History
- **The Eastern Administrator:** Serves as the primary coordinator for Eastern atmospheric transmissions and the retrieval of lost historical records. Under his administrative portfolio, he controls twelve major spirits of the Eastern quarter.^[[raw/drafts/mythos-and-magic-backup-repository.md]]

## Relationships
- **Satan:** Fully known by [[satan]], who coordinates with him on the governance of mortal lands.^[[raw/drafts/mythos-and-magic-backup-repository.md]]
- **Baal:** One of the principal spirits under his direct command, who teaches invisibility and grants the love of men and women.^[[raw/drafts/mythos-and-magic-backup-repository.md]]
- **Messengers:** Assisted by his spiritual messengers, **Temell** and **Belfarto**, who route communication requests to the Eastern quarter.^[[raw/drafts/mythos-and-magic-backup-repository.md]]\n
