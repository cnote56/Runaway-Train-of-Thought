# Solar Fission Project

## Overview
A geoengineering initiative proposed in the early 2010s by meteorologists and planetary scientists from Harvard and Yale. Hypothesized as a solution to global warming, the project explored controlling the nuclear fission of the sun to "turn down the heat."^[raw/drafts/kill-the-sun.md]

## Outcomes & Legacy
- **The Great Climate Shift:** The attempts to regulate solar output led to an unintended catastrophic failure. Rather than cooling the planet safely, it triggered a wild, fifty-year global blizzard, dropping temperatures below combustion engine thresholds and forcing the remaining humans into geothermal-powered refuges like [[the-ark]].^[raw/drafts/kill-the-sun.md]
- **The Eternal Winter:** Features unrelenting cycles of blizzards, minor spring-like thaws, and 30-degree daily temperature shifts.^[raw/drafts/kill-the-sun.md]
