# Dobly

The Harp Keeper. A select few individual capable of hearing the frequency intonations of [[the-harp]].

## Overview
Dobly spent his life toiling in the belly of [[the-harp]]. He became aware of the "apocalypse" and systemic control being enforced through his machine.

## Actions
- Sabotaged [[the-harp]] by allowing it to fall out of balance, triggering a chain reaction that destroyed orbiting Arched Metaliod Ships.
- Committed suicide via a glowing green pill, effectively ending the oversight cycle he was forced to maintain.

## Narrative Significance
Dobly represents the [[humanist]] voice: a soul trapped in a clinical machine that chooses rebellion over complicity.
