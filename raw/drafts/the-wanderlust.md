      Henry sat on the floor with scraps and sheets of paper scattered
around like debris. He looked like a beaten hungry dog to weak to eat and
to frightened to move as he looked at her.
      Christina wasn’t doing much just parading around the apartment in her
cream colored silk slip. Her skin rubbed under the fine silk as her
movements rippled over her smooth skin with each step. She was cleaning the
kitchen and how simple that sounds the sight of it cored poor Henry as if
every step she took was a scoop of him being exhumed from his gut.
      Christina didn’t even realize the horrible yet lovely effect she was
having on Henry.
