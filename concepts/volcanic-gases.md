# Volcanic Gases (Mount Mazama Cataclysm)

## Overview
A detailed historical scientific ledger of toxic gases emitted during the eruption of Mount Mazama 7,700 years ago (forming Crater Lake). It details the gases' compositions, toxic effects, and role in flash-boiling and suffocating regional wildlife.^[raw/drafts/kill-the-sun.md]

## Gas Inventory
- **Water Vapor & Steam:** Flash-boiled wildlife.^[raw/drafts/kill-the-sun.md]
- **Carbon Dioxide (CO2):** Odorless, invisible death that pooled in crevasses and caves.^[raw/drafts/kill-the-sun.md]
- **Sulfur Dioxide (SO2):** Pungent, breathing hazard, and source of acid rain.^[raw/drafts/kill-the-sun.md]
- **Hydrogen Sulfide (H2S):** Rotten egg odor, highly corrosive, lethal at low exposure.^[raw/drafts/kill-the-sun.md]
- **Hydrochloric Acid (HCl):** Formed via water-gas interactions; corrosive to lungs.^[raw/drafts/kill-the-sun.md]
- **Hydrogen Fluoride (HF):** Highly corrosive; damages lungs and bones.^[raw/drafts/kill-the-sun.md]
- **Carbon Monoxide (CO):** Odorless gas preventing oxygen absorption.^[raw/drafts/kill-the-sun.md]
- **Sulfuric Acid Aerosols:** Corrosive droplets damaging eyes and skin.^[raw/drafts/kill-the-sun.md]
