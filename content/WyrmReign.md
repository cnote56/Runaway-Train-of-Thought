WyrmReign

A space-borne parasite joined our ecosystem—an alien. A cast-off from a
mass of horrible we have yet to imagine.

The invasion took hours - silent and effective. Many had not woken up
yet, but some were awake enough to resist the worms as they took over
the sleepers.

A few moments before the invasion began.

The coiled lifeform held tightly around a landing gear strut tucked into
the shell of the reentry rocket. Its form then was a boiled and broken
refugee cast off from the crash and destruction of another planet that
found its way to the surface of the Earth’s moon. It languished there,
slowly dying in the harsh burn of the sun’s endless shine. Resigned to
perish, but for a flicker of light glistening nearby. This poor jumble
of life, composited with rock and debris, was damaged and nearly dead.

It sensed the pulse in the moving mass nearby. The creak of the tank
tread slapped and swung the gloopy mass aboard the exposed anti-drone
explosive framing and cut across the drilling sunlight. Suddenly, the
wounded mass of life slapped toward the relief in the shadow of the
vehicle rolling by.

The rocket atop the launch tower was shaking the final fuel bubbles from
its tanks.

Slapping from the rover's side, the lifeform is slowly healing from its
UV exposure and compelled toward the low rumble of the rocket engines
priming for takeoff. The engines now sparked just after the clinging
sludge and tendrils slapped to this side and tucked into the rocket's
metal skin folds.

Now inside the rocket's frame, the sludge glistened and twisted on
itself, regenerating and adapting to its environment as it pivoted
toward the blue Pacific Ocean below.

Once the return had the craft hovering over the landing pad, it sprang
outward in all directions. Riding the flames and thrust of the rocket,
the substance splayed toward anything organic. It found plenty of hosts.

The coils darted and struck like cobras to the necks and torsos of its
victims.

Alien tendrils struck and spread across the victims' bodies and arms.
Ultraviolet light spawned rainbows of colors instantly mutating in this
new world’s sunlight. Splayed out across the skin, the tendrils embedded
themselves into their hosts like ribbon worms. The hosts only felt a
moment of panic before the tendrils wrapped around their brains to
alleviate them of those pesky higher brain functions like self.

After a few hours that ached into days, the human species became meat
skins for the new dominant species on the latest battlefield: Earth.

###

Just as Pandora wept as the parade of horribles unleashed on the earth
there remains hope left unseen. The hope that drives into the core of
the last of the humans that remain. The ones that the Wyrms haven’t
taken.

TREATMENT AND STORY NOTES

Plot Ideas

Following the Discovery: A scientist, Dr. Elara Finch, who is a part of
the first team to encounter the organism in space, realizes the threat
too late. She races against time to find a solution to reverse the
effects of the parasite's takeover before they completely dominate the
human population.

Inside the Host: Switch the perspective to the perspective of one of the
hosts, a typical factory worker named Alex, who fights for consciousness
within his own body. At the same time, the parasite attempts to
manipulate his thoughts and actions.

The Last Stand: The story culminates in a climactic battle between the
remaining free humans and the full-blown Wyrm-controlled populace,
leading to a desperate plan to destroy the creatures and reclaim their
planet.

Survivor Clan: A small group of survivors who retain free will form a
clan in a remote part of the world, struggling to combat the Wyrms'
influence. They develop a ritual that involves deterring the parasite
using fire and light, believing it to be an ancient protective measure
against evil.

False Angel: As society crumbles under the invasion, a cult worships the
Wyrms, seeing them as divine beings. Some survivors are lured into
loyalty, complicating the efforts to resist the invasion.

 

Main Storyline - Following the Discovery

Beginning: Dr. Elara Finch heads an astrobiology team that discovers the
alien parasite aboard the rocket returning from the Moon. Ignoring
warning signs of the creature during their expedition, the scientists
are unaware of the impending doom until it's too late. The rocket lands
and the alien organism begins its silent invasion of the nearby
population.

Middle: As people start to disappear and exhibit zombie-like behaviors,
Dr. Finch races to figure out a way to reverse the effects of the
parasite. She experiences personal loss further motivating her to halt
the spread of the parasite. Together with a small band of uninfected
survivors, they embark on a journey through their decimated city to
unravel the mysteries of the organism while facing infected humans and
the challenges of survival.

Climax: The story builds tension as Dr. Finch discovers that the Wyrms
require a specific biological agent from living humans to sustain
themselves. In a final stand, the survivors set a trap for a group of
hosts, aiming to extract the antidote that may help save humanity from
complete ingestion.

Ending: They find that it only partially works while extracting the
antidote. The team decides to spread the agent across the remaining
regions to fight back the Wyrms. The story ends with Dr. Finch adjusting
to her new reality, knowing the battle isn’t over.

Summary: Elara’s journey from ignorance to awareness, inner conflicts,
and determination to save what remains of humanity pave an engaging
narrative arc.

Character Profiles

1. Dr. Elara Finch (Protagonist)

- A 35-year-old astrobiologist who has devoted her life to exploring
extraterrestrial life. Raised in a scientific family, she is deeply
passionate about her work.

- Driven by guilt for missing the signs during the initial expedition,
she wishes to save humanity and atone for her oversight.

- Intelligent, determined, often analytical to the point of being overly
cautious; struggles with leadership due to past failures.

2. Marcus Tane (Secondary Character)

- A former military police turned private contractor now providing
security for the research team. Skeptical of science when it crosses
into the unexplained.

- Motivated to protect humanity despite the overwhelming odds; has a
soft spot for Elara due to shared history in training.

- Stoic, protective, with a dry sense of humor; more action-oriented
than Elara’s scientific approach because of his military background.

3. Jasmine Reed (Secondary Character)

- A young technician enthusiastic about technology and robotics. She
aspires to use technology to improve life but faces the harsh realities
of her environment.

- Motivated to make a difference and prove her capability among her
peers.

- Idealistic, innovative, and optimistic; struggles with the creeping
despair around her.

4. Alexander "Alex" Pan (Secondary Character)

- A rocket maintenance technician caught in the chaos who becomes one of
the early hosts for the Wyrms. His struggle becomes a key subplot.

- Initially helpless, he seeks to regain control over his mind and body
as he becomes aware of what’s happening. He struggles with the wyrm and
its effects but learns to control it, which makes it a strength - he
suffers some split personality issues with the Wyrm’s voice in his mind.

- Witty, relatable, and morally complex as he grapples with the chaos
and his actions under parasitic influence.

Key Scenes Outline

Scene 1: Discovery in Space

- Setting: The rocket's interior as it's re-entering Earth’s atmosphere.

- Characters: Dr. Elara Finch and her team.

- Development: The excitement of discovery gives way to tension as
signals of an anomaly appear on monitors.

Scene 2: The Invasion Begins

- Setting: A small coastal town where the rocket lands.

- Characters: Residents and Dr. Finch's team.

- Development: The parasites infiltrate the town unnoticed, with eerie
signs of change as individuals start behaving erratically.

Scene 3: The First Host

- Setting: A dimly lit rocket maintenance and repair facility where Alex
works.

- Characters: Alex, coworkers.

- Development: The moment Alex becomes a host, showcasing his thoughts
and feelings as the Wyrms take control.

Scene 4: Allies Unite

- Setting: The remnants of the research facility.

- Characters: Elara, Marcus, Jasmine.

- Development: They assemble as survivors, vowing to combat the threat
and search for a solution.

Scene 5: Climax at the Factory

- Setting: Outside the factory, turned into a battleground.

- Characters: Elara, her team, and a group of hosts.

- Development: The desperate attempt to extract the antidote culminates
in high-stakes action.

Scene 6: Resolution at Dawn

- Setting: A hillside overlooking the sunrise radiating over the city.

- Characters: Elara and her team.

- Development: They reflect on their losses and victories, determined to
continue fighting, hinting at the ongoing struggle.

### Step 6: Full Story

**Title: Invasion of the Wyrms**

**Prologue**

The universe is littered with the remnants of ancient civilizations:
structures, once teeming with life, now husks on barren worlds. Each
cast-off organism tells a tale of desperation, survival, and ultimately,
extinction. Among these drifting echoes, a particular Wyrm
languishes—waiting.

**Chapter 1: The Discovery**

Dr. Elara Finch and her team aboard the Luna Explorer focus on their
return to Earth, oblivious to the silent horror hitching a ride,
clinging to the landing gear strut with tenacity. The rocket's descent
is smooth, yet an unsettling feeling lingers in the spaces between their
conversations—a sense of impending doom.

**Chapter 2: The First Signs**

Upon landing in a coastal town, Elara’s telemetry alerts them to strange
readings. As local residents greet the returning explorers with
excitement, paranoia spirals as media reports gloss over mysterious
disappearances. The Wyrms sense opportunity, the first victims veering
from perception into darkness.

**Chapter 3: Alex’s Suffering**

Meanwhile, Alex feels invaded; panic surges as his consciousness
collides with alien intent. Frustrated thoughts swirl, helpless against
the creeping tendrils infiltrating his mind. The Wyrms' control
supplants his identity, but a flicker of resistance remains.

**Chapter 4: Rallying the Uninfected**

Elara realizes the need for drastic measures. Aware of the horrifying
reality unfurling around her, she bands with Marcus and Jasmine. They
devise plans based on ancient symbols of light and fire that could
reverse or at least hinder the Wyrm's influence.

**Chapter 5: Battleground**

As panic escalates into chaos, humanity's remnants clash in desperation.
The group confronts the Wyrm-possessed populace at a renamed
factory—once a humble factory, now a warzone. Amid dynamic entropy, the
antidote becomes the center of their aims.

**Chapter 6: Redemption and Resilience**

In the aftermath of an arduous battle, wounds, both physical and
emotional, manifest. The remaining survivors will wear the scars of
their journey forever as they ponder humanity's fate. Yet, the antidote,
though trunked in success, is only partial; they are forced to expand
their efforts. Dr. Elara Finch knows that alight exists in the depths of
the dark. With defiance, they strategize—humanity’s resilience will rise
anew.

**Epilogue**

Days and weeks blur into one grim recognition; humanity stands teetering
on the brink. Yet, the glimmer of hope persists among the remaining
unaligned. Elara, with a determined rallying spirit, prepares to survive
and wage the ultimate war against the darkness threatening their home.

**Closing Thoughts**: In the fight against the sinister Wyrms, humanity
clings to remnants of individuality and unity, determined to explore
what it means to live and perhaps regain Earth as its own.
