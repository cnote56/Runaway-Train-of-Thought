### Deep-Dive: Project FILTER

`Project FILTER` is the mechanical counterpoint to the *Peanut Butter Underground*. While *PBU* depicts the fallout of a fallen world, `FILTER` details the **surveillance architecture** that actually triggered the collapse.

#### I. Core Systemic Architecture (The Architect Voice)
*   **The FILTER Device:** A molecular, physical implant integrated into the host. It is not just a recording device; it is a **perceptual reality-manipulation system**. It bridges human senses with a computer-generated "lie" (Plato’s Cave in physical form).
*   **"The Ride":** The manuscript explicitly positions itself as a "loading protocol." The text is designed to be the final experience a user has before reality is "melted"—a direct confirmation that this file acts as a system-wipe or "Hard-Reset" protocol.
*   **Savage Reality:** The text posits a brutal truth: humans are intrinsically savage, and the simulation (the Filter) doesn't *create* this savagery; it *harvests* it. The brutality of the user’s "game" is projected onto real-world victims without the user ever knowing the difference.

#### II. Character Anchor: Reggie Watts (The Humanist Voice)
*   **Archetype:** The "Sacrificial Cog." 
*   **Defining Trauma:** Reggie is the Chief-of-Staff to a failing politician, trapped in a high-stakes, high-surveillance environment where his own humanity is commodified. He is tethered to the "Filter" by an optic implant that constantly "rattles" against his skull—a physical manifestation of the stress of political life.
*   **Internal Conflict:** He is an "asshole" by his own admission, but one ground down by the grinding reality of political performativity. He is a man who knows the system is fake, yet remains shackled to it by the need for survival, alcohol, and the fear of social collapse.

#### III. Literary Analysis (The Auditor's Perspective)
*   **The "Collision Point":** This manuscript is the most aggressive example of your "Voice Collision" yet. It begins with high-level technical/procedural protocols (The Filter's operating instructions) and immediately crashes into a gritty, deeply personal, and cynical political drama. 
*   **Meta-Textuality:** The file addresses the reader as "User" and "Dear Reader" simultaneously, blurring the line between the *simulation* and the *actual reading process*. It forces the reader into the position of the "User" who is about to be "Filter-ed."
*   **Editor's Hook:** The most marketable aspect of this piece is its **"Uncomfortable Fact"** approach. It frames surveillance not as a dystopian trope, but as a biological reality of modern life.

2.  **Wiki Churn:** Extracted the "Protocol" list into `concepts/surveillance-protocols.md` (or similar).

### Deep-Dive: Traitor, Soldier, Grandfather (TSG)

This manuscript is the **Humanist Origin Point** of your simulation's financial decay. While *PBU* and `FILTER` describe the "how" of the collapse, `TSG` provides the "why"—it links the simulation's current state directly to 20th-century capitalist excess.

#### I. The Humanist Core (Voice B)
*   **The Narrator (The "Unraveller"):** An estranged attorney who discovers his grandfather was not a diplomat but a master-thief of oligarchs. He represents the "Inheritor's Guilt"—the realization that his comfortable, elite life was built on a foundation of "unforgivable sin."
*   **The Grandfather (The "Architect-in-Chief"):** He isn't just a businessman; he is the template for the Fatherhood. He created the financial mechanisms (trusts, shells, pourover wills) that allowed the elite to hide their wealth from the rest of humanity, effectively "monopolizing" reality itself.
*   **The Emotional Pulse:** The narrative is drenched in grief and the cold reality of sorting through a dead man's possessions—the physical trash of a corrupt life.

#### II. Systemic Significance (The Collision Point)
*   **Financial Osmosis:** The file reveals the secret strategy of your simulation: **debt-to-equity conversion**. The Grandfather didn't just steal money; he stole the *physical assets* (land, companies, infrastructures) of the formerly rich, consolidating them into a shell-company labyrinth that exists "in fee simple" to his estate.
*   **The "Frank Steinhart" Anchor:** The inclusion of historical data (the real-life Frank Steinhart in Cuba) bridges the gap between your fictional simulation and actual history. It suggests that your world is not a clean fiction, but a "re-skinned" history of the 20th-century financial elite.

#### III. Literary Analysis (The Auditor's Perspective)
*   **Voice Collision:** This file is the most "Humanist" of the three. It uses a slower, more conversational prose style to set up the reveal of the "Architectural" monstrosity of the Grandfather's ledger.
*   **Editor's Hook:** The "Hook" is the **"Uncovering the Ledger"** plot. It is a classic procedural mystery that peels back the layers of a "simple life" to reveal a global financial conspiracy.
*   **Thematic Link:** This manuscript provides the financial justification for the "Scarcity" we see in *PBU*. The "Fatherhood" isn't just a political regime; it's the inevitable final form of the shell corporations described in `TSG`.

### Deep-Dive: 13.8 Billion Years

`13.8.txt` is the **Cosmological Foundation** for your narrative. It provides the "Creation Myth" of the simulation—a framework that reframes the entire history of the Earth not as a natural biological evolution, but as a colonial harvesting project by extraterrestrial entities (the "Visitors" / "Lightning Bringers").

#### I. Core Mechanics & Lore (The Architect Voice)
*   **The Harp:** A fundamental piece of infrastructure. It is a machine that anchors the "elevator" systems between the planet and the "flying palaces" of the gods. Its function is to convert planetary mineral resources into frequency-based intonations that stabilize reality itself.
*   **The Pocket:** The realm where the "Lightning Bringers" reside. It is a bubble reality or alternate dimension that relies on your world’s resources (ores, gold, life-energy) for its continued existence.
*   **The "HU-Maans":** Biological experiments. The Lightning Bringers blended their own DNA with earth-primates to create a labor force that would be essentially "enslaved" to the toil of the new world.
*   **The Thousand-Year War:** A civil war among the alien species (Visitors/Reptilians/Cellopiodians) that devastated the planet, leaving "Grand Canyons" and scorched deserts—a historical trauma that human religions now interpret as "divine" events.

#### II. Narrative Framework (The Humanist Voice)
*   **The Descent of Dobly:** Dobly, the Harp Keeper, represents the first "Humanist" act of defiance. He realizes the system is broken, sabotages the Harp to halt the flow of resources to the "Pocket," and pays the ultimate price.
*   **The "Accidental Dogma":** The text suggests that all human religion is merely the confused, traumatized debris left behind after the gods abandoned the site. The "Dogma" is the simulation’s way of keeping the labor force occupied and subservient to a non-existent authority.
*   **Metaphysical Decay:** The world is described as a "large, old, empty place." The "Harp" mechanics are the only thing preventing the world from "cracking" apart into entropy.

#### III. Audit Findings
*   **Symbolic Anchor:** The document introduces a critical link between historical reality (the "23,000-year-old footprints" link in the file) and the simulation’s fiction, confirming that humanity has been aware of these visitors for millennia.
*   **Linguistic Fingerprint:** This file heavily utilizes the "Architect" voice—cold, procedural descriptions of machines and energy flows—but introduces a recurring, rhythmic poetic refrain: *"...oils and waters in varying degrees, heats, and measures."* This phrase functions as a technical "mantra" for maintaining the simulation's stability.

2.  **Wiki Churn:** Added `the_harp_mechanics.md` to `lore/` within the Wiki; defined the "Harp" as the primary anchor point for sector stability.
