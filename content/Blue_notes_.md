title: Blue notes_
---

The maddening sense of dissatisfaction courses through his veins.
Dissatisfaction in everything that this society uses to judge a person
for either their success or failure.

At this moment, he is his worst critic and the world feeds this
self-doubt.

And there is nothing to be done about it. The course of the empire has
dictated his role. Born at bat and facing nothing hittable. Facing the
brutal truth that there is nothing for but for what he is willing to
steal. And he is not willing to steal.

All of the success stories of his day and age are of their that had
somebl blinding flash if brilliance and then the cut throat decisiveness
to eat the dead and press forward. Oh and luck. Luck always helps.

But his family shield - even if he had one - wouldn't have Noble
patterns or animals. It would say in the plainest if truth, “if it's
broke and has shit on it; it is his.”

He's too far gone. Caught up in the trap and now racing for his
survival. And he's losing. Everyday he is a fraction behind. Who?
Himself and every hope he had before he realized what the system really
was.
