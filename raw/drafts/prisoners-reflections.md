Greetings,

I have decided, for lack of better judgment, to explain the reason’s for my
incarceration in this cage. I have sinned, like Cain I have slain my
neighbor. I know I have taken a life. I realize the facts realize what I
have done. Since I am currently here, in prison, I would like to convey my
experiences to you. I wish to tell you my side of the story, so you may
exercise your right to think. So please do not discard this as a pathetic
outcry for help or a man cleansing his soul. I am merely giving reality a
chance to clean its glasses…
