# The Peanut Butter Underground - Plot Outline

## Overview
This outline traces the narrative progression of *The Peanut Butter Underground*, a dystopian science fiction novel set in [[the-capital-city]] after a global conflict. The story chronicles [[tony]]'s transition from a hollowed-out reclamation worker to a central agent in a subterranean rebellion, and eventually to uncovering the deep history of [[the-fatherhood]]'s rise to power. ^[raw/drafts/the-peanut-butter-underground.md]

---

## Act I: Compliance and Survival (Book One)
- **Introduction (Dystopian Afternoon):** We are introduced to [[tony]], a limping reclamation laborer and three-war veteran, scraping a living in [[the-capital-city]] under [[the-fatherhood]]'s watchful [[bloxes]].
- **The Raid & Due Process:** A brutal crackdown on a residential block shows the terrifying efficiency of the standard [[bloxes]]. Dissenters are sprayed with noxious red liquid and dragged to re-education centers.
- **The Pedwalls:** Tony struggles to navigate the scheduled rising of physical walls slicing through intersections, barely avoiding detection by charging drones. He meets [[personati]], a gentle redhead who salvage art from fuel-destined books.
- **The Tunnels:** After oversleeping her state-assigned shift, Personati joins Tony in escaping to [[the-underground]]. They seek shelter within the damp sewage conduits and build a makeshift home. ^[raw/drafts/the-peanut-butter-underground.md]

---

## Act II: The Underground Takes Root
- **Lucinda's Store:** Tony is introduced to the feline merchant [[lucinda]] in her hidden shop, tasting her prized [[peanut-butter]] ("butter"), which provides instant relief for his chronic joint pain.
- **Feeding the Masses:** Willie pushes Tony into acting as a "runner" to buy out Lucinda's stock of butter. Tony and Personati travel through the tunnels to purchase supply crates to sustain the growing refugee population.
- **Tenuous Allegiance & Conspiracies:** The underground community expands. Tensions rise as Toro, Willie, and Tony coordinate logistics to avoid the expanding reach of the heavier bear-sized drones and magistrate police.
- **Coup & Duress:** Ground squads and specialized search-drones begin breaching the lower tunnels, forcing the underground rebels to scramble and defend their refuge. ^[raw/drafts/the-peanut-butter-underground.md]

---

## Act III: The Mad King and Sentient Archive (Book Two)
- **Reeducation Island:** Capture or siege forces some rebels onto a high-security penal colony where they face psychological fracturing and physical torture.
- **Washington, 1886 / 1899 (The Timeline):** Through historical records or flashbacks, the narrative shifts to reveal the origins of [[the-fatherhood]] in late 19th-century and 20th-century technocracy.
- **The Unmasking:** Tony stays out during a massive "Pacification Night," avoiding the drones and slipping into a hidden, deep subterranean vault.
- **The Sentient Archive:** Tony discovers that the digital Archive is alive and sentient. It reveals the real timeline: how the early planners and "Chaos Calcutists" engineered scarcity, weaponized agricultural land, and triggered endless global wars to force absolute human compliance.
- **The Endgame:** Armed with the true history, Tony, Personati, and the peanut-butter resistance prepare to strike at the heart of the Fatherhood's digital and physical chokeholds. ^[raw/drafts/the-peanut-butter-underground.md]
