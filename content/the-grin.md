Grin

It’s comforting to know
Uncanny to feel
And amazing to touch

From the scent
To the eyes that search
It’s the extremes that comfort me

I think to myself
It’s just a friendly grin
But there’s too much light to just be a grin

I’ll see it all
With a pair of imperfect eyes
That face that smile that mind

If it’s nothing more
Than just a grin
Let it be, let it be

But be a mere star in the sky
Let me see
Your grin again
