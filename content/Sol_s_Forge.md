A Galaxy’s Nursery

Earth is the most memorable and unique place in the known galaxy. It is
less so because it remains humanity’s home, but it is the only known
planet on which spontaneous life is possible.

The strangest anomalies exist only on this peculiar rock, three short
jumps from its sun.

See,
https://www.sciencealert.com/new-images-reveal-the-tiniest-known-life-forms-on-earth

Scientists have captured images of the tiniest known life forms on
Earth, putting to rest a decades-old debate over the existence of these
ultra-small bacteria. The bacteria have an average volume of 0.009 cubic
microns, making them several times smaller than previous estimates for
the lower size limit of life on Earth. These single-celled organisms
belong to one of three microbial species that have been difficult to
study.

Paragraph 2: The researchers captured these ultra-small bacteria by
filtering groundwater through successively smaller filters down to an
incredible 0.2 microns. The bacteria were then flash-frozen, and their
internal structures were captured using cryogenic transmission electron
microscopy. The researchers found that the bacteria were not starved or
inactive, as previously suggested, but were healthy, active, and
replicating themselves. However, much remains unknown about these
enigmatic creatures, including their function and their roles in
microbial communities and ecosystems.

In all of creation across the universe, only a few special places, such
