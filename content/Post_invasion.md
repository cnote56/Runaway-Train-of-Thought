---
title: Post invasion
---

I do not speak like my grandfather spoke.

I do not know the sound of his voice.

I do not the words he formed in his throat to expose the passion in his
heart.

My words now are formed in another’s.

I only know the harsh cuts and guttural trills alarming to old senses

The words I know are of a language imposed; they do not originate in me.

The ships pierced the sky long after they were seen barreling down from
the darkness of space. The battle lost the second their alien metal
touched the soil. our militaries lost in mere days darter which the
cities found themselves swallowed whole by the alien ships as they grew
and absorbed all the richness found here on our world.
