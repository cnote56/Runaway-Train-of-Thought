# Lucifer

## Overview
Lucifer is the supreme monarch of Hell, described as the "father of all Devils." He resides in the deepest depths of Hell and remains unreachable by direct conjuration. His authority is absolute, with all other spirits and demons offering him reverence, worship, and unwavering obedience.^[[raw/drafts/mythos-and-magic-backup-repository.md]]

## Appearance & Traits
- **Monarchical Majesty:** Possesses a supreme, dark majesty that commands absolute obedience from all of Hell’s hierarchy.^[[raw/drafts/mythos-and-magic-backup-repository.md]]
- **Inaccessibility:** Unsummonable by any mortal operator. He is bound to the deepest abyss of the infernal grid, representing the ultimate, uncoordinated counter-force.^[[raw/drafts/mythos-and-magic-backup-repository.md]]

## Biography & History
- **The Absolute Sovereign:** Established by divine ordinance as the ruler of the infernal legions. All devils, regardless of their rank or order, revere and worship Lucifer, obeying him with a "kind of majesty" ordained by God.^[[raw/drafts/mythos-and-magic-backup-repository.md]]
- **The Prime Minister's Service:** Governs hell through his appointed Prime Minister, [[prime-minister-of-hell-lucifuge-rofocale]], who acts as his terrestrial proxy and contract enforcer.^[[raw/drafts/mythos-and-magic-backup-repository.md]]

## Relationships
- **Lucifuge Rofocale:** Serves as Lucifer's "Prime Minister" and "General Counsel," managing the day-to-day administrative operations of Hell by Lucifer's direct order.^[[raw/drafts/mythos-and-magic-backup-repository.md]]
- **Infernal Trinity:** Reigns alongside [[beelzebub]] (Prince of Devils) and [[satan]] (The Great Tempter) as the peak of the Necromantic hierarchy.^[[raw/drafts/mythos-and-magic-backup-repository.md]]
- **The Four Kings:** Subservient to his ultimate will, with [[paymon-of-the-west]] noted as being the most obedient to Lucifer's command among all of the Air Kings.^[[raw/drafts/mythos-and-magic-backup-repository.md]]\n
