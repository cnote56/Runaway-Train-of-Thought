                                JUST A LETTER

I STARE ACROSS THE WAY A AT THING OF BEAUTY.
HER FORM SO SOFT AND WARM TO THE TOUCH.
IT CALLS TO MY EYES WHEN EVER SHE COMES.
TO RIP THEM AWAY IS A PAINFUL EXPERIENCE.
BUT IN THEIR PAIN ARE THEY MOST POWERFUL.
YOU CALL MY NAME IN MANY DIFFERENT TONES.
YOU SCREAM, YOU SPEAK SOFTY,   YOU CALL TO MY SOUL
YOUR THOUGHTS SO DEEP YOU DON’T REALIZE HOW SO.
AND WITH YOUR MOUTH YOU GIVE BIRTH TO THEM.
THEY CARESS MT EARS AND SOFTEN THE HARSHNESS,
IN THE SOUNDS ABOUT ME AND YOU.
OUR SPACE AND AREA ABOUT US SO UNCLEAN.
TO SCRUB AWAY THE DIRT OF THIS WORLD,
A BLASPHEMOUS TASK, INDEED
WOULD STEAL AWAY OUR CONNECTION, SO FRAIL.
YES IT IS TRUE YOUR VOICE CARRIES AWAY,
THE FIFTHLY REMNANTS OF THE DAY.
YES. THE DIRT THAT YOU WASH AWAY,
STAINS MY MIND AND SOUL.
COVER ME IN MY SINS JUST TO LET,
YOU KISS THEM AWAY WITH YOUR WORDS.
HOW MIGHT I BECOME PURE?  THE WAY,
PROVIDED BY YOU AND YOUR HEART.
YOUR VOICE, SO SWEET TO MY EARS.
IT GIVES ME HOPE AND IT LICKS AWAY MY TEARS.
BUT IN YOUR ABSENCE I AM VOID.
AN UNWORTHY CUSS IN DEED AM I.
OF YOU I DO NOT DESERVE THE PLEASURE.
THE PLEASURE OF YOUR KISSES TO MY FACE.
AN ALL I TASTE IS THE SWEET COPPER,
TASTE OF ITS PULSING RHYTHM.
IN MY MOUTH AND THROUGH MY VEINS.
I LOOK TO YOU MY SWEET TEMPTRESS,
PLEASE FOR ALL THE PRICE OF HEAVEN,
MAY I GIVE IT TO YOU AND MORE WITH IT.
THERE COULD BE NO END TO THE PRIZES,
THAT COULD BE YOURS MY SWEET.
YOU SWEET WONDER FULL KILLER OF HEARTS.
AND MY HEART IN MY HAND SOAKED WITH MY BLOOD.
I AM BATHED WITH MY OWN CRIMSON.
AND NOW DRINKING IT UP IN SIPS AND GULPS.
SHALL I OFFER YOU A TASTE OF IT?
IF I DO SO THEN SHOULD YOU TAKE IT?
ONE QUESTION
IF YOU TASTE OF ME?
THEN MAY I TASTE OF YOUR PLEASING ESSENCE?
TO THIS QUESTION I LEAVE YOU TO CONTEMPLATE.

DOWDEN 97
