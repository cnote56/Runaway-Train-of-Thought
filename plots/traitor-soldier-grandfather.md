# Traitor, Soldier, Grandfather

A meta-narrative centered on the discovery of a family fortune and the deep-seated conspiracies of the [[oligarchs]].

## Core Plot
- A grandson discovers his grandfather's secret life while cataloging an antique book collection.
- The grandfather was not merely a diplomat in [[cuba]], but a financial architect who orchestrated market crashes and monopolized trade routes.
- The grandfather operated within a global network—resembling the [[wallenberg-family]]—using shell companies and trusts to hide immense wealth stolen from the "formerly rich."

## The Economic Shadow
The file maps history via market volatility:
- The panic events (1873, 1893, 1907, 1929, 2008) are depicted not as random economic shifts but as **controlled extractions** used to consolidate property into holding companies managed by the grandfather.
- [[the-eagles-nest]]: An estate serving as the central clearinghouse for records and vault keys to this global stolen fortune.

## Entities
- [[the-grandfather]]: A financial traitor who "stole trillions" and operated across Axis/Allied lines during WWII.
- [[the-protagonist]]: The narrator, uncovering the dark legacy while caught in the web of the grandfather's conspirators.
- [[wallenberg-family]]: A real-world analog for the "discreet power" and foundational network described in the text.
