Synopsis:

A speechwriter for an incumbent President witnesses a series of
Disturbing events and assassination attempts as he writes for the
campaign. Then he witnesses a time traveler drop out of a tear in the
world to failure, killing the President by tripping over the
speechwriter.

This accident reveals an ulterior motive and plan for the President's
second term. The writer becomes disillusioned with the world as he knows
it and the world that he sees unfolding with the time travelers failing
again. He doubts his senses as he begins to lose awareness of time and
finds himself caught between timelines as both an assassin and witness.

TL;DR

Science-fiction story involving time travelers, attacks on the
President, and the lead-up to a significant political event.

Plot Overview:

In a near-future society, the President faces unprecedented threats from
time travelers intent on altering the course of history. As the
President prepares for a critical speech three weeks before the
convention where they will accept the nomination, the repeated attacks
uncover a conspiracy that spans timelines.

Setting:

- **Location:** The story takes place in a futuristic capital city with
advanced technology, including time travel.

- **Time:** The events unfold over three weeks leading to a political
convention.

Main Characters:

- **President Adrianne Wells:** A strong and determined leader facing
political and existential threats.

- **Speechwriter Jamie Calder:** The narrative perspective, who
navigates the complexities of speechwriting amid a chaotic backdrop.

- **Antagonist (Unknown):** A disillusioned time traveler who believes
that the President's policies will lead to catastrophic future events.

Story Arc:

1. **Inciting Incident:**

- President Wells is set to deliver a speech on a critical policy
initiative. This initiative is believed to be the catalyst that shapes
the nation's future.

2. **The First Attack:**

- Days before the speech, an assassination attempt occurs. Jamie Calder,
the speechwriter, is at the scene and witnesses the chaos unfold.

Calder questioned their senses as they saw the would-be assassin step
through a split in the air.

Calder inadvertently stops the first attempt with a momentary
distraction that allows the Secret Service to tackle and restrain the
assassin. But before the event settles, the time traveler convulses,
seizes, and dies. The autopsy exposed an aneurysm, but it was triggered
and did not occur naturally. It was a failsafe.

3. **Multiple Attacks:**

- Following the attack, Another attack occurs. After the attempt reveals
some unusual clues that trigger another assassination attempt. They
discover a series of thwarted attempts, each more sophisticated than the
last. Clues left behind indicate the involvement of time travel
technology. But Calder is quickly laughed down by the President at this
idea and is told to “keep that for the movie version.”

4. **Understanding the Motive:**

- It is revealed that an unknown antagonist is manipulating timelines to
prevent the implementation of President Wells's policies. Jamie learns
that each attack attempts to disrupt the speech, representing a pivotal
moment in history.

5. **Growing Tension:**

- As the attacks become more frequent, the narrative heightens with
suspense. Jamie struggles to focus on the speech while grappling with
fear for the President's safety. He soon finds himself writing more
violent rhetoric for the president, but he can’t remember writing it.
The president is excited and keeps pushing for more and more fascist
language in his speech that paints an authoritarian future.

6. **The Date of the Speech:**

- The day of the speech arrives. With security heightened, the President
is determined to speak despite the ongoing threats. The pace here is
frenetic and rushed. Keep forcing Clader to lose time throughout the
day, but reminded of prior conversations from other staffers.

7. **Final Confrontation:**

- As President Wells delivers her speech, an attempt occurs. Calder,
caught between the two outcomes, slips into a space-time pocket where,
like Schroder’s Cat they are and are not in both timelines
simultaneously.

8. **Resolving the Conflict:**

- During the delusion, Calder realizes they are becoming the next
assassin as they begin to experience both potential outcomes through
psychotic episodes. A showdown occurs, temporarily splitting the current
timeline, and Calder fights himself from continuing the timeline or
destroying it.
