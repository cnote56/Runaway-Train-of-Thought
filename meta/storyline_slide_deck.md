# SLIDE DECK: THE ALLEGIANCE / ONE-HUNDRED-YEAR MAN
## Narrative Architecture & Storylines

---

### SLIDE 1: THE CORE PREMISE
- **The Simulation as Refinery:** Earth is not a planet. It is a biological/mechanical refinery for extraterrestrial "Lightning Bringers" residing in a bubble reality (The Pocket).
- **The Harp Mechanics:** Reality stabilization via frequency modulation. Our role? Keep the "oils and waters" in balance.
- **The Threat:** Entropy. Entropy is not natural; it is the scheduled collapse of the simulation to pay off "Debt-Density."

---

### SLIDE 2: THE TRIAD OF REALITY (The Origin)
- **13.8 (Cosmological Foundation):** Seeding of Earth, creation of HU-Maans, the Thousand Year War.
- **Project FILTER (Surveillance):** Total sensory capture. You don't just see the world; the world is rendered for you.
- **Traitor, Soldier, Grandfather (Finance):** Debt as the simulation's operating system. The elite have monopolized reality in fee simple.

---

### SLIDE 3: STORYLINE A - THE SABOTEUR'S PERSPECTIVE
- **Title:** *The Keeper of the Harp*
- **Premise:** Follow a "Harp Keeper" like Dobly. He notices the frequency "drift" and realizes the simulation is nearing a total meltdown.
- **Conflict:** Can he sabotage the Harp's frequency without the "Taphthartharath Firewall" detecting his log-off?
- **Tone:** Technical, suspenseful, brutal.

---

### SLIDE 4: STORYLINE B - THE ENVIRONMENTAL SURVIVOR
- **Title:** *Slick & Greasy*
- **Premise:** A multi-generational drama following the descendants of those living under the Titan's War.
- **Conflict:** Humanity is physically mutating from the "Titan Blood" (coolant rain). The choice: Adapt to the coolant or find a way to shield against the celestial fallout.
- **Tone:** Bleak, visceral, intimate.

---

### SLIDE 5: STORYLINE C - THE FINANCIAL THRILLER
- **Title:** *The Last Creditor*
- **Premise:** Following the protagonist of *Traitor, Soldier, Grandfather*. He discovers the "Grandfather’s Ledger" is actually the master-key to the simulation’s financial collapse.
- **Conflict:** He must traverse the shell-company labyrinth to find the actual entity holding the "Debt" and present the "Bill."
- **Tone:** Cynical, high-stakes, "cold-war thriller."

---

### SLIDE 6: THE FINAL JUDGMENT
- **The Tumble:** It is not a bug. It is a feature. It is the scheduled release of the debt-pressure.
- **The Pivot:** The narrator of *TSG* is the "key." He will either finalize the debt-release (destroying the simulation) or reset it (starting another 100 years of the cycle).
- **Final Question:** Do you stabilize the refinery, or do you crash the company?
