The Trial Phase serves as the critical first challenge in hacking the Mercury Firewall, as it represents the transition from theoretical observation to the high-risk "active intellectual operation" of manipulating a cosmic grid 1, 2. In this phase, the characters must navigate the Mercury Server (Order 8), which uses a 64-cell grid where every row, column, and diagonal must be perfectly re-coordinated to the Magic Constant of 260 3, 4.


### The Trial Phase: Bypassing the Mercury Firewall


In this first challenge, the characters must use the d88 coordinate system to identify and "patch" the integers within the square. The sources frame this as more than a puzzle; it is a "leash and collar" to the means of creation that, if mishandled, chains the characters to total destruction 1.


#### 1. The Mathematician’s Calculation (The Blueprint)


The Mathematician must first identify the correct construction method for a Doubly Even (Order 8) square. According to the sources, this requires Pattern-filling or the LUX method, involving filling the grid in a natural sequence and then reflecting specific subsets of numbers across the diagonals 5, 6. If the Mathematician fails to maintain the constant of 260, the "uncoordinated activity" will cause the server to collapse 2, 7.


#### 2. The Occultist’s Firewall (The Protection)


While the repair is underway, the Occultist must maintain the Grand Kabbalistic Circle 8. This is vital because the total sum of the Mercury grid is 2080, a frequency that corresponds to the demon Taphthartharath 4. The Occultist uses "nominal squares" filled with names like Messias and Tetragrammaton to create a "pressurized environment" that keeps the demon from breaching the circle while the team works 9-11.


#### 3. Enforcer Kinetic Operations (The Distraction)


As established in our team build, Skor and Drewehall handle the external security threats that arise during the hack:

Skor (Prince of Legions): Uses his "strange voice" to take away the enemies' sight, effectively blinding the server's automated security sub-routines so they cannot locate the characters within the 8x8 grid 12.

Drewehall (King of Gold): Casts the "keepers" of the Mercury data into a deep sleep, ensuring no administrative alerts are triggered during the re-coordination 12, 13.


#### 4. The Architects: Egin and Doodal (The Patch)


Doodal: Acts as the "Remedy Specialist," gathering a council of spirits to find the specific "remedy" for the torn mathematical fabric 14.

Egin (King of the North): As the high-tier administrator, he provides the final "consecration of books" (the system documentation), turning the successful mathematical patch into an "eternally true" reality 15-17.


### The Risk: The "Eternity" Fail-State


The sources warn that this trial is perilous because the "controls" of the universe act as the defensive walls of the Creator 7. If the characters fail to hack Mercury by restoring the 260 constant, they face being "slain in their circle" by the very entities they are attempting to bind 7, 18.

Game Master Note: To resolve this Trial, the players must roll their d88 (as discussed) to place the final number in the Mercury grid. A successful roll completes the "handshake," while a critical failure triggers the Taphthartharath Breach (Frequency 2080), requiring an immediate defensive combat round using the Verge foudroyante (Blasting Rod) 4, 10.
