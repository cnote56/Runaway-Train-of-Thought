# Trevor

Trevor is a practical, tight-lipped guide and operative who operates within the shadowy margins of the surveillance state. He represents the link between the mainstream political world and the secure networks of the resistance.

## Biography
Little is known about Trevor's background prior to meeting **[[reggie-watts]]**. He drives a low-profile vehicle within the D.C. area, facilitating the relocation of rogue officials and sensitive cargo. He leads Reggie to a secure basement hideout in New Hampshire Avenue, protected by biometric scanners.

## Characteristics
- **Appearance:** Rugged, low-profile. His right eye has a distinctive metallic shine—the signature reflection of a modified secure-link/cybernetic lens.
- **Philosophy:** Practical and pragmatic. Believes in strict operational security ("No need for last names").
- **Affiliation:** The underground network operating within **[[the-digital-underground]]**.
