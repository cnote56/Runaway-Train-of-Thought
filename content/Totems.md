Historical sci-fi focused on Native American Totems and folklore
surrounding their origin. The Evolution of man is used as a background
for the world domination by an ancient alien race against intelligent
primates and animals. A war is waged across the planet with the
objective of subjegating the animals in favor of this new human race.
The humans given genetic indicators to cause havoc and destruction in
order to terraform the planet for the ultimate home of the alien race.

Totem poles are not just fanciful art, they are memorials - to the
victims of an ancient Alien Apocalypse.

Sticks and stones. Arrows and bows. Against blinding and burning light
and vast ships burning through the atmosphere.

This is the story of the world we sprang from. The battles that
subjegated the animals beneath us and the stars above our heads.

Our world was not born out of chance nor divine guidance, but rather
with domination against the wild. Our forefathers and foremothers
genetically tied with primates were designed to tame the wildness of the
planet and fight against the previous superior races.
