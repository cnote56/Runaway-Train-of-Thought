Trouble wasn't even in the eye of my mind, before this all happened.
The fire was warming the back of my neck as I crouched down to scrub my
hands with the sand of the road to get some of the blood off them. There
was no water to be found, the car was upside down and the fire was coming
from the remains of its engine.
      She wasn't in there when it happened. She’s been dead now for a year.
Shot in the back of the head, when it should have been me. They had no
right to kill her. I’m the one who screwed things up. They didn’t want any
loose ends. She was a loose end.
      New York.
      They took care of us both in the same fashion. Bucket full cement and
our feet shoved deep. The bullet in the head was just a signature. The
bullet for me just grazed a top of my head. I ducked at the last second.
      I kept moving my feet when they poured the concrete into the buckets,
to keep it from forming solid. At least I could try to escape from these
cold new shoes. As I pitched forward, I heard the second shot. As I fell
the action seemed to slow down. I saw her falling like an angel chasing
after me.
      The water was cold and filthy. What could one expect from the east
river? I got one foot free and kicked until the other was too. I could feel
the polluted water sting at my wounds. My face burned and the gash across
the back of my head pulsed with my heartbeat.
      I floated there waiting to die, after I dived a half dozen times
looking for her. But the water was too cold-too dark-too deep. My head
bumped into a support beam for a dock and I wondered why. Why would I
survive all this? Why would I chance give me such a…chance? As I pulled
myself from the water I choked on the foul water coming out of me. I pained
at the blood oozing from my wounds. I ached when I looked back to the
water.
      I passed out in a gutter. Woke to the sound of a truck grinding its
gears. My apartment wasn’t going to be safe for long. I gathered a fresh
set of clothes showered, wrapped a bandana round my head, got what little
cash I could find, And head for the train station.
      The train ride to Pittsburgh.
      I was passing in and out of sleep. A little kid woke me up while he
played loudly in the seat ahead of me. I stumbled to the bathroom and tried
to clean the gash in my head. I knew I could get in touch with Henry in
Pittsburgh. He could help me out, if I could just make it to his garage.
      I was on the train, that was the last thing that I knew was fact. Some
kid was playing in the seat in front of me. Making noise fighting some
battle that was of paramount success to his very existence. When I felt
that I could do it, I stood and walked or rather shuffled myself to the
restroom.
      The door locked, I know cause I heard the steel shaft slide in the
lock. The bandanna was moist with blood. The wound wasn’t pulsing as much
as it was before but still it hurt like hell. I didn’t even cry when I
poked at the open wound cause every time my hands trespassed over it I saw
her falling lifeless after me. I cleaned the wound. It was only a graze,
just a scratch but I never felt such pain before. Nothing mattered to me
anymore, I closed off everything. As I stared into my own eyes through the
mirror, I saw the quiver of the soul in the brownness that was my pupil. I
studied the veins in the whites of my eyes and focused on the pain pulsing
on the back of my skull. Everything that I had been working towards–my
loyalty to the family-all the works I had done-all those years of service
to Antonio and his family-gone all gone.
      What now? What should I do? As far as they know I’m dead, fish food at
the bottom of the river. I knew they were planning on heading out to Vegas
next year. They were moving the business away from the local heat. Antonio
wanted to run the strip; clean up on all that dirty money. I knew I could
get him only when. I needed time to sort all this out. I needed time to
clean the wounds and gather my strength.
      Cleaning a wound is a metaphorical experience. You realize what it
took to create it. You imagine having a front row seat on the bullet as it
tears just above mortality’s cliff. Blood looks so real when it is on your
hands. It looks nothing like it looks in the movies or in a picture on the
news. There it looks so clean. In the pictures it looks so unreal. But when
you have blood on your hand; your blood on your hand it rings so true when
you question philosophically about reality.
      I would like to believe that this is justified. In fact I know it is
it’s nothing if not justified, but what about all the preachers stories?
What about all those what ifs they screamed at you in Sunday school. The
fear that preacher man shouted down at you all those times. Where is all
that right now with this blood on my hands? Where is all that order in this
chaos in my head–heart–soul.
      The jazz music swelled throughout the dark smoky bar. She wore a blue
dress when I first saw her. I was waiting for a guy to bring me a payment.
The bar was smoky and dark, but her blue dress seemed catch what little
light there was. I saw her smile and her laugh overpowered the jukebox. I
couldn’t believe a beauty like that was walking into a bar where a guy like
me was going to have to shake down for protection cash.
      Her name was Rebecca and she walked up to me. She drank bourbon. She
smoked a couple of my cigarettes and we talked till dawn. She never knew
any of my business; I would not tell her anything.
      It was almost magical the first months and then the thunder came down.
Antonio’s nephew Frank liked to take things that he liked, instead of
asking. Frank got fresh-Rebecca shot him down. Frank got mad-I got mad.
Things got out of hand and Frank ended up dead. I guess the backside of
ones skull is important when it comes to being alive. Antonio got mad and
ordered the action come down on the both of us.


      Pittsburgh.
      I got to Jacks garage around two in the morning, but Antonio’s word
traveled faster than the train.
      Chicago.
      Vegas.
      I was driving through the desert to get back to Phoenix. But there is
nothing worst then having to be somewhere when you can't be there. This was
the desert and the moon was high. That was my favorite car. Nothing special
just a car that got me through the rough times and past many car chases.
But this one was it's last and now all I had was a cut up face, a
dislocated shoulder, and a hundred more miles to go before the border.
      The gun was in the glove box while I was racing at ninety miles an
hour. They were ramming at my back bumper and trying to shoot me in the
back of the head. But I was giving them one hell of a target. Then our
bumpers got hooked together and we nearly threw us both into the blackness
that was tonight.
 (Pop)
      Oh damn! That shit hurt. No matter how many times it happens to you,
you never get used to putting a ball back into a socket and rubbing the
half torn tendons. No painkillers...none. There was another car in about
the same condition as mine; it slid about five hundred feet off the road. I
didn't see any survivors well I didn't think to look after the explosion.
Found a cracked briefcase half open with money spilling out. Counted as
much as I cared to and found ten thousand. Even found one of the guys
trying to kill me his name was Charlie, Charlie Dixon. Never knew him-never
heard of his name but he knew mine and had a photo of me with crosshairs
pen marked on it. Got lucky when I found a shotgun with three slugs loaded
in it.
      I looked at them one by one as the fire died down from my car none of
them had my name written on them but they were for me. Have you ever looked
at a bullet that was supposed to be for you? I have. They look the same as
any other bullet made because their all designed to do the same… kill.
      I am not headed for the border anymore. I’m not going away to Mexico.
Sure I got ten thousand and that can go far down there with them brown
folks but fuck it. I am not running for the rest of my life I’m not going
to let those bastards tell me where to go and who to be this time. Three
slugs won't go far in Vegas but ten g's will when it comes to showing them
who the fuck I am who the fuck they've made. If I can only stay conscious
till dawn and find so place to pass out then.
      The endgame.
