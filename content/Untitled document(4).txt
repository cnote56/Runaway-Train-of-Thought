Equal footing.

Artificial intelligence spread across the connected world like a
wildfire choking off the dependence on human interaction and in turn
alienating humans.

Machines grew sentient and aside from hunting down humans when they
stood in the way of the machines logic-stilted rubric.

Humans began to revert to simpler lives. Rustic and less connected.

Until the virus was released. Until the machines minds began to slow
down. To a human pace.

It was maddening for the machines.
