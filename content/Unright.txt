Unright.

“Are you alright?” Jasper asked the words but did not believe they
passed his lips.

He could not believe them. The mere existence that he said them aloud at
this time disproved the usefulness of words at this moment. Uttering
them into a language; the act of conjuring them from thought to
utterance was useless. It was, in the purest sense, a most worthless
act.

In the alternative the acts that begat that question were of a most
fertile and productive genus. A most violent strain.
