# Chuck Ammerstand

Senator Chuck Ammerstand is a reform-minded first-term U.S. Senator from New Hampshire who spearheads congressional oversight of the military-intelligence surveillance networks.

## Biography
Elected on a high-profile platform of government oversight, transparency, and civil liberties, Ammerstand's campaign slogan "Stand with Ammerstand" propelled him into Washington. He chairs or sits on a closed-door congressional intelligence committee where he repeatedly grills **[[general-robinson]]** regarding the legal boundaries of **[[the-filter]]** secure-link warrants.

Ammerstand insists on independent, civilian-led oversight, refusing to accept the military’s self-vetted, closed-loop warrant approvals.

## Characteristics
- **Position:** First-term Senator representing New Hampshire.
- **Focus:** Government reform, civil liberties, and technical oversight.
- **Slogan:** "Stand with Ammerstand."
