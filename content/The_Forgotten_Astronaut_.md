---
title: The Forgotten Astronaut_
---

When you spend 3000 years in hypersleep traveling to a distant star only
to wake up at the destination and to be greeted by an entire human
civilization because they invented a spaceship 50 years after you left.

You left a world where you were the elite, the best, the highest
caliber. now, you are slower as the humans here are more adapted to the
new planet—they run faster and jump higher.

They want to put your capsule in a museum—an artifact of their past
lives on a world long left cold.
