# Beelzebub

## Overview
Beelzebub (also known as Beel) is the Prince of Devils and the second of the Three Devils bound in the art of Necromancy. He belongs to the high-ranking **Order of Cherubim** and commands a massive legion of one million spirits. In ancient times, prior to the reign of Solomon, he was identified and worshipped under the guise of the idol **Charon**^[[raw/drafts/mythos-and-magic-backup-repository.md]]

## Appearance & Traits
- **Exquisite Beauty:** Unlike common demonic caricatures, Beelzebub appears in an exceptionally beautiful form to the operator who successfully summons him.^[[raw/drafts/mythos-and-magic-backup-repository.md]]
- **Limited Duration:** He remains stable and visible to the master for only half an hour per summon.^[[raw/drafts/mythos-and-magic-backup-repository.md]]
- **Invocation Protocols:** Requires specific fumigants and materials, including amber, aloes, woods druid mastic, and an orientation towards the East.^[[raw/drafts/mythos-and-magic-backup-repository.md]]

## Biography & History
- **The Classical Proxy:** Originally worshipped as Charon before Solomon's era, his administrative profile was later captured and bound within the Solomonic grimoires.^[[raw/drafts/mythos-and-magic-backup-repository.md]]
- **The Merchant of Science and Wealth:** He acts as a key administrative portal for material and intellectual advancement, granting the operator wealth (gold and silver), expert knowledge in sciences, and faithful familiar servants.^[[raw/drafts/mythos-and-magic-backup-repository.md]]

## Relationships
- **Lucifer:** Revers and obeys Lucifer, operating within the absolute monarchical hierarchy of Hell.^[[raw/drafts/mythos-and-magic-backup-repository.md]]
