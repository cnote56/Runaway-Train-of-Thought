SCENE: Tiny Tim Becomes Newborn Lighting

1846, SagradaFamilia, Barcelona, Spain.

Ebenezer Scrooge was as good as his word. He became like a father to me
as he showered our family with long overdue care. His guilt weighed on
him, no doubt. As my back and bones remained twisted despite the
immolation of his wealth.

I was leaps and bounds beyond what my father spoke of the great
awakening in Mr. Scrooge. Suddenly his wintery mood stuffed into a warm
spring nearly instant to one Christmas eve.

Three years since I've been to the shrine by which it feels Mr Scrooge
prays for his own soul. In that time I've had the most advanced
treatments, specialists, injections, surgeries, and yes, mutations
applied to me in hope of finding some cure beyond even my understanding.

The line between medicine and magic is fine that the quantum bit god's
needle could not scratch. The sharp edge of the horizon that pierces
between the flesh of the sky and the darkness of last night.

Strange for a boy I was and growing stronger, faster, all seemingly in
days. It was the first lesson I learned that with all the magic found in
medicine there is much madness as there is genius.

Just this morning off a boat and roughly driven up a mountain I was in
the belly of this castle. My host is clearly a madman. A man whom all
called Doctoro Schrodeger. He was no doctor. His art, medicine, magic
was impossible to study as it was “everywhere and in everything” as he
was fond of saying.

Then the storm rolled in from the sea. And the lighting began.

The ground underfoot sizzled. Crackling with an unholy earthen-fire. As
though the stones and sand were innate one moment and then suddenly
glowing charged with the very power of the earth itself.

The bastard druid howled and chanted from his perch his arms slashing
like katanas in the air.

The castle was nearly flame-engulfed as I choked awake on a table
staring upward at fits of lightning and deluge of rain. Clapping thunder
barrelled through the rafters of the castle-come-laborotory.

Howling at the machines spraying sparks at him the Doctoro Schrotoger
raced from machine to machine; smashing buttons and throwing breaker
pulleys; I was jolted and shocked into a dizzying state flashing hot
bright blue and white with the clash of ozone on my skin. The, into my
skin. And suddenly all of the pulse. The lighting above my head dancing
across the castle metal-netted roof funneled it all around me.

I saw everything in clear beautiful crisp details. The sharpness of
raindrops is so fine I can smell their fractals spinning off into vapor
and fueling the charge imbued into me. Distilling me like a new born
liquid light.

New born lightning. Fresh and twisted violent and stark. All the pain
and all the pleasure. The fire and ice hot to cool all in the flex of
plasma and the release of ionization.

The madness flowed through me. In the bones of my bones through the
roots of my blood. The cold heat of Doctoro's magic calculus woven into
a new power. A ppwer of the earth. Born into the sky. And surrounding us
all. Fear the vacuum, for it would tear our lungs through our throats.

It was here, that i stopped being whom I was before. It was here in the
sanctuary.

### TREATMENT ###

This story treatment outlines a dark, alternative Victorian history
where industrialization meets metaphysical horror.

# Movie Treatment:

*The Living Undead and Tiny Tim*

**Logline:** In a London fueled by human souls, a mutated Tiny Tim must
infiltrate a subterranean hive-city to destroy a voodoo-powered monarchy
and face the father who was forced to become his executioner.

---

### **I. Project Overview**

* **Genre:** Alternative Historical Fiction / Steampunk Horror / Action.

* **Setting:** 1888 London (The Hive Beneath Buckingham Palace) and 1846
Barcelona (The Laboratory of the Bastard Druid).

* **Tone:** Gritty, visceral, and tragic. Think *John Wick* meets *Bram
Stoker’s Dracula* with a heavy dose of **body horror**.

---

### **II. Principal Characters**

| Character | Role | Description |

| :--- | :--- | :--- |

| **Tiny Tim** | The Protagonist | A "twisted, violent, and stark" man
distilled into **liquid light** through radical science [1, 2]. |

| **Queen Victoria** | The Antagonist | A monstrous, **hive-like
entity** resembling an "Alien Queen" who commands the living undead [1].
|

| **Bob Cratchit** | The Enforcer | Tim's father and the Queen’s
**undead assassin**; he is aware of his actions but bound by voodoo. |

| **Ebenezer Scrooge** | The Double Agent | A "Mad Rich Man" who
provides Tim with tech but secretly serves the Queen’s financial
interests [1]. |

| **Dr. Schrodeger** | The Architect | A "bastard druid" in Spain whose
**"magic calculus"** traded souls for eternal machines [2]. |

---

### **III. Plot Synopsis**

#### **Act I: The Price of a Cure**

The story opens in 1846 at a castle-laboratory in Barcelona [1]. Driven
by a "wintery mood" of overwhelming guilt, **Ebenezer Scrooge** and
**Bob Cratchit** "press" a experimental cure upon the sickly Tiny Tim
[1]. Under the direction of **Doctoro Schrodeger**, Tim is subjected to
an unholy distillation process [2]. During a massive storm, the castle
is "nearly flame-engulfed" as lightning is funneled into Tim,
transforming his blood into **"plasma and ionization"** [2]. Tim
survives, but he is no longer human; he is **"new born lightning"** with
the power of the earth in his bones [2].

#### **Act II: The Weaponized Empire**

Jump to 1888 London. **Weaponized capitalism** has turned the working
class into "mindless subjects"—the living undead. The **Hope Diamond**
serves as the central focus of a voodoo ritual that powers the Queen’s
hive [1]. Tim, now a battle-hardened warrior who views himself as his
**"own worst enemy,"** works with Scrooge to infiltrate the Palace [1].

Scrooge acts as Tim's "Q," providing an arsenal including a
**flamethrower blunderbuss** packed with nails and shot [1]. However,
Scrooge is a **double agent**. He has been managing the Queen’s ledgers,
trading human souls for the empire's indestructible infrastructure.
Throughout the mission, Scrooge subtly sabotages Tim, leading him into
traps while maintaining his "sincere" facade.

#### **Act III: The Hive and the Heart**

Tim reaches the throneroom deep under Buckingham Palace [1]. He finds
his **oldest sister**—the only one who truly loved him—wrapped in a
"thick web" beside the Queen [1]. Standing in his way is his father,
**Bob Cratchit**. Bob is the Queen’s elite assassin, fighting a losing
internal battle against the voodoo spell.

In a climactic struggle, the dramatic tension peaks as Bob regains a
moment of clarity. He **rejects the Queen’s power**, turning his blade
against her guards to protect his son. Bob is **sacrificed** in the
process, dying in Tim's arms after finally apologizing for the "Cure" he
forced upon him [1].

#### **The Climax**

Tim confronts the Queen. As he prepares to destroy the **Hope Diamond**,
Scrooge reveals his final betrayal, attempting to seize the jewel to
"balance his own ledger." However, seeing Tim’s grief over Bob's body,
Scrooge has a final **redemption arc**. He turns on the Queen, using his
knowledge of the "magic calculus" to destabilize the diamond's energy.
Tim unleashes his "liquid light" [2], vaporizing the Queen and the hive.

---

### **IV. Key Themes**

* **The Ledger of Souls:** The idea that "progress" always requires a
sacrifice, usually from the most vulnerable [2].

* **Generational Trauma:** Tim fighting the father who tried to "save"
him through violence and mutation [1].

* **The Duality of Man:** Tim’s struggle with the "madness" and "genius"
woven into his very blood [2].
