Dear sir,
I have had many years to think on this subject that seems to soak within your organization. The idea being one of knowing what is right and wrong for other people and dictating to those people what they should and shouldn't view, listen to, and or enjoy. Your organizations efforts to "clean" the airwaves of HOWARD STERN and RUSS MARTIN and various other talk show hosts are at least ridiculous and feebleminded. For you sir to have the arrogance to force your lifestyle choice upon others has what I see a nazi root. For you to think you have the right to tell others in this our free country betrays the blood shed by our forefathers and spits upon the very fabric of this our great nation. Your efforts only amplify the actions of these entertainers. And that's exactly what they are "entertainers" nothing more nothing less. If you feel that this crusade that you have undertaken to rid the airwaves of this crudeness then I see it has my duty as a citizen of this country to oppose your opinion and understand you to be a dictator with no force behind the words you spit out. There is nothing wrong with the words "FREEDOM OF SPEECH" and I have the understanding of phrase in my mind and respect buried deep in my heart for the cornerstones of this our great country. And yes I understand that even that phrase applies to you and your efforts, but remember I too have a voice. I feel that you are an un-American dictating shell of a man that has nothing better to do with his time that shout at the rain simply because you refuse to understand it.  
 
I enjoy the Howard Stern show. 
I enjoy the Russ Martin show. 
I enjoy listening to my favorite radio station KYNG 105.3, based in Dallas, Texas. 
I enjoy being an American. 
I enjoy expressing my right to listen to what I wish to listen to when I wish to listen to it. 
And you sir has no right to attempt to supersede my choice as an American citizen.  For you to attempt to persuade advertisers to pull their funds from a certain show simply because he or she does not hold your same moral values is an ignorant, yellow bellied, coward-like action that simply shows the tremendous lack of backbone.  
 
Thank you,
Cole D.
