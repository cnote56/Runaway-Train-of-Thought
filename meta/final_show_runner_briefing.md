# SHOW RUNNER BRIEFING: "THE ALLEGIANCE" (PROJECT FILTER)

## I. PROJECT OVERVIEW
This is an industrial horror thriller set in a collapsing reality-loop. The world is a refinery, the humanity is the labor, and the "Gods" are the creditors. The simulation is entering a high-density "debt-event"—The Tumble.

## II. THE NARRATIVE ARC
### Act 1: The Render (The Illusion)
- Focus: Daily life under the Fatherhood and the Bloxes.
- The Pivot: Discovery of the "Grandfather’s Ledger" by the narrator.
- Aesthetic: Oppressive, gray, digitized, biometric compliance.

### Act 2: The Breach (The Mechanics)
- Focus: Tactical resistance. Utilizing the Peanut Butter Underground to smuggle Time Engines. Discovering the "Mercury Firewall" vulnerabilities.
- The Pivot: Realizing that the environment (Titan’s War) is not a natural disaster, but the byproduct of the Creditors' decaying machines.
- Aesthetic: Gritty, mechanical, metallic, ionized.

### Act 3: The Foreclosure (The Synthesis)
- Focus: Infiltration of "The Pocket."
- The Climax: The protagonist presents the final bill to the Lightning Bringers. The choice: Pay the debt (dissolve the refinery) or default (humanity inherits the wreckage).
- Aesthetic: Ethereal, static-heavy, high-definition reality collapse.

## III. TONE GUIDELINES
- "Traitor, Soldier, Grandfather" (Clinical, cynical, cold-war thriller).
- No fluff. No AI-isms (delve, leverage, tapestry, etc.).
- Technical Peer-to-Peer communication: Data-first, narrative-second.
